# Command palette design

Date: 2026-11-23 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/command-palette.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-071

## Goal
A person needs one place that takes them anywhere: a conversation, a person, a channel they could join, a message, or a quick action such as "Set status". This ticket builds that place: the palette that opens with Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) or from the Search field, calls the search request of TOKI-070, draws its groups, and lets the keyboard (or a finger) choose.
The palette decides nothing about who may be found: the request answers only what the person may see (TOKI-070 R4 to R7), and the palette draws it as it comes. What it owns is the screen (groups, keys, focus, empty and failed states), the quick actions and the list of recent conversations, the way it asks for more, the call and its state in `client-core`, and the entry points (the Search field of the shell, the Search field of the narrow Home, the opener for the More sheet).
Some of what the log says about the palette is open or disagrees with itself: the order after exact matches (D-187), the groups (D-112 against F29), what "recent" means, the narrow entry, how a message is opened, where a profile opens, and what the commands of Pause, Focus time and Switch look do. Each is an area of concern with a marked proposal; the requirements are written as the proposal, and the sentence "Check" of each says what changes if the owner answers otherwise.

## Scope
- In:
  - The palette dialog and its parts: the field, the groups, the rows, the footer, the empty, offline and failed states (R2, R4 to R6, R11, R12).
  - The opening and closing keys, the hint on the Search field, the one function that opens it (`useOpenPalette`) and the narrow entry (R1, R15).
  - The keys inside: arrows, Enter, Esc, input methods (R3).
  - What is shown before typing: recent conversations and the quick actions (R4, R14).
  - The groups and the rows of the search request, the order they keep, "Show more" (R5, R7, R10).
  - The quick actions: the registry, the actions this ticket registers, the Admin-only "Create channel" (R8).
  - What choosing a result does (R9).
  - The highlight of the match and the window of a message (R12).
  - The call of `GET /api/v1/search` from the web app and its state: debounce, one request at a time, stale answers dropped (R13).
  - The texts, styles, wiring and tests (R16 to R19).
- Out (not now):
  - The request, who may be found, the order of names and of messages, the cursor and the 20 per kind: TOKI-070. The index and the word rule for messages: TOKI-069. This ticket does not rank again what the route ordered (R7).
  - The search inside one conversation: TOKI-072. The Files search, the conversation list's search, Browse channels and the people picker's search, which filter data the client holds: TOKI-063, TOKI-033, TOKI-034.
  - Opening a message and the flash on it: TOKI-068. The People screen and the address of a profile outside a conversation: TOKI-079. The profile panel: TOKI-057.
  - The More sheet that draws the Search row: TOKI-092. It calls `useOpenPalette` of this ticket (R1).
  - The commands "Pause notifications" and "Start Focus time" (TOKI-076) and "Switch look" (TOKI-078): their owners register them in the registry of R8; this ticket registers none of them, so the palette never shows a dead command.
  - A shortcut for anything but opening the palette, and a settings page for shortcuts: not in phase 1.
  - Anything of the guest portal, Meet, Calendar, Tasks or AI that the prototype's palette lists: not phase 1 (D-002, D-008, D-152).

## Requirements
- R1. Opening and closing. `PaletteProvider` (`apps/web/src/features/command-palette/PaletteProvider.tsx`) is mounted once by `AppShellRoute`, inside the shell's providers, and holds the open state. `useOpenPalette()` returns one stable function `open(options?: { returnFocusRef? })`; the More sheet of TOKI-092 calls it. While the provider is mounted, one `keydown` listener on `document`, in the capture phase, opens the palette on `Ctrl+K` or `Cmd+K` (`event.key.toLowerCase() === "k"` with `ctrlKey` or `metaKey`, and no `altKey` or `shiftKey`; on every device and in every field, also a text field, as the prototype does) and closes it on the same key while it is open. It calls `preventDefault` on every such event, so the browser's own Ctrl+K never runs. It acts only when the event is not part of an input method composition (`isImeEvent` of TOKI-043 R2), and it does not open the palette while another dialog or menu is open (`document.querySelector('[role="dialog"], [role="menu"]')` finds an element other than the palette's own): the key is then prevented and nothing else happens (Decided in this spec, concern 13). The listener is removed when the provider unmounts, so nothing opens it on `/login`. Check: with a Testing Library render of the provider, `Ctrl+K` and `Meta+K` open it, a second press closes it, `Ctrl+Shift+K` and `Ctrl+Alt+K` do not, the event has `defaultPrevented` true, a press while an open `Modal` is on screen leaves that dialog alone and the palette closed, a press with `isComposing` true does nothing, and after unmount a press changes nothing.
- R2. The dialog. The palette is one `Modal` of TOKI-014 in its form without `title` (R5 (b)): `aria-label` `t("palette.label")` ("Command palette", the prototype), `className="pal"`, `initialFocusRef` the field, `returnFocusRef` the element that had focus when it opened (or the one `open` was given), `backdrop` `dim`, and `onRequestClose` that closes it for every reason (`escape`, `outside`). `Modal` is not changed: the scrim is a grid that centres its child, and the rule `.pal { width: min(600px, 100%); align-self: start; margin-top: 8vh }` puts the panel near the top, as the prototype does (lines 424 and 2196; TOKI-014's overlays spec left this placement to this ticket, and a class on the panel is enough). The panel holds, in this order: the field row (a `SearchIcon`, an `input type="search"` with `data-autofocus`, `aria-label` `t("palette.field.label")` ("Search", the prototype), `placeholder` `t("palette.field.placeholder")` ("Search conversations, people, actions", the prototype), `autocomplete="off"`, `autocapitalize="off"`, `spellcheck="false"`, and a `span.kbd` with `t("palette.key.esc")` ("Esc")), the results box `div.pres` (`max-height: min(420px, 60vh)`, scrolling), and the footer `div.pfoot` with three hints: `↑` `↓` and `t("palette.footer.move")` ("move"), `Enter` and `t("palette.footer.open")` ("open"), `Esc` and `t("palette.footer.close")` ("close") (the prototype's words). On a window under 720 px the footer is not drawn (there is no keyboard to hint) and `.pal` has `margin-top: 0` (the prototype's line 559). The palette is not drawn for a person who is not signed in. Check: a render test finds one `role="dialog"` named "Command palette", the field focused, the three footer hints, and no footer under 720 px; a browser test at 1100 px reads the panel's top (8% of the window height, within 1 px) and its width (600 px), and at 360 by 560 px the panel is inside the window with no horizontal scroll.
- R3. Keys inside. The field is an ARIA combobox: `role="combobox"`, `aria-expanded="true"`, `aria-controls` the results box (`role="listbox"`), `aria-activedescendant` the id of the active row, `aria-autocomplete="list"`. Each choosable row is `role="option"` with an id and `aria-selected`; each group has a heading `div.g` and `role="group"` with `aria-labelledby` that heading. There is one active row at a time. `ArrowDown` makes the next row active and `ArrowUp` the previous one, wrapping from the last to the first and from the first to the last (the prototype); `Home` and `End` are left to the field. The active row scrolls into view (`block: "nearest"`). `Enter` runs the active row's choice (R9) and closes the palette. `Escape` closes it (the Esc of TOKI-014 R9 does this; nothing else handles it). Every one of these keys is ignored when it is part of an input method composition (`isImeKey` of `useComposition`, TOKI-043 R2: a person typing Vietnamese with Telex or VNI presses Enter to end a word, and that Enter must not open the active row), and `preventDefault` is called for the arrows and for an Enter that runs a row, never for an ignored one. The first row is active when the list changes (a new query, a loaded group); loading more of a group keeps the active row where it is. A press on a row runs its choice, and moving the pointer over a row makes it active. With no row, Enter does nothing. A "Show more" row (R10) and the retry row (R11) are rows too. Check: `user-event` tests for each key, for the wrap in both directions, for Enter on a row of each kind (a spy on the choice), for a keydown with `isComposing` true and one with `keyCode` 229 and `isComposing` false (no choice, no `preventDefault`), for `compositionstart`, Enter, `compositionend`, Enter (one choice), and for the roles and the `aria-activedescendant` after each key; a browser test drives the real input method with the CDP command `Input.imeSetComposition` ("Việt") and finds that Enter changes nothing, then commits the word and finds the results of "Việt".
- R4. Before typing. With an empty field (after trimming) the palette draws two groups, with no request: `t("palette.group.recent")` ("Recent", a proposal, concern 3) with up to `PALETTE_RECENT_MAX` (5, proposal, concern 3) conversations from the list of R14, newest first, and `t("palette.group.actions")` ("Actions", the prototype) with every quick action of R8 in the order of the registry. A group with nothing in it has no heading. The first row is active. Check: a render with three recent conversations and the built-in actions finds "Recent" with three rows then "Actions"; with no recent conversation there is no "Recent"; the search call is never made (a spy); the rows are in the order of the lists.
- R5. While typing. The text typed is trimmed at both ends (`String.prototype.trim`) and, when it is empty, R4 applies. Otherwise the controller of R13 asks the route for the first page of every kind (no `kind`), after the pause of R13, and the palette draws these groups, each only when it has a row, in this order (concern 2): `t("palette.group.conversations")` "Conversations", `t("palette.group.people")` "People", `t("palette.group.channels")` "Channels you can join", `t("palette.group.actions")` "Actions", then `t("palette.group.messages")` "Messages" (the five words are the prototype's, which lists the first four and adds the actions, and F29's order). The Actions group does not wait for the route: it is made on every key from the registry (R8) and is on screen while the route is still answering. Messages are asked for by the route from 2 characters (TOKI-070 R7); the palette draws nothing about that rule and never hides the group by itself. While the route is answering, a line `t("palette.searching")` ("Searching…", a proposal under D-139) follows the Actions group and the groups of the previous text are gone (the answer of an older text is never drawn under a newer one, R13). Check: a render with a fake route answer holding the four kinds finds the five headings in the order above; an answer with no channels has no "Channels you can join"; while the answer is pending the Actions group and the line are drawn and no group of the route; typing a second text removes the groups of the first.
- R6. The rows. Every row is one `button` (`type="button"`, `tabIndex={-1}`, so Tab leaves the dialog's field and the arrows move) with an icon or an `Avatar` (24 px) and `span.grow.ell` holding the label and, after a space, `span.muted.xs` with the second text (the prototype, line 2203). (a) A conversation (`SearchConversation`, TOKI-070 R3): label `conversationName` of `packages/client-core/src/conversations/names.ts` (the function TOKI-070 calls `getConversationName`, TOKI-032 R17; its first parameter is widened to the `kind`, `name` and `memberIds` that `SearchConversation` carries, a change to that file made here), the people from the store; icon `lock` for a private channel and `hash` for another, `users` for a group, and the `Avatar` (`personHue`, TOKI-033 R28) of the other person for a direct message; second text the topic of a channel. An archived channel carries the word `t("palette.row.archived")` ("Archived", the word of Browse channels, TOKI-034) as its second text before the topic. (b) A person (`SearchPerson`): `Avatar` of the person, label `displayName`, second text the job title when the store's person carries one (TOKI-057 concern 5) and nothing otherwise. (c) A channel to join (`SearchChannel`): icon `hash`, label the name, second text the topic. (d) A message (`SearchMessage`): icon `home` (the prototype's) and label the snippet of R12, second text `t("palette.row.messageIn", { author, conversation })` ("{author} in {conversation}"; the prototype's "first name in #channel", with the full display name because the first load carries no short name, TOKI-032 R17; the conversation is `#name` for a channel and the name of R6 (a) for a direct message or a group, as the prototype's `cname`); with an author the store does not hold, the second text is `t("palette.row.messageInOnly", { conversation })` ("in {conversation}"). (e) An action: label, optional second text, icon from the registry. (f) The row of a person whose status is `deactivated` in the store carries no mark: the route does not list them (TOKI-070 R5); a direct message with a deactivated person is a conversation row, and a message of one is a message row. Under 720 px every row is at least 40 px high (D-067, F38; the prototype has 38 px for every width, a change made here, as TOKI-033 R14 does for the list). Check: one render test for each kind with a fake answer, reading the roles, the icon names, the labels and the second texts; the archived word; the group's icon; a message whose author is not in the store; `min-height` 40 px at 360 px and 38 px at 1100 px in the browser test.
- R7. Order. The palette keeps the order in which the route returned the items of the four server groups and does not sort, filter or rank them again (TOKI-070's concern, "must not rank again what the route already ordered"): the answer is the order, the first 20 of a kind are the route's first 20, and the rows appended by "Show more" follow them. The two groups the palette builds itself are ordered with the functions of `@toki/shared` that TOKI-070 R6 exports for this: the Actions group by `nameRank`, then `compareSearchNames` over `foldText` of the action's label and the folded query (rank 0 equal, 1 starts with, 2 contains; an action whose label has rank `null` is not drawn; ties by the registry's order: the sort key of an action is its folded label and its id the registry's own id), and the Recent group by recency (R14) with no query. There is no fuzzy match and no word-start rank in this ticket (concern 1). Check: a test gives the palette an answer in a deliberately non-alphabetical, non-ranked order and finds the rows in that order; a table test of the Actions group for the queries `br` (Browse channels first), `status` and `s` (every label that contains an `s`, ranked by the rule), `Đặt` against a Vietnamese label, and a query that matches nothing; the order of two actions with an equal rank follows the registry.
- R8. Quick actions. `apps/web/src/features/command-palette/commands.tsx` exports `CommandsProvider`, `useRegisterCommand(command | null)` and `useCommands()`, with `Command = { id: string; label: string; detail?: string; icon: IconName; run(): void }`. `useRegisterCommand` registers the command while the calling component is mounted and removes it when it unmounts or passes `null`; for one id the last registration wins, and an unmount removes a command only when it is still the registered one (the rule of `useRegisterListActions`, TOKI-033 R25, and `useRegisterAccountEntry`, TOKI-099 R3); the registry order is the order of first registration. A command is drawn only while it is registered, so a command whose feature is not built is not in the palette (the rule of the account menu: an entry appears when its own ticket is built). This ticket registers, from `PaletteCommands` (mounted by `PaletteProvider`), these commands, each with the label from `t(...)` in the current language, matched on its label only (concern 1): (1) "Browse channels" (`palette.action.browse`, the prototype's words; `detail` "Join or rejoin a channel", `palette.action.browse.detail`; icon `hash`) which calls `browseChannels` of `useListActions()` (TOKI-033 R25) and is not registered while that opener is not; (2) "New direct message" (`palette.action.newDirect`; icon `plus`, not the pencil, D-155) which calls `startDirect` of the same registry; (3) "Set status" (`palette.action.setStatus`, the prototype's words; icon `smile`) which calls `useOpenStatusDialog()` of TOKI-056 R15; (4) "Create channel" (`palette.action.createChannel`; icon `plus`), registered only when `useCurrentRole()` of TOKI-098 R3 is `"admin"` (a Member or an unknown role registers nothing: D-084, D-151; the server refuses a Member's request in any case), which calls `open()` of `useCreateChannelDialog` (TOKI-098 R4), whose `element` the provider renders; (5) "Go to Activity", "Go to Later", "Go to Files", "Go to People" and "Go to Preferences" (`palette.action.goTo`, "Go to {section}", the prototype's form, with the section's own label from `nav.*`; the icon of the section), one for each row of `visibleSections(role)` (TOKI-015 R36) whose `id` is `activity`, `later`, `files`, `people` or `preferences` (the five of F29), so a section that is not built or not allowed has no command; each navigates to the section's `to`. There is no "Go to Home" and no "Go to Admin": F29 lists neither. The commands of "Switch look", "Pause notifications" and "Start Focus time" are registered by TOKI-078 and TOKI-076 (concern 8); the palette needs nothing more for them. A command's `run` is called after the palette has closed (R9). Check: a registry test (register, replace, unmount only the owner's, `null`, order); with a Member nothing named "Create channel" exists in the palette, in any language, and with an Admin it does and its choice opens the dialog; a `role` change adds or removes it with no reload; a section row that is absent from `visibleSections` has no "Go to"; with `browseChannels` not registered there is no "Browse channels"; each registered command's `run` calls its target (spies); a Vietnamese render shows the Vietnamese labels and matches them folded.
- R9. Choosing. A choice (Enter, a press) first closes the palette, then runs: the palette's state is set closed, and the choice is run from an effect of `PaletteProvider` after the dialog has unmounted and returned focus (the dialog's own effects run before its parent's, TOKI-014 R13 and R33), so that a dialog that the choice opens finds focus where the palette left it and returns focus there. By kind: (a) a conversation or a channel the person belongs to, `navigate({ to: "/c/$conversationId", params })`, a push (D-089), through `useGo` of the router seam (R19); an archived channel goes to the same address (TOKI-101 owns what its page shows). (b) A person: `useOpenPerson()(personId)` of `apps/web/src/features/command-palette/deps.ts`, which opens the profile panel of TOKI-057 at the People screen's address with `useOpenProfileAt` (TOKI-057 R11, R17); the address is concern 7. (c) A channel to join: `browseChannels({ initialSearch: channel.name })` of the registry of TOKI-033, so Browse channels opens with the name as its search text, as the prototype does (line 2178); this needs `ListAction`'s options to carry `initialSearch` (Plan changes, concern 14). (d) A message: `useOpenMessage()` of `deps.ts` called with `{ conversationId, messageId, parentId }` of the result (the owner of the jump and the flash is TOKI-068; for a reply, concern 6). (e) An action: the command's `run`. A choice made while the connection is down is run all the same: what needs the connection gates itself with `useOnlineGate` (D-079), and the palette adds no gate. The query and the results are dropped when the palette closes (R18). Check: for each kind, a test chooses a row and asserts the order (the dialog is gone from the document before the target is called: a spy that records `queryByRole("dialog")`), the target and its arguments, and that focus is on the element that had it before the palette opened when the target opens nothing; a test with a target that opens a `Modal` finds the focus in that modal and, on its close, back on the first opener.
- R10. More on request. A group of the route (Conversations, People, Channels you can join, Messages) whose `nextCursor` is not null ends with one row, a "Show more" row (`t("palette.more")`, "Show more", a proposal under D-019 and D-139; D-112 says only "more on request"), with the same look as the others. A choice of it (Enter or a press) does not close the palette: it asks the route for the next page of that kind (`kind` and `cursor`, R13), appends the items below the last row of the group, and keeps the active row on the first appended row (the one that followed the previous last row); while the request runs the row is `aria-busy="true"`, shows `t("palette.moreLoading")` ("Loading…") and a second choice does nothing (D-111); when the page has no `nextCursor` the row is gone. A message page may hold fewer than 20 items, or none, while `nextCursor` is not null (TOKI-070 R7): the row stays until a page comes with `nextCursor` null. A failed request keeps the items, shows `t("palette.moreFailed")` ("Could not load more. Try again.") on the row, and the next choice asks again. The Actions group has no such row: all its rows are drawn (there are fewer than 20). Check: a test with a fake route that returns 20 items and a cursor, chooses the row, receives 20 more and finds 40 rows once each in order, the active row on the 21st, the row busy during the request and absent after the last page; a double Enter sends one request; a failed request leaves 20 rows and the failure text, and the next Enter succeeds; a message page that returns 0 items with a cursor keeps the row.
- R11. Empty, offline and failed. (a) Nothing at all (no group of the route has a row, the Actions group is empty and the answer is not pending): the line `t("palette.empty", { query })` (`No results for "{query}"`, the prototype's words, line 2201; D-074: written for this context), centred, `role="status"`, with the trimmed text as typed. (b) When the connection is down (`isOnline()` of `patterns/online.ts` is false) while a text is typed, the route is not called (D-079: every other action is blocked with an explanation); the Actions group is still drawn (it is made in memory) and the line `t("palette.offline")` ("You are offline. Search needs a connection.", a proposal under D-139) takes the place of the groups of the route; when the connection returns the controller asks again without another key (Decided in this spec, concern 9). With a text and no action matching while offline, the line of (b) is drawn and not (a). (c) A request that fails (any error of the client except an abort, including a validation failure of the answer) leaves the Actions group, draws a row `t("palette.failed")` ("Search is not available. Try again.", a proposal) which is choosable and asks again on a choice, and never shows the text of the error. A 401 is handled by the client's own hook (TOKI-032 R4) and shows nothing here. (d) A screen reader is told the state in one `aria-live="polite"` region of the dialog that holds, after each change settles: the number of rows (`t("palette.count", { count })`, plural forms), the line of (a), (b) or (c), or nothing while the answer is pending. Check: a test for each of (a), (b) (with `isOnline` mocked, the route spy never called, the Actions group present, and the call made once when the mock turns true), (c) (the retry row, the key of the error not in the DOM) and (d) (the live region's text after each state, once per settle, not once per key).
- R12. Highlight and snippet. `apps/web/src/features/command-palette/highlight.ts` (no DOM, pure) exports `matchRanges(text: string, query: string, mode: "name" | "words"): Array<[number, number]>` and `messageSnippet(text: string, query: string, max?: number): { text: string; ranges: Array<[number, number]> }`. The text is folded one code point at a time with `foldText` of `@toki/shared` (TOKI-050 R2), keeping, for every folded position, the code point of the original that made it, so the ranges are in code units of the original and never split a pair of a surrogate or a decomposed letter from its mark. Mode `name` marks the first place where the folded query occurs as one string in the folded text (the rule of TOKI-070 R6); mode `words` marks, for each word of `searchWords(query)` (TOKI-069 R6), every word of the text that starts with it (the rule of the message index, `ha:*`). A query with no folded text marks nothing. `messageSnippet` makes the label of a message: white space runs (including line breaks) are collapsed to one space and the text is trimmed, then, with `max` code points (default `PALETTE_SNIPPET_MAX`, 70, the prototype's `msgSnippet(m, 70)`), the first `max` code points when the first range ends inside them, with `…` added when the text was cut; otherwise a window that starts `PALETTE_SNIPPET_LEAD` (20, Decided in this spec, concern 15) code points before the first range (moved forward to the start of a word when one is within those 20) and has `max` code points, with `…` before it and, when more text follows, after it. The text is the stored text as it was sent (marks of formatting and `@name` as typed, as the prototype shows `m.text`). The rows draw the ranges as `<mark>` (no colour literal: `background: transparent; color: var(--accent-text); font-weight: 700`), for the label of a conversation, a person and a channel (mode `name`) and of a message (mode `words`); the label of an action is marked the same way. Check: table tests: `nguyen` marks `Nguyễn` (a precomposed and a decomposed form, both ranges covering the whole letter and its mark), `duong dan` marks `đường dẫn` as two words in mode `words` and as one string in mode `name`, `ha` marks the start of `Hà Nội` but in mode `words` marks `Hà` and `Hạ` and not `Nhà`, an emoji before the match leaves the ranges on the right code units, a query of one combining mark marks nothing, a text of 300 code points with the match at 250 gives a window of 70 with the match inside and the `…`, a match at 10 gives the first 70, and a text with a line break is one line.
- R13. The call and its state. `packages/client-core/src/search/` holds `calls.ts`, `createSearchCalls(api)` returning `{ search(params: { q: string; kind?: SearchKind; cursor?: string }, options?: { signal?: AbortSignal }): Promise<SearchResponse> }`, a `GET /search` through the client's request function with the `SearchResponseSchema` of `@toki/shared` (TOKI-070 R12) so an answer that does not parse is an error and not data; and `palette-search.ts`, `createPaletteSearch({ calls, timers, debounceMs? })` returning a store with `subscribe`, `getState`, `setQuery(text)`, `loadMore(kind)`, `retry()` and `clear()`. The state is `{ query: string; status: "idle" | "loading" | "ready" | "failed"; groups: Record<SearchKind, { items: T[]; nextCursor: string | null; more: "idle" | "loading" | "failed" }> | null }` and is replaced, never changed in place. `setQuery` trims; an empty text goes to `idle` with `groups` null and aborts any pending call and timer; a new text cancels the timer and aborts the call of the older text, sets `status: "loading"` with `groups` null and starts a timer of `debounceMs` (`PALETTE_DEBOUNCE_MS`, 150, Decided in this spec, concern 11) with the `timers.after` of `apps/web/src/client.ts` (TOKI-032 R25), after which it calls `search({ q })` with no `kind`; the same text set again while `loading` or `ready` does nothing. Every call carries a sequence number; an answer or an error of any call but the newest is dropped, so a slow answer of an older text never replaces a newer one, and an aborted call is not a failure. `loadMore(kind)` is ignored unless `groups[kind].nextCursor` is not null and `more` is not `loading`; it calls `search({ q, kind, cursor })` for the text of the state, appends the items (an `id` already held is not added twice) and replaces the cursor; its answer for a text that is no longer the state's is dropped. `retry()` repeats the first call (when `failed`) at once, with no pause. `clear()` is `setQuery("")` and drops the groups. The store holds no result after `clear`, and nothing in it is written to storage. `apps/web/src/client.ts` makes the one instance with the client and the timers and `usePaletteSearch` (`apps/web/src/features/command-palette/usePaletteSearch.ts`) binds it with `useSyncExternalStore`; `clear` is called when the palette closes, when the session ends (the store's reset of TOKI-032 R11 and TOKI-099 R7) and on sign-out. The pause is counted with the `TestClock` and the fake `timers` of TOKI-008 in every test, never with a real timer. ## Design
### How the pieces fit
```
Ctrl+K / Cmd+K  ──┐
Search field   ───┼─> PaletteProvider (open state, recent, choice runner)
More sheet ───────┘      │
(useOpenPalette)         ├─> Palette (Modal, form b) ── field ── listbox
                         │       ├─ Recent / Actions   (no text: R4)
                         │       └─ Conversations / People / Channels / Actions / Messages (R5)
                         ├─> CommandsProvider  <── PaletteCommands (this ticket)
                         │                     <── TOKI-076, TOKI-078 register theirs
                         └─> usePaletteSearch ─> createPaletteSearch (client-core)
                                                   └─> GET /api/v1/search (TOKI-070)
```
The palette is a view over four things it does not own: the route's answer, the store, the command registry and the list of recent ids. It owns the rows' look, the keys, the one active row, and the order of the groups.

### Why the palette does not rank again
Decided in this spec (concern 1, part of it). TOKI-070 orders names with one rule (rank, then folded name, then id) and messages by time, and its pages are cut at 20 by that order, so a second sort on the client would reorder the first 20 against the server's and would break "Show more", which continues after the server's last item. The palette therefore draws the answer in the order it came. The only items it orders itself are the ones the server never sees (the actions), and it does so with the server's own functions so the two rules are one.

### Why messages and names come in one request
One request with no `kind` answers the four kinds in one round trip and one snapshot (TOKI-070 R10), so a person who types "lan" sees conversations, people, channels and messages that agree with each other. "Show more" then asks for one kind with its cursor. The Actions group is made locally, so it is on screen at once.

### Close, then run
A choice that opens a dialog (Browse channels, Set status, Create channel) must find focus where the palette found it and give focus back there. Running the choice in the same event as the close would let the new dialog read the palette's focus. The provider therefore sets the palette closed, keeps the choice in a ref, and runs it in an effect after the commit in which the dialog has unmounted.

### Recent
No time of last activity exists for channels in what the client holds (`lastMessageAt` is sent for direct messages and groups only, TOKI-037 R21), and nothing orders the person's own use of a conversation. The proposal (concern 3) is the person's own visits on this device: it needs no server field, holds ids only, and is what "recent" means to someone who presses Ctrl+K to go back.

The palette draws the messages in the order the route returns them, 20 at a time, and shows replies like any message. Whatever the owner answers changes the route and not the palette, except that a different order that cannot be continued by a cursor would change "Show more" (R10). A second difference for the owner: the prototype's palette leaves replies out (`!m.parent`) and TOKI-070 returns them (concern 6 below). Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
6. Opening a message result, and a reply in a thread. Not decided. F29 says "choosing a message opens its conversation at that message"; the jump and its flash are TOKI-068 and not written; a reply is not a row of the conversation's list unless it was also sent to the channel (TOKI-062 R12), and TOKI-070 returns replies with their `parentId`. Proposal, not a decision (R9 d): the palette calls one function with the conversation, the message and the parent id and leaves the rest to TOKI-068: for a message of the list the conversation at that message; for a reply, the thread panel of its parent (`/c/{id}/t/{parentId}`) with the reply in view. Until TOKI-068 exists the seam goes to `/c/{id}` and shows no message. If the owner wants replies left out of the palette, the condition is TOKI-069 R9's (concern 5). Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
7. Where a profile opens from the palette. Not decided. The palette opens on any screen; the profile panel has an address only inside a conversation (`/c/{id}/p/{personId}`, TOKI-057 R13) and TOKI-057 concern 6 leaves "the address each of them gives the panel" to the People screen and to this ticket; the technical design lists `/people` and no address for a profile; D-089 says each panel has an address. The prototype opens the panel over whatever screen is open. Proposal, not a decision (R9 b): `/people/{personId}`, the People screen with that person's profile in the panel, through `useOpenProfileAt`; TOKI-079 owns the route and the mount, and this ticket calls `useOpenPerson`. Until TOKI-079 exists the choice of a person does nothing. If the owner prefers the person's direct message with its profile card, the address is `/c/{dmId}/p/{personId}` and TOKI-057's existing route is enough, at the cost of creating a direct message by looking. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
8. What the commands of Pause, Focus time and Switch look do. Not decided. F29 lists "Pause notifications", "Start Focus time" and "Switch look" with no duration and no target look; the prototype's palette has one fixed option each ("Pause notifications for 20 seconds", a demo, "Start Focus time for 1 hour", and a toggle between Calm and Focus, "Calm / Focus"); the account menu has several durations, and Preferences will add "Match my device" (TOKI-078). Proposal, not a decision (R8, Contract): the owner tickets register their commands with `useRegisterCommand` and decide their words and their effect: TOKI-076 one command for a pause and one for Focus time (the duration being TOKI-076's decision, the prototype's one hour for Focus), TOKI-078 a command that sets the look to the other of Calm and Focus. This ticket registers none, so until those tickets are built F29's list is short of three commands. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
9. The palette while offline or after a failed request. Decided in this spec (R11): D-079 says every other action is blocked with an explanation and loaded content stays readable, so the palette does not call the route offline, keeps the Actions group (made in memory) and says why in a line; a failed request has a row that asks again; the controller asks again by itself when the connection returns. Reason: a search is a read that needs the server for three of its four kinds, and showing names the store holds would be a second match rule that the route's order and "Show more" cannot continue. The words of the three lines are proposals under D-139. Owner: Thanh Truong.
10. The way "more on request" looks. Decided in this spec (R10): a row at the end of the group that asks for the next page of that kind. Reason: D-112 says "more on request" and draws nothing; a row is reachable by the arrow keys and by touch (D-067), is not hover-only, and needs no scroll event inside a box that already scrolls for the arrows. Decided under D-019 (the prototype is the only source; the builder decides what it does not draw); the words are a proposal under D-139. Owner: Thanh Truong.
11. The pause before a search and one request at a time. Decided in this spec (R13): 150 ms after the last key, an older call aborted and an older answer dropped, groups cleared when the text changes. Reason: the route reads every conversation and person the person has (TOKI-070 R10) and should not run for every key of a word; 150 ms is a builder choice shorter than a typical gap between words and long enough to skip the keys inside one; a stale answer under a new text would let Enter open a result that no longer matches. The number is counted with the controllable clock in tests. Owner: Thanh Truong.
12. Where the controller lives. Decided in this spec (R13): in `packages/client-core/src/search/`, with the call, so the debounce, the sequence and the cursor logic run in Vitest with no DOM and are reused by a native client. Reason: the technical design keeps server calls and their state in `client-core` and interface state in components. Owner: Thanh Truong.
13. Ctrl+K while another dialog or menu is open. Decided in this spec (R1): the key is prevented and nothing opens. Reason: the prototype clears other overlays when it opens the palette, which a controlled layer here cannot do (a layer never closes another, TOKI-014 R8), and a palette over a form that holds typed text would lose the person's place (D-070). A person closes the dialog first. Owner: Thanh Truong.
14. How "Channels you can join" opens Browse channels. Decided in this spec (R9 c): Browse channels with the channel's name as its search text, through the registry of TOKI-033 with a new option `initialSearch`, as the prototype does (line 2178), and not a second Browse dialog owned by the palette. Reason: the prototype is the design source (D-019); one owner of the Browse dialog keeps the extra of TOKI-101 (restore for an Admin) and the focus return in one place; `BrowseChannelsDialog` already takes `initialSearch` (TOKI-034 R4). The alternative, the unjoined-channel view at the channel's address (TOKI-034 R16), shows the same Join button and is left out because the prototype does not use it. Owner: Thanh Truong.
15. The snippet and the highlight. Decided in this spec (R12): the first 70 code points of the text (the prototype's `msgSnippet`), or a window of 70 that starts 20 code points before the first match when the match is further in, with the matched letters marked. Reason: TOKI-070 concern 11 returns 300 code points and leaves the window and the highlight to the palette; a match that is not visible in the label would look like a wrong result. The 20 and the mark's style are builder choices. Owner: Thanh Truong.
16. The key hint. Decided in this spec (R15 a): "Ctrl K" (the prototype's) on every platform except Apple ones, where it reads "⌘ K"; both keys open the palette on every device. Reason: F29 says "Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac)"; the platform package has no operating-system member, so the one read of `navigator` lives in the palette's folder and is a hint only, never a condition for the key. Owner: Thanh Truong.
17. The quick actions of this ticket and their set. Decided in this spec (R8): the commands built from F29's list that exist when this ticket is built (Browse channels, New direct message, Set status, Create channel for an Admin, and "Go to" for Activity, Later, Files, People and Preferences when their routes exist), matched on their label only, an action absent when its owner is not built. The prototype's actions for Meet, Calendar, guest, automations, offline and AI are not phase 1. The words "New direct message" and "Create channel" are proposals under D-139. Owner: Thanh Truong.
