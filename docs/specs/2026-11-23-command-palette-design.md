# Command palette design

Date: 2026-11-23 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/command-palette.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-071

## Goal
A person needs one place that takes them anywhere: a conversation, a person, a channel they could join, a message, or a quick action such as "Set status". This ticket builds that place: the palette that opens with Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) or from the Search field, calls the search request of TOKI-070, draws its groups, and lets the keyboard (or a finger) choose.
The palette decides nothing about who may be found: the request answers only what the person may see (TOKI-070 R4 to R7), and the palette draws it as it comes. What it owns is the screen (groups, keys, focus, empty and failed states), the quick actions and the list of recent conversations, the way it asks for more, the call and its state in `client-core`, and the entry points (the Search field of the shell, the Search field of the narrow Home, the opener for the More sheet).
Some of what the log says about the palette is open or disagrees with itself: the order after exact matches (D-187), the groups (D-112 against F29), what "recent" means, the narrow entry, how a message is opened, where a profile opens, and what the commands of Pause, Focus time and Switch look do. Each is an area of concern with a marked proposal; the requirements are written as the proposal, and the sentence "Check" of each says what changes if the owner answers otherwise.

## Scope
- In:
  - The palette dialog and its parts: the field, the groups, the rows, the footer, the empty, offline and failed states (R2, R4 to R6, R11, R12).
  - The opening and closing keys, the hint on the Search field, the one function that opens it (`useOpenPalette`) and the narrow entry (R1, R15).
  - The keys inside: arrows, Enter, Esc, input methods (R3).
  - What is shown before typing: recent conversations and the quick actions (R4, R14).
  - The groups and the rows of the search request, the order they keep, "Show more" (R5, R7, R10).
  - The quick actions: the registry, the actions this ticket registers, the Admin-only "Create channel" (R8).
  - What choosing a result does (R9).
  - The highlight of the match and the window of a message (R12).
  - The call of `GET /api/v1/search` from the web app and its state: debounce, one request at a time, stale answers dropped (R13).
  - The texts, styles, wiring and tests (R16 to R19).
- Out (not now):
  - The request, who may be found, the order of names and of messages, the cursor and the 20 per kind: TOKI-070. The index and the word rule for messages: TOKI-069. This ticket does not rank again what the route ordered (R7).
  - The search inside one conversation (its messages, matched on text and sender): TOKI-072. The palette searches every conversation the person belongs to; TOKI-072 searches one, on its own route, and neither re-ranks the other's answer. The Files search is made by the server list of TOKI-063 (its R3), not on data the client holds, and has no row in the palette: the palette's only door to Files is the action "Go to Files" (R8 (5)). The conversation list's search, Browse channels and the people picker's search filter data the client holds: TOKI-033, TOKI-034.
  - Opening a message and the flash on it: TOKI-068, which owns the one hook `useOpenMessage` that this ticket, TOKI-062, TOKI-063 and TOKI-072 call (R9 (d), Plan changes). The People screen and the address of a profile outside a conversation: TOKI-079. The profile panel: TOKI-057.
  - The More sheet that draws the Search row: TOKI-092. It calls `useOpenPalette` of this ticket (R1).
  - The commands "Pause notifications" and "Start Focus time" (TOKI-076) and "Switch look" (TOKI-078): their owners register them in the registry of R8; this ticket registers none of them, so the palette never shows a dead command.
  - A shortcut for anything but opening the palette, and a settings page for shortcuts: not in phase 1.
  - Anything of the guest portal, Meet, Calendar, Tasks or AI that the prototype's palette lists: not phase 1 (D-002, D-008, D-152).

## Requirements
- R1. Opening and closing. `PaletteProvider` (`apps/web/src/features/command-palette/PaletteProvider.tsx`) is mounted once by `AppShellRoute`, inside the shell's providers, and holds the open state. `useOpenPalette()` returns one stable function `open(options?: { returnFocusRef? })`; the More sheet of TOKI-092 calls it. While the provider is mounted, one `keydown` listener on `document`, in the capture phase, opens the palette on `Ctrl+K` or `Cmd+K` (`event.key.toLowerCase() === "k"` with `ctrlKey` or `metaKey`, and no `altKey` or `shiftKey`; on every device and in every field, also a text field, as the prototype does) and closes it on the same key while it is open. It calls `preventDefault` on every such event, so the browser's own Ctrl+K never runs. It acts only when the event is not part of an input method composition (`isImeEvent` of TOKI-043 R2), and it does not open the palette while another modal layer or menu is open (`document.querySelector('[aria-modal="true"], [role="menu"]')` finds an element other than the palette's own; every `Modal` and `Dialog` of TOKI-014 sets `aria-modal`, and the Details and thread side panels, which are not modal layers, do not block it): the key is then prevented and nothing else happens. This is a proposal, not a decision (concern 13): F29 says the palette opens "on any screen" and the prototype opens it over the screen and closes the open menu (`openPalette`, line 2208; it draws the palette in place of an open dialog, line 2136), so the owner may want the palette to stack over the dialog or to close it. The listener is removed when the provider unmounts, so nothing opens it on `/login`. Check: with a Testing Library render of the provider, `Ctrl+K` and `Meta+K` open it, a second press closes it, `Ctrl+Shift+K` and `Ctrl+Alt+K` do not, the event has `defaultPrevented` true, a press while an open `Modal` (or a `Menu`) is on screen leaves it alone and the palette closed, a press while a side panel is open opens the palette, a press with `isComposing` true does nothing, and after unmount a press changes nothing.
- R2. The dialog. The palette is one `Modal` of TOKI-014 in its form without `title` (R5 (b)): `aria-label` `t("palette.label")` ("Command palette", the prototype), `className="pal"`, `initialFocusRef` the field, `returnFocusRef` the element that had focus when it opened (or the one `open` was given), `backdrop` `dim`, and `onRequestClose` that closes it for every reason (`escape`, `outside`). `Modal` is not changed: the scrim is a grid with `padding: 16px` (12px under 720 px) that centres its child, and the rule `.modal.pal { width: min(600px, 100%); align-self: start; margin-top: 8vh }` puts the panel near the top, as the prototype does (lines 404, 424 and 2196; the prototype's scrim has the same padding; TOKI-014's overlays spec left this placement to this ticket, and a class on the panel is enough). The selector is `.modal.pal`, two classes, so that it wins over `.modal { width: min(520px, 100%) }` of `Modal.css` whatever the order of the style files. The panel's top is therefore 16px + 8vh from the window's top (12px with `margin-top: 0` under 720 px). The panel holds, in this order: the field row (a `SearchIcon`, an `input type="search"` with `data-autofocus`, `aria-label` `t("palette.field.label")` ("Search", the prototype), `placeholder` `t("palette.field.placeholder")` ("Search conversations, people, actions", the prototype), `autocomplete="off"`, `autocapitalize="off"`, `spellcheck="false"`, and, from 720 px, a `span.kbd` with `t("palette.key.esc")` ("Esc"); under 720 px an `IconButton` (`icon="x"`, `label` `t("palette.close")`, 40 px) that closes the palette, so that a phone has a touch close beside the press on the scrim, D-067), the results box `div.pres` (`max-height: min(420px, 60vh)`, scrolling), which holds the listbox `div.plist` (R3) and, under it, the status lines of R5 and R11, and the footer `div.pfoot` with three hints: `↑` `↓` and `t("palette.footer.move")` ("move"), `t("palette.key.enter")` ("Enter") and `t("palette.footer.open")` ("open"), `t("palette.key.esc")` and `t("palette.footer.close")` ("close") (the prototype's words). On a window under 720 px the footer is not drawn (there is no keyboard to hint) and `.modal.pal` has `margin-top: 0` (the prototype's line 559). The palette is not drawn for a person who is not signed in. Check: a render test finds one `role="dialog"` named "Command palette", the field focused, the three footer hints, and under 720 px no footer, no "Esc" and a close button that closes the palette; a browser test at 1100 px reads the panel's top (16 px plus 8% of the window height, within 1 px; 12 px at 719 px) and its width (600 px), and that `.modal.pal` wins over `.modal` for the width, and at 360 by 560 px the panel is inside the window with no horizontal scroll.
- R3. Keys inside. The field is an ARIA combobox: `role="combobox"`, `aria-expanded="true"`, `aria-controls` the listbox `div.plist` (`role="listbox"`; it holds groups and options only, and the status lines of R5 and R11 are outside it), `aria-activedescendant` the id of the active row (the attribute is removed when there is no row), `aria-autocomplete="list"`. Each choosable row is `role="option"` with an id and `aria-selected`; each group has a heading `div.g` and `role="group"` with `aria-labelledby` that heading. There is one active row at a time. `ArrowDown` makes the next row active and `ArrowUp` the previous one, wrapping from the last to the first and from the first to the last (the prototype); `Home` and `End` are left to the field. The active row scrolls into view (`block: "nearest"`). `Enter` runs the active row's choice (R9) and closes the palette. `Escape` closes it (the Esc of TOKI-014 R9 does this; nothing else handles it). Every one of these keys is ignored when it is part of an input method composition (`isImeKey` of `useComposition`, TOKI-043 R2: a person typing Vietnamese with Telex or VNI presses Enter to end a word, and that Enter must not open the active row), and `preventDefault` is called for the arrows and for an Enter that runs a row, never for an ignored one. The first row is active when the list changes (a new query, a loaded group); loading more of a group keeps the active row where it is. A press on a row runs its choice, and moving the pointer over a row makes it active. With no row, Enter does nothing. A "Show more" row (R10) and the retry rows of R11 are rows too. Check: `user-event` tests for each key, for the wrap in both directions, for Enter on a row of each kind (a spy on the choice), for a keydown with `isComposing` true and one with `keyCode` 229 and `isComposing` false (no choice, no `preventDefault`), for `compositionstart`, Enter, `compositionend`, Enter (one choice), and for the roles, the `aria-activedescendant` after each key and its absence when no row is drawn, and that the listbox holds no `role="status"`; a browser test drives the real input method with the CDP command `Input.imeSetComposition` ("Việt") and finds that Enter changes nothing, then commits the word and finds the results of "Việt".
- R4. Before typing. With an empty field (after trimming) the palette draws two groups, with no request: `t("palette.group.recent")` ("Recent", a proposal, concern 3) with up to `PALETTE_RECENT_MAX` (5, proposal, concern 3) conversations from the list of R14, newest first, and `t("palette.group.actions")` ("Actions", the prototype) with every quick action of R8 in the order of the registry. A group with nothing in it has no heading. The first row is active. Check: a render with three recent conversations and the built-in actions finds "Recent" with three rows then "Actions"; with no recent conversation there is no "Recent"; the search call is never made (a spy); the rows are in the order of the lists.
- R5. While typing. The text typed is trimmed at both ends (`String.prototype.trim`) and, when it is empty, R4 applies. Otherwise the controller of R13 asks the route for the first page of every kind (no `kind`), after the pause of R13, and the palette draws these groups, each only when it has a row, in this order (concern 2): `t("palette.group.conversations")` "Conversations", `t("palette.group.people")` "People", `t("palette.group.channels")` "Channels you can join", `t("palette.group.actions")` "Actions", then `t("palette.group.messages")` "Messages" (the five words are the prototype's; the order is F29's alone: the prototype draws Conversations, People, Channels you can join, Messages and then Actions, and keeps at most 4 messages, `paletteItems`, lines 2173 to 2194). The Actions group does not wait for the route: it is made on every key from the registry (R8) and is on screen while the route is still answering. Messages are asked for by the route from 2 characters (TOKI-070 R7); the palette draws nothing about that rule and never hides the group by itself. While the route is answering, a line `t("palette.searching")` ("Searching…", a proposal under D-139; a `role="status"` element under the listbox, not inside it) is drawn and the groups of the previous text are gone (the answer of an older text is never drawn under a newer one, R13). Check: a render with a fake route answer holding the four kinds finds the five headings in the order above; an answer with no channels has no "Channels you can join"; while the answer is pending the Actions group and the line are drawn and no group of the route; typing a second text removes the groups of the first.
- R6. The rows. Every row is one `button` (`type="button"`, `tabIndex={-1}`, so Tab leaves the dialog's field and the arrows move) with an icon or an `Avatar` (24 px) and `span.grow.ell` holding the label and, after a space, `span.muted.xs` with the second text (the prototype, line 2203). (a) A conversation (`SearchConversation`, TOKI-070 R3): label `conversationName(entry, userId, people)` of `packages/client-core/src/conversations/names.ts` (the function TOKI-070 calls `getConversationName`, TOKI-032 R17), with `userId` of the signed-in person and `people` from the store; its first parameter, today a `BootstrapConversation`, becomes the structural type `{ kind; name: string | null; memberIds: readonly string[] | null }` that both a `BootstrapConversation` and a `SearchConversation` fit (a change to that file made here, with its tests unchanged); a channel whose `name` is null, or a direct message or a group whose `memberIds` is null, gives the empty text as a direct message with nobody known does today; icon `lock` for a private channel and `hash` for another, `users` for a group, and the `Avatar` (`personHue`, TOKI-033 R28) of the other person for a direct message; second text the topic of a channel. An archived channel carries the word `t("palette.row.archived")` ("Archived", the word of Browse channels, TOKI-034) as its second text before the topic. (b) A person (`SearchPerson`): `Avatar` of the person, label `displayName`, second text the job title when the store's person carries one (TOKI-057 concern 5) and nothing otherwise. (c) A channel to join (`SearchChannel`): icon `hash`, label the name, second text the topic. (d) A message (`SearchMessage`): icon `home` (the prototype's) and label the snippet of R12, second text `t("palette.row.messageIn", { author, conversation })` ("{author} in {conversation}"; the prototype's "first name in #channel", with the full display name because the first load carries no short name, TOKI-032 R17; the conversation is `#` and the `conversationName` of the result for a channel, and for a direct message or a group, where the route gives `conversationName: null` (TOKI-070 R7), the name of R6 (a) built from the entry that `useConversationEntry(conversationId)` returns, as the prototype's `cname`); with an author the store does not hold, the second text is `t("palette.row.messageInOnly", { conversation })` ("in {conversation}"); when the store holds no entry for a direct message or a group, the conversation part is left out and the second text is the author alone; with neither, there is no second text. (e) An action: label, optional second text, icon from the registry. (f) Deactivated people. The route does not list them in People (TOKI-070 R5), but it returns a direct message with one and their old messages, and TOKI-070 concern 12 gives the label "Deactivated" to the palette, from the status the client holds (F41). So the row of a direct message whose other person has the status `deactivated` in the store has `t("palette.row.deactivated")` ("Deactivated") as its second text, and in a message row the author's name is written `t("palette.row.deactivatedName", { name })` ("{name} (Deactivated)"); a group row carries no mark; a person the store does not hold carries none. Under 720 px every row is at least 40 px high (D-067, F38; the prototype has 38 px for every width, a change made here, as TOKI-033 R14 does for the list). Check: one render test for each kind with a fake answer, reading the roles, the icon names, the labels and the second texts; the archived word; the group's icon; a message whose author is not in the store, one with a deactivated author, a direct message with a deactivated person and a message in a direct message or a group whose entry the store lacks; `min-height` 40 px at 360 px and 38 px at 1100 px in the browser test.
- R7. Order. The palette keeps the order in which the route returned the items of the four server groups and does not sort, filter or rank them again (TOKI-070's concern, "must not rank again what the route already ordered"): the answer is the order, the first 20 of a kind are the route's first 20, and the rows appended by "Show more" follow them. The two groups the palette builds itself are ordered with the functions of `@toki/shared` that TOKI-070 R6 exports for this: the Actions group by `nameRank`, then `compareSearchNames` over `foldText` of the action's label and the folded query (rank 0 equal, 1 starts with, 2 contains; an action whose label has rank `null` is not drawn; ties by the registry's order: the sort key of an action is its folded label and its id the registry's own id), and the Recent group by recency (R14) with no query. There is no fuzzy match and no word-start rank in this ticket (concern 1). Check: a test gives the palette an answer in a deliberately non-alphabetical, non-ranked order and finds the rows in that order; a table test of the Actions group for the queries `br` (Browse channels first), `status` and `s` (every label that contains an `s`, ranked by the rule), `Đặt` against a Vietnamese label, and a query that matches nothing; the order of two actions with an equal rank follows the registry.
- R8. Quick actions. `apps/web/src/features/command-palette/commands.tsx` exports `CommandsProvider`, `useRegisterCommand(command | null)` and `useCommands()`, with `Command = { id: string; label: string; detail?: string; icon: IconName; run(): void }`. `useRegisterCommand` registers the command while the calling component is mounted and removes it when it unmounts or passes `null`; for one id the last registration wins, and an unmount removes a command only when it is still the registered one (the rule of `useRegisterListActions`, TOKI-033 R25, and `useRegisterAccountEntry`, TOKI-099 R3); the registry order is the order of first registration. A command is drawn only while it is registered, so a command whose feature is not built is not in the palette (the rule of the account menu: an entry appears when its own ticket is built). This ticket registers, from `PaletteCommands` (mounted by `PaletteProvider`), these commands, each with the label from `t(...)` in the current language, matched on its label only (concern 1): (1) "Browse channels" (`palette.action.browse`, the prototype's words; `detail` "Join or rejoin a channel", `palette.action.browse.detail`; icon `hash`) which calls `browseChannels` of `useListActions()` (TOKI-033 R25) and is not registered while that opener is not; (2) "New direct message" (`palette.action.newDirect`; icon `plus`, not the pencil, D-155) which calls `startDirect` of the same registry; (3) "Set status" (`palette.action.setStatus`, the prototype's words; icon `smile`) which calls `useOpenStatusDialog()` of TOKI-056 R15; (4) "Create channel" (`palette.action.createChannel`; icon `plus`), registered only when `useCurrentRole()` of TOKI-098 R3 is `"admin"` (a Member or an unknown role registers nothing: D-084, D-151; the server refuses a Member's request in any case), which calls `open()` of `useCreateChannelDialog` (TOKI-098 R4), whose `element` the provider renders; (5) "Go to Activity", "Go to Later", "Go to Files", "Go to People" and "Go to Preferences" (`palette.action.goTo`, "Go to {section}", the prototype's form, with the section's own label from `nav.*`; the icon of the section), one for each row of `visibleSections(role)` (TOKI-015 R36) whose `id` is `activity`, `later`, `files`, `people` or `preferences` (the five of F29), so a section that is not built or not allowed has no command; each navigates to the section's `to`. There is no "Go to Home" and no "Go to Admin": F29 lists neither. The commands of "Switch look", "Pause notifications" and "Start Focus time" are registered by TOKI-078 and TOKI-076 (concern 8); the palette needs nothing more for them. A command's `run` is called after the palette has closed (R9). Check: a registry test (register, replace, unmount only the owner's, `null`, order); with a Member nothing named "Create channel" exists in the palette, in any language, and with an Admin it does and its choice opens the dialog; a `role` change adds or removes it with no reload; a section row that is absent from `visibleSections` has no "Go to"; with `browseChannels` not registered there is no "Browse channels"; each registered command's `run` calls its target (spies); a Vietnamese render shows the Vietnamese labels and matches them folded.
- R9. Choosing. A choice (Enter, a press) first closes the palette, then runs: the palette's state is set closed, and the choice is run from an effect of `PaletteProvider` after the dialog has unmounted and returned focus (the dialog's own effects run before its parent's, TOKI-014 R13 and R33), so that a dialog that the choice opens finds focus where the palette left it and returns focus there. By kind: (a) a conversation or a channel the person belongs to, `navigate({ to: "/c/$conversationId", params })`, a push (D-089), through `useGo` of the router seam (R19). When the store does not hold the id (`hasConversation` false: an archived channel the person belongs to, which TOKI-070 returns with `archived: true`, has no store entry), `readChannel(id)` of `deps.ts` (`conversations.readChannel`) runs first and the move follows whether it succeeds or fails, as `ConversationStarters.openChannel` does for Browse channels, because the page of such a channel is drawn only when the store holds its detail (TOKI-101 owns what that page shows). (b) A person: `useOpenPerson()(personId)` of `apps/web/src/features/command-palette/deps.ts`, which opens the profile panel of TOKI-057 at the People screen's address with `useOpenProfileAt` (TOKI-057 R11, R17); the address is concern 7. (c) A channel to join: `browseChannels({ initialSearch: channel.name })` of the registry of TOKI-033, so Browse channels opens with the name as its search text, as the prototype does (line 2178); this needs `ListAction`'s options to carry `initialSearch` (Plan changes, concern 14). (d) A message: `useOpenMessage()` of TOKI-068, re-exported by `deps.ts`, returns `open(target: { conversationId: string; messageId: string; parentId: string | null })`; the palette calls it with the `conversationId`, `id` and `parentId` of the result and nothing else. This is the one way to open a message for the palette, TOKI-062 (its `OpenMessageContext` and the Files results), TOKI-063 and TOKI-072. Its rule, asked of TOKI-068 in Plan changes: a message of the list (`parentId` null) opens the conversation at the message with the jump and the flash; a reply opens the thread panel of its root at the address of TOKI-045 R16 with `?reply={messageId}`, through `useOpenThread(conversationId)(rootId, replyId)` (the optional second argument `replyId` that TOKI-045 R16 gives it), with a new history entry and `state: { threadPushed: true }`, replacing the entry when the address already is a thread (the replace rule of TOKI-045 R16), the same as TOKI-072 R12 does for a reply of its own. `parentId` is required in the type of `target` (`string | null`, TOKI-068 concern 1), so a call that leaves it out does not compile; the palette always passes it. `deps.ts` re-exports the hook of TOKI-068 as it is, with no stand-in of its own (concern 6). (e) An action: the command's `run`. A choice made while the connection is down is run all the same: what needs the connection gates itself with `useOnlineGate` (D-079), and the palette adds no gate to a choice; its only gate is the one on the search call (R11 (b), R13). The query and the results are dropped when the palette closes (R18). Check: for each kind, a test chooses a row and asserts the order (the dialog is gone from the document before the target is called: a spy that records `queryByRole("dialog")`), the target and its arguments (for a conversation the store lacks, `readChannel` before the move, also when it fails), and that focus is on the element that had it before the palette opened when the target opens nothing; a test with a target that opens a `Modal` finds the focus in that modal and, on its close, back on the first opener.
- R10. More on request. A group of the route (Conversations, People, Channels you can join, Messages) whose `nextCursor` is not null ends with one row, a "Show more" row (`t("palette.more")`, "Show more", a proposal under D-019 and D-139; D-112 says only "more on request"), with the same look as the others. A choice of it (Enter or a press) does not close the palette: it asks the route for the next page of that kind (`kind` and `cursor`, R13), appends the items below the last row of the group, and keeps the active row on the first appended row (the one that followed the previous last row); while the request runs the row is `aria-busy="true"`, shows `t("palette.moreLoading")` ("Loading…") and a second choice does nothing (D-111); when the page has no `nextCursor` the row is gone. A message page may hold fewer than 20 items, or none, while `nextCursor` is not null (TOKI-070 R7): the row stays until a page comes with `nextCursor` null. A failed request keeps the items, shows `t("palette.moreFailed")` ("Could not load more. Try again.") on the row, and the next choice asks again. The Actions group has no such row: all its rows are drawn (there are fewer than 20). Check: a test with a fake route that returns 20 items and a cursor, chooses the row, receives 20 more and finds 40 rows once each in order, the active row on the 21st, the row busy during the request and absent after the last page; a double Enter sends one request; a failed request leaves 20 rows and the failure text, and the next Enter succeeds; a message page that returns 0 items with a cursor keeps the row.
- R11. Empty, offline and failed. (a) Nothing at all (no group of the route has a row, the Actions group is empty, and the state is `ready`): the line `t("palette.empty", { query })` (`No results for "{query}"`, the prototype's words, line 2201; D-074: written for this context), centred, `role="status"`, under the listbox, with the trimmed text as typed. (b) Offline. The gate sits in the controller of R13: when the pause of a typed text ends, `createPaletteSearch` calls its `isOnline` option (a function the app gives it: `apps/web/src/client.ts` passes `isOnline` of `patterns/online.ts`, the one function that tells whether the connection is up, TOKI-082 setting it with `setConnectionCheck`; `client-core` reads no browser global). When it returns false the route is not called (D-079: every other action is blocked with an explanation) and the state is `offline`. No subscription is needed: the check is read at that moment, on `retry()` and on `loadMore`, and never kept. The Actions group is still drawn (it is made in memory) and one choosable row follows it, with the label `t("palette.offline")` ("You are offline. Search needs a connection.", a proposal under D-139) and the second text `t("palette.retry")` ("Try again"); a choice of the row, or any new key (a changed text), calls the controller again, which reads `isOnline()` again, so the search comes back as soon as the person asks after the connection returns, with no event to listen to (the palette does not ask by itself; a later `retry()` call from the state of TOKI-082 would be one line in `usePaletteSearch` and is not part of this ticket). With a text and no action matching while offline, the row of (b) is drawn and not (a). Whether the conversations and people that the client already holds are searched in memory while offline is the owner's (concern 9); this ticket does not do it. ## Design
### How the pieces fit
```
Ctrl+K / Cmd+K  ──┐
Search field   ───┼─> PaletteProvider (open state, recent, choice runner)
More sheet ───────┘      │
(useOpenPalette)         ├─> Palette (Modal, form b) ── field ── listbox
                         │       ├─ Recent / Actions   (no text: R4)
                         │       └─ Conversations / People / Channels / Actions / Messages (R5)
                         ├─> CommandsProvider  <── PaletteCommands (this ticket)
                         │                     <── TOKI-076, TOKI-078 register theirs
                         └─> usePaletteSearch ─> createPaletteSearch (client-core)
                                                   └─> GET /api/v1/search (TOKI-070)
```
The palette is a view over four things it does not own: the route's answer, the store, the command registry and the list of recent ids. It owns the rows' look, the keys, the one active row, and the order of the groups.

### Why the palette does not rank again
Decided in this spec (concern 1, part of it). TOKI-070 orders names with one rule (rank, then folded name, then id) and messages by time, and its pages are cut at 20 by that order, so a second sort on the client would reorder the first 20 against the server's and would break "Show more", which continues after the server's last item. The palette therefore draws the answer in the order it came. The only items it orders itself are the ones the server never sees (the actions), and it does so with the server's own functions so the two rules are one.

### Why messages and names come in one request
One request with no `kind` answers the four kinds in one round trip and one snapshot (TOKI-070 R10), so a person who types "lan" sees conversations, people, channels and messages that agree with each other. "Show more" then asks for one kind with its cursor. The Actions group is made locally, so it is on screen at once.

### Where this and the conversation search meet
| | Palette (this ticket, on TOKI-070) | Search inside a conversation (TOKI-072) |
|---|---|---|
| Asks | every conversation the person belongs to, with people, channels to join and actions | one conversation |
| Matches | names, and the text of messages (TOKI-070 R6, R7); the sender is not matched | the text and the sender's name |
| Route | `GET /api/v1/search`, `kind` and `cursor` for "Show more" | its own route; TOKI-070's is not changed |
| Answer | four kinds, each a page; a message carries `conversationName` | `{ items, nextCursor }` of its own item, with no conversation name |
| Refused case | 200 with an empty page | 404 or 403, as the history |
| Shared | `foldText`, `searchWords`, `splitIndexedWords`, the newest-first order and the cursor of messages, `useOpenMessage` of TOKI-068 | the same |
Neither re-ranks, folds again or filters what its route returned (R7), and neither draws the other's rows.

### Close, then run
A choice that opens a dialog (Browse channels, Set status, Create channel) must find focus where the palette found it and give focus back there. Running the choice in the same event as the close would let the new dialog read the palette's focus. The provider therefore sets the palette closed, keeps the choice in a ref, and runs it in an effect after the commit in which the dialog has unmounted.

### Recent
No time of last activity exists for channels in what the client holds (`lastMessageAt` is sent for direct messages and groups only, TOKI-037 R21), and nothing orders the person's own use of a conversation. The proposal (concern 3) is the person's own visits on this device: it needs no server field, holds ids only, and is what "recent" means to someone who presses Ctrl+K to go back. `conversationList.lastViewed` (TOKI-033 R22) holds one id, so it cannot give five; the new key follows the naming of `conversationList.filter` and `conversationList.collapsed` (TOKI-033 R18) and the same `{ userId, value }` shape.
