# Later design

Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/later.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-067
Needs: TOKI-064 (the job queue: `defineJob`, `JobDeps`, `enqueue` and `cancelByKey` with `tx`; spec on main), TOKI-068 (`useOpenMessage`; spec on main), TOKI-037 (the message rows, `buildMessageEntriesByIds` of TOKI-070 R9 (b), `getConversationFacts`), TOKI-038 (the event log writer and the `user` scope), TOKI-040 (`registerEventHandler`, the sync seams), TOKI-052 (the bar and menu entries, `entries.ts`), TOKI-094 (`quickTimeChoices`, `validateFutureInstant`, merged), TOKI-093 (`useUndo`, `useOnlineGate`, the action id `removeFromLater`, merged). Written at the same time: TOKI-065 (Activity, server) and TOKI-102 (notification preferences, server); read them at the end of the work (Plan changes).

## Goal
A person who sees a message they must deal with later has nowhere to put it. This ticket builds Later: a personal list of saved messages with two states, In progress and Completed, an optional reminder on each, a reminder that fires on time through the job queue, a badge on the Later item of the navigation that counts reminders that have come due, and the page, the bookmark and the reminder entry that use them. The server keeps the list, the web app shows it, and the same list and badge appear on every device of the person.

## Scope
- In:
  - The `later` module of `apps/server`: the table `later_items`, four routes (list, save, change, remove), the reminder job and its handler, the change `later_changed` and the event `later.changed`, the port through which a due reminder is announced (R1 to R14).
  - The schemas and constants in `packages/shared`, the API calls and the store of `packages/client-core` (R2, R15, R16).
  - The Later page at `/later`, its rows, the reminder menu, Undo on removal, the badge, the reminder toast and the texts (R17 to R24).
  - The two entries this ticket adds to the message bar: Save for Later (the bookmark button) and Remind me about this (R19).
- Out (not now), each with its owner:
  - Everything that decides whether a due reminder makes a toast, a push or a sound while Pause or Focus time is on, and Pause and Focus themselves: TOKI-076 (the data and the time of Pause and Focus) and TOKI-073 (the decision), behind the port of R11. This ticket says what a held reminder is and who fires it afterwards (R11, concern 3).
  - The push itself: TOKI-074. The in-app sound and the toast rules for notifications in general: TOKI-075. This ticket draws only the content of the reminder toast and shows it when it is not held (R22).
  - The Activity screen and its snooze: TOKI-066. The pure rule that makes the time choices is `quickTimeChoices` of TOKI-094; the labels, the hook and the menu that both screens use are TOKI-066's, and this ticket uses them (R23, concern 6).
  - The More sheet and the badge of its tab on a narrow screen: TOKI-092. This ticket exports the number it shows (R20).
  - The summary at the end of Pause or Focus: TOKI-076. This ticket exports the read it needs (R12).
  - The action bar and its menu as such: TOKI-052. This ticket adds one line in `useBarSpecs` and one in `useMenuSpecs` (R19).
  - How the list opens a message and flashes it: TOKI-068; this ticket calls `useOpenMessage` (R18).
  - Saving while offline (queued writes): a later phase (D-060 says phase 6 queues saves to Later). Offline, a write is blocked with an explanation (D-079, R21).
  - Phone and desktop push content, mail, Tasks ("Turn into task") and Send later: not this ticket.

## Requirements
Server: table and rules
- R1. The module and the table. `apps/server/src/modules/later/` holds the Drizzle table `later_items` (`schema.ts`, not exported from `index.ts`) and a migration written for it (TOKI-005). Columns: `id` uuid primary key (UUIDv7 made by the server), `workspace_id` uuid not null, `user_id` uuid not null, `message_id` uuid not null, `conversation_id` uuid not null (the conversation of the message, copied once at save: a message never changes conversation), `saved_at` timestamptz not null, `updated_at` timestamptz not null, `completed_at` timestamptz null, `remind_at` timestamptz null, `fired_at` timestamptz null, `due` boolean not null. Foreign keys are written in the SQL of the migration (workspace, user, message, conversation). One row for each person and message: a unique constraint on (`user_id`, `message_id`). Checks: `due` is true only when `fired_at` is not null; `remind_at` and `completed_at` are not both set (concern 1, proposal). An index on `user_id`. No time column has a database default; every time is read from the clock of TOKI-008 (TOKI-024 R6). The table holds no message text, no name and no title: it holds identifiers and times. `index.ts` is the only public entry and exports `laterRoutes`, `laterReminderJob`, `createFireReminderHandler`, `listRemindersFiredBetween` (R12) and the types. Check: a database test reads the columns, the unique constraint, the two checks and the index from the catalog; two rows for one person and message are refused; `due` true with `fired_at` null is refused; `db:generate` reports no change; the export test of the module lists these names.
- R2. Shared schemas and constants. `packages/shared/src/later/` (exported from the package index) holds, as strict Zod objects: `LaterItemStateSchema` with `id`, `messageId`, `conversationId`, `savedAt`, `completedAt` (null or ISO 8601), `remindAt` (null or ISO 8601), `due` (boolean), `firedAt` (null or ISO 8601); `LaterSourceSchema`, a union on `state`: `available` with `authorId`, `parentId` (null or the root's id), `createdAt`, `snippet` (string) and `fileName` (null or string); `deleted` with `deletedBy` (`"author"` or `"admin"`); `unavailable` with nothing else; `LaterItemEntrySchema` = the state plus `source`; `SaveLaterBodySchema` `{ conversationId, messageId, remindAt?, completed? }`; `UpdateLaterBodySchema` `{ completed?, remindAt?, acknowledged? }` (R5); the answers `LaterListResponseSchema` `{ items, number }` and `LaterChangeResponseSchema` `{ item, number }`; `LATER_SNIPPET_MAX` = 140 (the prototype cuts the text of a row at 140 characters, `msgSnippet(m, 140)`); and the event schema of R13. Times are ISO 8601 UTC strings. `remindAt` in a body is an ISO 8601 string. Check: a schema test accepts each shape and refuses an added key, a missing key, a `state` that is not listed, a non-ISO time, `due` as a string and a body with both `completed: true` and a `remindAt` that is not null; a type test imports every name from `@toki/shared`.
- R3. Save. `POST /api/v1/later` takes `SaveLaterBodySchema` and needs a valid session (401). Steps in order: (1) the body schema (400 `validation_failed`); (2) `getConversationFacts(db, actor.id, conversationId)` of TOKI-028: `null`, or a private channel, a DM or a group of which the actor is not a member, is 404 `not_found`; (3) `canReadConversation` of `@toki/shared`: a person who is not a member of a public channel is 403 `forbidden` (D-116); a channel archived is readable (concern 5, proposal); (4) `remindAt`, when given, passes `validateFutureInstant` of TOKI-094 R14 with the clock of the request (R6); (5) one statement reads the message with `buildMessageEntriesByIds(db, workspaceId, [conversationId], [messageId])` (TOKI-070 R9 (b)): no entry, an entry of `kind` `system`, or an entry with `deletedAt` set is 404 `not_found` with the same body as step 2, so a message that is not in that conversation is not confirmed; a reply (an entry with a `parentId`) is allowed (the prototype draws the bookmark in the thread panel too); (6) in one transaction, the locks in the order of TOKI-024 R39: the account row `FOR SHARE` (a deactivated account is 401 `account_deactivated`), then the insert `ON CONFLICT (user_id, message_id) DO NOTHING`; when a row was inserted it is the new item (`saved_at` and `updated_at` from the clock, `completed_at` set to the clock when the body has `completed: true`, `remind_at` from the body); when none was inserted the existing row is read `FOR UPDATE` and the body is applied to it by the rules of R4 (a body with no field changes nothing); (7) the timer of R7; (8) `recordChange` once, as the last write, when something changed (R13); (9) `getLastEventNumber` of TOKI-036 R19, given by `app.ts` as a dependency. The answer is 201 with `{ item, number }` for a new item, 200 for an existing one (`number` null and `item` unchanged when nothing changed: a double press changes nothing, D-111). The item in the answer holds its `source` (R9). A person saves only for themselves: no field names another person. Check: a table test of each refusal, with a positive case for a public, a private channel, a DM, a group and an archived channel; saving a system line and a deleted message is 404; saving a message under another conversation's id is 404 and the same body as an unknown conversation; the same body twice gives 201 then 200 with `number` null and one row, one `recordChange` call; a reply is saved; a person who is not a member of a public channel gets 403 and no row; a deactivated account gets 401; the statement log shows the account lock before the insert and `recordChange` as the last write; another person's Later list does not hold the message (AC-062, TS-097).
- R4. Change. `PATCH /api/v1/later/{messageId}` takes `UpdateLaterBodySchema`, a strict object with at least one key (an empty object is 400), and changes the caller's own item for that message; a message that is not in the caller's list is 404 `not_found` (another person's item cannot be told from none). `completed: true` sets `completed_at` to the clock when it is null, and clears `remind_at` and `due` (concern 1, proposal); `completed: false` sets `completed_at` to null and changes nothing else. `remindAt` set to an ISO time sets `remind_at`, clears `due` and, when the item is completed, moves it back to In progress (concern 1, proposal); `remindAt: null` clears `remind_at` (and leaves `due` as it is). `acknowledged: true` clears `due` and changes nothing else (the person opened the row, as in the prototype); `acknowledged: false` is 400. Two of `completed: true` and a non-null `remindAt` in one body are 400. A body that changes nothing (a value equal to the stored one) writes nothing, calls `recordChange` zero times and answers 200 with `number` null. Otherwise 200 `{ item, number }` with `updated_at` from the clock. The steps are those of R3 from step 4 on (the future rule, the account lock, the item row `FOR UPDATE`, the timer of R7, `recordChange` last, the number). No read of the conversation is made: the person's own list stays editable after they lost access to the conversation (concern 2). Check: each key and each refusal; completing a row that has a reminder clears the reminder and the job (R7); setting a reminder on a completed row makes it In progress; `acknowledged` clears `due` only; the same body twice gives one event; another person's item is 404.
- R5. Remove. `DELETE /api/v1/later/{messageId}` deletes the caller's row and answers 200 `{ item: null, number }`; a message that is not in the list is 200 `{ item: null, number: null }`, writes nothing and calls `recordChange` zero times (a retry after a lost answer is safe). The pending job is cancelled (R7). Check: a delete twice is 200 twice with one event; the job is gone after the delete; another person's row is not touched.
- R6. The time rule. A reminder time must be later than the clock of the request: `validateFutureInstant(remindAt, now)` of TOKI-094 R14, where `at <= now` is `past`; the route answers 400 `validation_failed` with `details.field` `remindAt` and `details.reason` `past` (TOKI-094 leaves the code to the ticket that has the field), and nothing is written; a value that is not an instant is `invalid`, with the same code. The time of the clock is read once for the request. No upper bound is set (concern 4). Check, with the controllable clock: now, now minus one second and now minus one year are refused; now plus one second and now plus one minute are accepted (TS-098, TS-099); a refused request leaves the item as it was, its earlier reminder included.
- R7. The timer. Inside the transaction of R3, R4 and R5, after the row is written and before `recordChange`: `deps.jobs.cancelByKey(laterReminderJob, key, { tx })` with `key` = `later-reminder:` + the item id, then, when the row now has a `remind_at`, `deps.jobs.enqueue(laterReminderJob, { itemId, remindAt }, { startAfter: remindAt, key, tx })` (TOKI-064 R11, R12, R14), so a rollback leaves neither the row nor the job. The job type is `laterReminderJob = defineJob({ name: "later.fire-reminder", payload: z.strictObject({ itemId: uuid, remindAt: ISO date-time }) })`, declared in `modules/later` and exported from its `index.ts`; its type is added to `apps/server/src/job-types.ts` and the factory `createFireReminderHandler(deps: JobDeps)` to `job-handlers.ts` (TOKI-064 R9). The payload holds an identifier and a time and never any text (TOKI-064 R7). `cancelByKey` stops only a job that has not started, so the handler checks the row (R8). Check, on a jobs database (`createJobsTestDb`, TOKI-064 R5): a save with a reminder leaves one job with `startAfter` equal to the reminder; changing the reminder leaves one job for the new time; completing, removing and clearing the reminder each leave none; a transaction that throws after the enqueue leaves no job and no row; a request made through the fake queue (`createFakeJobQueue`) records the calls in this order.
- R8. The handler. `createFireReminderHandler(deps)` returns `defineHandler(laterReminderJob, async (context, data) => ...)`. It first asks `deps.reminderNotifier.isHeld(userId, now)` (R11; `userId` is read from the row, so the check needs one read before the transaction), then runs one transaction: (1) the row is read `where id = $1 and remind_at = $2 and completed_at is null for update` with the exact time of the job, so an item that was removed, completed, given another reminder, or already fired (a second delivery of the same job) matches nothing, and the handler writes nothing and completes; (2) the account row `FOR SHARE`: for a deactivated account the handler does nothing and completes; (3) the row is updated: `due` true, `fired_at` the time of `context.clock`, `remind_at` null, `updated_at` the same time; (4) `deps.recordChange` once, as the last write, with the change of R13, reason `reminder_due` and `held` the answer of `isHeld`. After the commit, when `held` is false, it calls `deps.reminderNotifier.notify(input)` (R11); an error of `notify` is logged by TOKI-064 R18 and does not undo the due state (a retry of the job finds no row and sends nothing again; a push that failed is TOKI-074's to retry). Retry and backoff are the defaults of TOKI-064 R8. The handler reads no clock to decide whether the time has come: the queue hands the job over at `remindAt` (TOKI-064 R14); with the poll of TOKI-064 R8 a reminder fires within 2 seconds after its time while the worker is running, and late but still after the worker was stopped (concern 7). Check, with the library's `TestClock` and `enableSpies` (TOKI-064 R19): a job for a row of a pending reminder, with the clock moved to one millisecond before the time, is not run, and moved 2 seconds past it, sets `due`, `fired_at`, clears `remind_at` and writes one event; the same job delivered twice writes one event; a job whose row was changed to another time, completed or removed writes nothing; a deactivated account writes nothing; with `isHeld` true the row is due and the change says `held: true` and `notify` is not called; with false `notify` is called once with identifiers and times only; a failing `notify` leaves the row due and the job completed; the error message of the handler holds identifiers only (TOKI-064 R18).
- R9. The list and its sources. `GET /api/v1/later` needs a session and returns `{ items, number }`: `number` is `getLastEventNumber` read first, then one statement reads all the caller's rows, newest `saved_at` first (then `id`). The conversations the person may read are found by one statement over their member rows: this ticket adds `listReadableConversationIds(db, userId, conversationIds)` to the `conversations` module (exported by its `index.ts`, one statement, the person's own member rows only, archived channels included, so an Admin who is not a member has no extra right, D-047). For the rows of those conversations the entries are built with `buildMessageEntriesByIds` and one `listFiles` read of TOKI-058 R15 for the available messages (injected by `app.ts`; when it is not given `fileName` is null). The `source` of each row is: `available` when the conversation is readable and the entry is a message that is not deleted (`snippet` is the first `LATER_SNIPPET_MAX` code points of `text`, cut by code points and never in the middle of a pair, and `""` when the text is empty; `fileName` is the name of the first file or null; the snippet is the stored text as sent, with no rendering); `deleted` when the entry has `deletedAt` set (`deletedBy` from the entry; no text, no author); `unavailable` when the conversation is not readable by the person now (concern 2). The list holds no row of another person. An item whose message cannot be found at all is `deleted` (a message is never removed from the table). The number of statements is the same for any number of items (one for the number, one for the rows, one for the conversations, one for the entries, one for the files). Check: a person with 120 saved messages in 12 conversations gets 120 items, the statement count equals that of a person with 3 (counting fake); each `source` state has a fixture (a text of 300 characters is cut at 140, with an emoji at the cut not split; a message deleted by its author, by an admin; a conversation the person left, by SQL); no item of another person appears; the 401 case.
- R10. Cost and limits of the list. The list is returned whole in one answer, because the counts of the chips must equal the rows (D-074, concern 4); no page parameter exists. Check: a request with a `limit` key is 400 (the query is strict); a list of 1,000 rows is answered in the statement count of R9.
- R11. The port for a due reminder. `JobDeps` (TOKI-064 R9, its table "Fields of `JobDeps`") gains one field from this ticket, `reminderNotifier`, of the type `ReminderNotifier = { isHeld(userId: string, at: number): Promise<boolean>; notify(input: ReminderNotice): Promise<void> }` with `ReminderNotice = { userId, workspaceId, itemId, messageId, conversationId, firedAt: number }` (identifiers and a time, no text), built in `buildJobDeps` (TOKI-064 R9; no other configuration area is needed). Until TOKI-073 gives the real one, `buildJobDeps` builds the default `{ isHeld: async () => false, notify: async () => {} }`, so a reminder is never held and no push is sent; this is a stand-in and is named so in the code. The owners of what stands behind it: TOKI-076 knows whether Pause or Focus time covers a moment and gives that answer to TOKI-073; TOKI-073 builds the real `isHeld` (true while Pause or Focus time covers `at`, D-072) and the `notify` that decides push and sound (with TOKI-074 and TOKI-075 sending them). A reminder that is held is not a stored state: the item becomes due at its time exactly as an unheld one does (the row, the badge and the event, D-072: "badges still update"), and only the toast and the push are left out; the event carries `held: true` so the open clients draw no toast (R22). Nothing fires a held reminder afterwards, because D-072 says the end of Pause or Focus gives one summary of what arrived and no second notification: the summary is TOKI-076's and reads the rows through R12 (concern 3). A Pause that starts between the `isHeld` read and the commit leaves a toast that the person did not want; the window is the length of one handler run and is accepted (concern 3). Check: a type test of `ReminderNotifier`; the default is used when none is given and never holds; a fake that returns true holds as R8 says; no file of `modules/later` imports a Pause or notification module.
- R12. The read for the summary. `listRemindersFiredBetween(db, userId, fromMs, toMs)` of `modules/later`, exported by `index.ts`, returns `{ itemId, messageId, conversationId, firedAt }[]` for the person's rows whose `fired_at` is at or after `fromMs` and before `toMs`, oldest first, identifiers and times only. It is the one thing TOKI-076 reads from Later to count "what arrived" while it was on. A row that was removed since is not returned, and a row that was completed since is (it fired). Check: three rows fired at three times, one removed, one completed: the bounds include the lower and exclude the upper; another person's rows are not returned; no text is in the result.
- R13. The change and the event. `Change` of `conversations/changes.ts` gains a third member, `LaterChange`: `{ type: "later_changed", workspaceId, userIds: [userId], at, messageId, reason, item, held? }` with `reason` one of `saved`, `updated`, `removed`, `reminder_due`, `item` the `LaterItemState` of R2 (null for `removed`) and `held` for `reminder_due` only (the same way `MemberStatusChange` of TOKI-056 R8 has no `conversationId`; a handler narrows on `change.type`). The event handler of TOKI-038 gets one row (R20 there, "Rows of later tickets"): `later_changed` becomes the event `later.changed`, scope `user` (the one person of `userIds`), with `data` `{ messageId, reason, item, held }` (`held` false when absent). The kind is added to the registry of `events.ts` with that schema and to `kinds.ts` (it changes no conversation map), and the test that every kind has a row there passes. The system-line handler writes nothing for this type. The event holds no message text, no name and no snippet, so the log (kept 7 days) never holds the person's saved text. Every effective change of R3, R4, R5 and R8 calls `recordChange` once; a request that changes nothing calls it zero times. Check: for each route a recording handler receives the change with the fields above; the real event handler writes one `later.changed` event of scope `user` and no other person's connection receives it (a second person's connection hears nothing); the registry and `kinds.ts` tests pass; a search of the event payload finds no text.
- R14. Boundaries and wiring. `modules/later` imports the `conversations` and `messages` modules only through their `index.ts` (`getConversationFacts`, `listReadableConversationIds`, `buildMessageEntriesByIds`), `modules/jobs` through its `index.ts`, and the types of `@toki/shared`; it imports no `realtime`, no `members` and no module of Pause, Focus or notifications. `app.ts` registers `laterRoutes({ db, clock, jobs, recordChange, getLastEventNumber, listFiles? })` and passes the one `recordChange` (TOKI-037 R18). The routes are registered under `/api/v1/later`. The `Origin` and media-type rules of TOKI-022 cover the writes. Check: `pnpm lint` exits 0 with the boundary rules; a test builds the app and finds the four routes; a foreign `Origin` on a write is 403 and a body that is not JSON is 415.
Client
- R15. The API calls. `createApiClient` (TOKI-032 R9, the same rules: one request core, 10 seconds, no retry, a path part encoded, an optional `signal` last) gains `api.later.list(signal?)`, `api.later.save(body, signal?)`, `api.later.update(messageId, body, signal?)` and `api.later.remove(messageId, signal?)`, each reading its answer with the schema of R2. Check: each call sends the method, path and body of R3 to R6 with a fake `fetch`, parses the answer with the schema, and fails with the client's `ApiError` on a body that does not parse; a path part that holds a slash is encoded.
- R16. The store. `packages/client-core/src/later/` exports `createLaterStore({ api, conversations })` (the store that owns the person's list; the app makes one in `client.ts`) with `getState()` `{ status, items, number, error }` (`status` `idle`, `loading`, `ready`, `failed`), `subscribe`, `load()`, `getBadge()` (the number of items with `due` true and `completedAt` null), `isSaved(messageId)`, `counts()` `{ inProgress, completed }`, `rows(tab)`, and the commands `save(body)`, `update(messageId, body)`, `remove(messageId)`, each returning a promise that resolves to `{ ok: true }` or `{ ok: false, error }` and never rejects. Rules: (a) `load()` is made when the conversation store becomes `ready` with a user (as the engine of TOKI-040 R19 starts) and again when the connection becomes `online` after being down and after `sync.reload()`; one load is in flight at a time and a second asked while one runs is queued once. (b) The store keeps the `number` of the answer that filled it and, for each message, the number of the last change applied to it; a change from an event or from the answer of the person's own request is applied only when its number is above the list's number and above that message's number; a change with `number` null (nothing changed) changes nothing; a removal keeps its number until the list is replaced, so an older answer cannot bring the row back. (c) An event `later.changed` is applied through `registerEventHandler("later.changed", …)` of TOKI-040 R18: with `item` null the row is removed; with an item for a row the store holds, the state fields replace the held ones and the held `source` stays; with an item for a row it does not hold (another device saved it) the store calls `load()`; the same for a change whose `source` it cannot know. (d) A reset of the conversation store (sign-out, another person) empties the list. (e) `counts()` and `rows(tab)` are computed from the held rows only, so a count is the length of the rows shown (D-074); `rows("progress")` puts the rows with `due` first, then the rest, each group in the order of the list (newest saved first); `rows("done")` keeps the list's order. (f) The reference of `items`, `rows(tab)` and `getBadge()` is kept when a change changes nothing. (g) Commands call `useOnlineGate` through the web wrapper, not the store; the store sends the request and applies the answer by (b). Check: unit tests with a fake `api` and a fake `registerEventHandler`: the load fills the list; the event of save 2 (number 12) before the answer of save 1 (number 11) leaves save 2; the answer first and then an older event leaves the answer; an event for an unknown row calls `load()` once; an event with item null removes the row and a late answer of the earlier save does not bring it back; a held `source` survives a state patch; `getBadge()` counts due rows that are not completed; the counts equal the length of `rows` for every tab in a table of fixtures that includes `deleted` and `unavailable` rows; two `load()` calls while one runs send two requests, not three; the reset empties the list; the reference is stable for a no-op.
Web
- R17. The page. `apps/web/src/routes/later.tsx` adds the route `/later` as a child of the signed-in layout (R30 of the app shell spec, TOKI-015: `appLayoutRoute` is the parent), and `apps/web/src/shell/sections.ts` gains the row `later` (icon `bookmark`, label `nav.later`, `tab: true`, the badge of R20), which TOKI-092's More sheet and the shell's bottom bar (Home, Activity, Later, Files, F38) read. The page is the shell's `SectionPage` (`.sechead` with the title `later.title` and `.secbody`) with a `.toolbar` that holds two chips, `Chip` of TOKI-012, labelled `t("later.chip.progress", { count })` ("In progress · {count}") and `t("later.chip.done", { count })` ("Completed · {count}"), `aria-pressed` for the chosen one, In progress first and chosen on first open (the prototype); the counts are `counts()` of R16. The chosen chip is state of the page, not of the address. While the store is `loading` and holds no row the page shows the loading placeholder of TOKI-082 once it exists (until then a plain text), and `failed` shows the page's error with Retry (D-126). Check: a render test with a fake store: the route is registered under the layout; the section row exists with `tab: true`; the chips show the counts and `aria-pressed`; choosing the other chip shows its rows; the first open shows In progress; the count of each chip equals the number of rows drawn for it for fixtures of 0, 1 and 7 rows.

## Design
### Data model
`later_items` (R1): one row for each person and saved message. The reminder has two states in the row and nothing else: pending (`remind_at` set, `due` false) and due (`remind_at` null, `due` true, `fired_at` set). A completed row has no pending reminder (concern 1, proposal). The saved text is never copied into the row: the list reads it from the message each time, so an edit, a deletion and a loss of access show at once and the log never holds it.

### API
All routes need a session. Bodies are JSON. The writes carry the `Origin` rule.
| Method | Path | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| GET | `/api/v1/later` | The person's items with their sources and the event number (R9) |
| POST | `/api/v1/later` | Save a message, optionally with a reminder; 201 new, 200 existing (R3) |
| PATCH | `/api/v1/later/{messageId}` | Complete, reopen, set or clear a reminder, acknowledge a due row (R4) |
| DELETE | `/api/v1/later/{messageId}` | Remove from Later (R5) |

### Event
`later.changed`, scope `user`: `{ messageId, reason, item, held }`. Every connection of the person receives it, so the list and the badge follow on every device (D-142's rule for personal data, as `settings.changed`).

### The life of a reminder
```
POST/PATCH with remindAt --> row.remind_at set --> cancelByKey + enqueue(startAfter = remindAt)   (one transaction)
                                   ...  worker poll (<= 2 s late)
later.fire-reminder: isHeld? --> tx: row (remind_at = job time, not completed) --> due = true, fired_at = now
                                    --> recordChange(later_changed, reminder_due, held) --> commit
                                    --> held ? nothing : reminderNotifier.notify(...)   (push: TOKI-074 through TOKI-073)
clients: event --> store --> badge, due row on top --> LaterHost: toast unless held or old
Pause/Focus end: TOKI-076 reads listRemindersFiredBetween(from, to) for its one summary
```

### Errors
- No session -> 401 `unauthenticated`; a deactivated account -> 401 `account_deactivated`.
- Unknown conversation, a private conversation the person is not in, a message that is not in that conversation, a system line, a deleted message, another person's item -> 404 `not_found`, one body for all (R3, R4).
- A public channel the person has not joined -> 403 `forbidden` (R3).
- A reminder that is not later than the clock, or not an instant -> 400 `validation_failed` with `details.field` `remindAt` (R6).
- A body with an extra key, an empty PATCH, `acknowledged: false`, or `completed: true` with a reminder -> 400 `validation_failed` (R2, R4).
- The job queue unreachable when a reminder is set -> the transaction fails and the route answers `internal_error`; nothing is saved (R7).

## Data safety
- A saved message belongs to one person: the list, the badge, the event and the summary read only that person's rows (R3 to R5, R9, R12, R13).
- No job payload, no event, no log line and no error message holds a message's text or a name: the payload holds an identifier and a time (R7), the event holds state and identifiers (R13), the notifier input holds identifiers and a time (R11), the list reads the text from the message at each request (R9) and shows nothing of a message the person can no longer read (`unavailable`, R9).
- A person who loses access to a conversation keeps the row and sees no content of it (R9, concern 2). Nothing is written to browser storage by this ticket.

## Open questions from the intent
- What happens to a pending reminder when the item is completed, and to a completed item that is given a reminder? Not answered. Carried forward as concern 1.
- What does a Later row show when the person can no longer read the conversation of the message? Not answered. Carried forward as concern 2.
- Who fires a reminder that fell due during Pause or Focus time, and when? Answered in this spec: nobody fires it afterwards; it is due at its time, only the toast, push and sound are left out, and the summary of TOKI-076 lists it (R11, R12, concern 3).
- Is there a limit on the number of saved messages, or a furthest reminder time? Not answered. Carried forward as concern 4.

- Who owns the time choices that Later and Activity share? Answered in this spec: the rule is TOKI-094's, the labels, hook and menu are TOKI-066's (R23, concern 6).

## Areas of concern
1. Completed items and reminders. Not decided. The prototype lets a completed row keep a reminder that never fires while it is completed, and fires at the next tick when the row is reopened (its `!l.done` test); F28 and the log say nothing about it. The spec needs one behavior so that a reminder cannot fire for a finished item or sit unseen. Proposal, not a decision (R1, R4): completing clears the pending reminder and the due mark; a reminder set on a completed row moves it back to In progress (the prototype does this for "Remind me about this", `remindmsgset`, and not for the row's own button); a completed row shows no reminder line.
