# Activity screen design

Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: draft for review (concerns 1 to 5 are open for the product owner)
Intent: docs/intents/activity-screen.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-066

## Goal
The Activity server (TOKI-065) keeps, for each person, the things that need their attention, and tells every open screen when they change. A person cannot see any of it: the navigation has an Activity entry with no page and no number. This ticket builds the screen: the page with its filter chips and rows, the press of an item, "Mark all read", Done with Undo, Snooze and Unsnooze, the number on the navigation, the client store that keeps all of it right while other people act and while the connection comes and goes, and the labels, the hook and the menu body of the time choices that Activity and Later share.
The rules that must hold on the screen are D-074 (a count on a chip equals the rows shown), D-069 (Done happens at once and offers Undo for 5 seconds), D-067 and D-053 (Done and Snooze work on a phone with no hover), D-114 (a past time choice is not offered; "In 3 hours" takes its place) and D-079 (writes are blocked with an explanation while disconnected). The server decides what an item is, who has it and when a snoozed item wakes; the screen decides how it reads, looks and responds.

## Scope
- In:
  - The route `/activity`, the section row, the page frame and the chip row (R1, R2, R7, R8).
  - The client store `createActivityStore` of `packages/client-core`, its API methods, its handling of events and notices, and its wiring (R3 to R6).
  - The row: its parts, its sentence for each of the six kinds, its time, and its press (R9 to R12).
  - The actions: Done with Undo, Snooze, Unsnooze, Mark all read (R13 to R16).
  - Done and Snooze visible without hover on narrow and touch screens (R17), the empty state (R18), the number on the navigation (R19), the loading, failed and offline states (R20), more rows on scroll (R21).
  - The time-choice labels, hook and menu body (R22), the texts (R23), the boundaries and what does not change (R24, R25), the tests (R26).
- Out (not now):
  - Everything on the server: the items, the routes, the events, the jobs (TOKI-065). What this screen needs from it that it lacks is in Plan changes.
  - The toast, push and sound for a new item and the toast "Back from snooze": TOKI-073 decides, TOKI-074 and TOKI-075 deliver (TOKI-065, "What the later tickets get"). This screen shows the label "Back from snooze" on the row and draws no toast for it.
  - Later (saved messages and reminders): TOKI-067. It uses the labels, the hook and the menu body of R22 and builds its own page, store and badge.
  - How a message is opened, flashed and placed: TOKI-068 (`useOpenMessage`) and the pieces it names (TOKI-041, TOKI-045). This screen calls them (R12).
  - The unread counts of conversations, the read pointer and the tab title: TOKI-053 and TOKI-054. This screen gives the shell the Activity number only (R19).
  - "Catch up on everything" (a later phase, D-053 note), the kinds that belong to later phases (an approval request), the More sheet (TOKI-092), loading placeholders and the shared "no access" page (TOKI-082).
  - The notification preferences: TOKI-102. Nothing on this screen depends on a preference (D-174).

## Requirements
- R1. The route and the section. `apps/web/src/routes/activity.tsx` exports `activityRoute`, a child of `appLayoutRoute` with the path `/activity`, whose component is `ActivityPage`, and `router.tsx` adds it to the children of `appLayoutRoute`. `apps/web/src/layout/sections.ts` gains the row `{ id: "activity", icon: "bell", labelKey: "nav.activity", to: "/activity", match: p => p === "/activity" || p.startsWith("/activity/"), tab: true }`, placed after `home` and before the rows that exist after it, in the order of the prototype's list (TOKI-015 R30); no `roles`, so every role sees it (the server compares no role, TOKI-065 R24). The shell therefore draws the item in the sidebar and the Focus rail and the tab in the bottom bar of a narrow screen (F38). Check: a test of `sections` finds the row with those values and the order of the ids of `visibleSections("admin")` and of `visibleSections("member")` with `activity` second; a route test finds `/activity` under the layout and the page inside the shell; the item has `aria-current="page"` on `/activity`; the bottom bar test (TOKI-015 R6) finds the Activity tab.
- R2. The page frame. `ActivityPage` (`apps/web/src/features/activity/ActivityPage.tsx`) is a `SectionPage` of TOKI-015 R12 with the title `t("nav.activity")` and, as its `actions`, one `Button` (TOKI-012) with the check icon and the text `t("activity.markAllRead")`. The chip row of R7 sits between the header and the list as the prototype's `.toolbar` does (lines 1552 to 1553 of `index.html`): `SectionPage` gains the optional prop `toolbar`, drawn as `div.toolbar` between `div.sechead` and `div.secbody` when given and not at all otherwise (one edit of `packages/ui-web/src/layout/SectionPage.tsx`, concern 11); with no `toolbar` the markup is what TOKI-015 R12 says. The rows are the children, so only the rows scroll. Check: a render test of the page finds the heading, the button and the chip row in that order, with the chip row outside the scrolling body; a test of `SectionPage` with and without `toolbar` finds the markup of TOKI-015 R12 for the second.
- R3. The store. `createActivityStore(options)` of `packages/client-core/src/activity/`, exported from the package index with its types. `options`: `api` (the methods of R4), `sync` (the engine of TOKI-040: `registerEventHandler`, `registerLiveHandler`), `conversations` (the store of TOKI-032, read for its `user` and its `status`), `clock` (`Clock` of `@toki/shared`) and `timers`. It uses no DOM or browser global, no `Date.now(` and no `new Date()` (TOKI-008 R19), no timer of its own and no key of `@toki/i18n`. It holds this state, replaced and never changed in place, read with `getState()` and `subscribe(listener)` (the shape of the other stores of the package): `status` (`idle`, `loading`, `ready`, `failed`: the state of the list), `filter` (one of `ACTIVITY_FILTERS` of TOKI-065 R2, `all` at the start), `items` (the `ActivityItem` objects of the chosen filter that are held, in the order of R8), `nextCursor` (string or null), `isLoadingMore`, `error` (the `ApiError` of the last failed load, or null) and `summary` (`{ unread, counts }` or null before the first answer). It has `setFilter(filter)`, `load()`, `loadMore()`, `refresh()`, the actions of R13 to R16 (`markRead`, `markMessageRead`, `markAllRead`, `markDone`, `reopen`, `snooze`, `unsnooze`), `attachConnection(connection)` (R6) and `reset()`. The store follows the conversation store as the engine of TOKI-040 R19 does: when that store holds a `user` and is `ready` and this store is not started for that person, it starts (reads the summary of R4; the list is read when `load()` is called by the page); when the user changes or the state returns to `idle` it resets (state back to the start, requests in flight aborted and their answers ignored by a generation number, nothing kept). No caller starts or resets it by hand. Check: with fakes, the store becoming `ready` with a user makes one summary request and no list request; `load()` makes one list request for the chosen filter; a store `reset()` and a second person's start leave no item, no summary and no pending answer; a late answer after a reset changes nothing; a package test finds no DOM reference and no use of the clock functions named above.
- R4. The API methods. `createApiClient` gains `api.activity` with `list(query?, signal?)`, `summary(signal?)`, `read(body, signal?)`, `done(id, signal?)`, `reopen(id, signal?)`, `snooze(id, until, signal?)` and `unsnooze(id, signal?)`, on the seven routes of TOKI-065 (`GET /activity` with `filter`, `limit` and `cursor` only when given; `GET /activity/summary`; `POST /activity/read` with exactly one of `{ itemIds }`, `{ messageId }`, `{ all: true }`; `POST /activity/{id}/done`, `/reopen`, `/snooze` with `{ until }`, `/unsnooze`), with the rules of TOKI-032 R9 (one request core, 10 seconds, no retry, the path part encoded, an optional `signal` last). Each answer is read with the schema of `@toki/shared` that TOKI-065 R2 names (`ActivityListResponseSchema`, `ActivitySummarySchema`, and `ActivityItemState` inside the answers of the other five), in the form that drops unknown keys (TOKI-032 R3). The screen asks for the server's default page size (`ACTIVITY_PAGE_DEFAULT`). Check: with the fake `fetch` of the existing tests, each method sends the method, path, query and body as written and parses a good answer; the list request has no `limit` or `cursor` unless given; a body with two keys is not built by `read` (the type allows one); an answer with an added key keeps working; an error answer ends as an `ApiError` of the existing kinds.
- R5. Events and notices. The store registers, in `createActivityStore` and through the seams of TOKI-040 R18, `registerEventHandler("activity.upserted", …)`, `registerEventHandler("activity.removed", …)` and `registerLiveHandler("activity.changed", …)` (TOKI-065 R14, R23). Rules, applied in this order for each: (a) An item or a state is applied only when its `version` is higher than the held version of that id; an equal or lower one changes nothing, so an event and a notice arriving in either order give one result (TOKI-065 R23). (b) `activity.upserted` sets the item in the store's map of held items and puts it in or takes it out of the chosen view by the rule of R8; an item of the chosen view that is not held and whose position is after the last held item while `nextCursor` is not null is not inserted (it comes with a later page). (c) `activity.removed` drops each id from the map and from the view and remembers it as removed with the event's number. (d) `activity.changed` applies each state to the held item: `done` true removes it from the view and the map; `unread`, `snoozedUntil` and `backFromSnooze` are set; the view is recomputed by R8. A state for an item that is not held, and not remembered as removed or done with an equal or higher version, makes the store call `refresh()` (the notice says "read the list again", TOKI-065 R23). (e) After any of the three, the store reads the summary again (R6, one request at a time). (f) A list or summary answer carries `asOf`: an item of an answer is dropped when its id is remembered as removed with a number above that `asOf`, or as done with a version equal to or above its own, so an answer read before an event does not bring back what the event removed; a held item whose version is higher than the answer's keeps its held copy. The store keeps at most 500 such memories, oldest first out, and drops them at `reset()`. (g) An item whose `snoozedUntil` is not later than `clock.now()` is a returned item: the store reads it with `snoozedUntil` null, `backFromSnooze` true and, as its sort time, the later of `occurredAt` and the old `snoozedUntil` (TOKI-065 R16, R21: the list treats such an item as returned before the wake job has run). Check: a table test with the reducer, for each of (a) to (g): an upsert with a lower version is ignored; an upsert for an unseen item at the top of the chosen view appears first; an upsert that arrives for an item older than the last held row with a cursor open is not inserted; a removal and then an older list answer leaves the item out; a state with `done` true removes the row; a state for an unknown item calls `refresh` once; the same notice applied twice leaves one result; every kind of change calls the summary read once however many events come together (R6); an item with `snoozedUntil` one second in the past reads as returned and sorts by that time.
- R6. The summary and keeping it right. The summary is `{ unread, counts }` of `ActivitySummarySchema` and is what the navigation badge and the "Snoozed · n" chip read. It is read from the server: at the start (R3), after every event or notice of R5, when the connection's `status` becomes `online` after a time it was not (the first `online` after the page loaded is skipped, as TOKI-053 R17 does), and after a failed read at the next of these moments. At most one read is in flight and one queued, however many things ask. An answer replaces the held summary when its `asOf` is not lower than the held one; a lower one is ignored. At the same moments, when the list has been loaded, the store also calls `refresh()` after an `online` that follows a loss, because events of the lost time are replayed by TOKI-039 but a replay that was refused as expired ends in a reload of the engine, which gives no event; `refresh()` reads the first page of the chosen filter again and keeps the loaded rows until the answer comes. The store has its own `attachConnection(connection)` taking the object of TOKI-039 R15 (`getState()` with a `status`, `subscribe(listener)`) and returning a remover; the engine's `sync.attachConnection` offers no state getter, which is why TOKI-053 R17 has its own. Local changes move the number at once and the answer replaces it: when a held item changes from counted to not counted (read, done, snoozed) or back, `summary.unread` and the counts it affects (`all`, the filter of its kind, `snoozed`) change by that item at the moment of the action (R13 to R16), so the badge falls when the person presses Done and not one round trip later. Check, with a fake connection: going `offline` then `online` makes one summary read and one list `refresh`, and the first `online` after the start makes none; ten events together make one read and one queued; an answer with a lower `asOf` is ignored; pressing Done on a held unread item lowers `unread` and `counts.all` at once and the answer then replaces them; a failed read changes nothing and the next moment repeats it.
- R7. The chips. The chip row is a `ChipGroup` of TOKI-012 with the accessible name `t("activity.filters")`, holding one `Chip` for each of `all`, `mentions`, `threads`, `reactions` and `other` in this order, with the texts `activity.chip.*` (R23), and, after them, `snoozed` with the text `t("activity.chip.snoozed", { count })` only while `summary.counts.snoozed` is above 0. The chosen chip has `aria-pressed="true"`, the others `"false"`; `all` is chosen when the page opens; the chosen filter is state of the page and not of the address (the prototype, `S.actFilter`). A press calls `setFilter`. When the chosen filter is `snoozed` and the count falls to 0, the filter becomes `all` and the chip is gone (the prototype's line 1550; AC-113). The five chips are drawn whatever the counts, so a kind with no item is still reachable (D-071, AC-110). The three kinds of `added_to_channel`, `role_changed` and `message_deleted` are under the chip `other` (`activityFilterOfKind` of TOKI-065 R2; F27, AC-110); its name is concern 1. The chips other than `snoozed` show no number (concern 2). Check: a render test finds the six chips in that order with and without a snoozed item (five without), `aria-pressed` on the chosen one only, the press calling `setFilter` with the id, and the fall back to `all` when the last snoozed item is unsnoozed; the chip texts come from the catalogue in both languages.
- R8. The view and its order. The store keeps one view, that of the chosen filter. `setFilter(filter)` makes the view empty and `loading` for the new filter and reads its first page with `api.activity.list({ filter })`; an answer for a filter that is no longer the chosen one is ignored. A held item belongs to the view of a filter when: for `all`, `mentions`, `threads`, `reactions` and `other`, it is not done, it is not snoozed (`snoozedUntil` null after R5 (g)) and `activityFilterOfKind(kind)` is the filter (or the filter is `all`); for `snoozed`, it is not done and `snoozedUntil` is later than `clock.now()`. The order is the one of the server (TOKI-065 R16): for the main views the sort time (`occurredAt`, or the later of `occurredAt` and the old `snoozedUntil` for a returned item) newest first and then the id in code-point order, descending; for `snoozed`, `snoozedUntil` earliest first and then the id. A page is added after the held rows (`loadMore`, R21) and the answer replaces the first page on `refresh()`. Check: with a fixture of every kind and state, each view holds exactly the items the rule says, in the order said, for the six filters (the fixture has two items of one time, ids in both orders); a mention at 10:00 and a reaction at 10:01 give the reaction first (TS-170); the chip `other` holds the three kinds and no other (TS-174); a snoozed item is in `snoozed` and not in `all`; a returned item is the first row of `all`; the count of each filter that the summary gives equals the length of the view read to its end (D-074).
- R9. The row. A row is a `div.activity-item` with `role="button"` and `tabindex="0"` (the prototype's `.item`; the class is `activity-item` so that it does not meet the Later row), with the class `un` while the item is unread. In order: the unread dot with the accessible name `t("activity.row.unread")` or `t("activity.row.read")` (the dot is drawn transparent when read, the prototype's `.udot.off`); the `Avatar` of TOKI-013 of the actor (the name and `personHue` as the rest of the app); a block holding the sentence (R10) and under it the meta line `{conversation} · {time}` (R11), followed, when the item is snoozed, by the clock icon and `t("activity.row.back", { when })` with `when` the `snoozedUntil` through `formatDateTime` of TOKI-094 R5 (the prototype's "back" text, `fmtAt`; Table A row 3 of TOKI-094), and, when `backFromSnooze` is true, by `t("activity.row.backFromSnooze")`; and, at the end, the buttons of R13 to R15. `{conversation}` is the name of the conversation as the prototype's `cname` draws it: `#` and the name for a channel, the other person's or people's names for a direct message or group (`getConversationName` of the store), and nothing for an item with no `conversationId` (`role_changed`). The actor's name is the `displayName` of `getPerson(actorId)`; a person the store does not hold gives the sentence with no name part and the avatar with no initials, and never throws. An unread row's name is drawn in the ink colour (the prototype's `.item.un b`). Enter and Space on the focused row do what a press does (R12); a press of a button of the row does not. Check: a render test of each part and of its order for an unread, a read, a snoozed and a returned item; an item with an unknown actor renders; a direct-message conversation shows the other person's name; `role_changed` shows no conversation; Enter and Space on the row call the press once and a click on Done calls only Done.
- R10. The sentence of each kind. The sentence is one translated text per kind and shape, with the actor's name in bold before it (the prototype's `<b>name</b> text`), built only from the item's data and the conversation store, never from text the server sends (TOKI-065 sends no wording): `mention` with `viaChannel` false: `activity.sentence.mention` with `{ preview }`; `mention` with `viaChannel` true: `activity.sentence.mentionChannel` with `{ preview }` (F13); `thread`: `activity.sentence.thread` with `{ preview }`; `reaction`: `activity.sentence.reaction` with `{ emojis, conversation, count }` where `emojis` is the item's `reactions.emojis` joined with a space and `count` is `reactions.actorCount` minus 1 (the key has a `one` and an `other` form for the other people, and the form for 0 is the plain one); `added_to_channel`: `activity.sentence.added` with `{ conversation }`; `role_changed`: `activity.sentence.role` with `{ role }`, the word `common.role.member` or `common.role.admin` from the item's `role`; `message_deleted`: `activity.sentence.deleted` with `{ conversation }`. A `preview` that is `null` (the message could not be read, TOKI-065 R15) uses the key with the suffix `NoText` (`mentionNoText`, `mentionChannelNoText`, `threadNoText`) that has no quoted text. The reaction lead is the name of the actor; when `actorCount` is above 1 the sentence says how many other people reacted. A long preview is cut by the layout to two lines (`-webkit-line-clamp`), not by the code. The wordings are in R23; the three that the prototype draws (mention, thread, reaction with one person) are its own words, and the others are proposals (concern 4). Check: a table test renders each kind and shape in `en` and `vi` and reads the text; a reaction with `actorCount` 1, 2 and 4 uses the plain, the one and the other form; a null preview gives the `NoText` text; no text comes from the item's own strings except the preview.
- R11. The time of a row. The time in the meta line is `formatTime(occurredAt, timeZone)` of TOKI-094 R1 (Table A row 1 gives "Activity item time" the 24-hour form) when `occurredAt` falls on today in the zone of the date context, and `formatDateTime` otherwise (concern 5, a proposal for the item of another day). The date context is `useDateContext()` of the app, whose `now` is the one clock of `apps/web/src/clock.ts` and whose zone is the one the app gives (TOKI-094 concern 2; concern 10 here). The `<time>` element carries the ISO value in `dateTime`. No other code in the feature formats a date or a time (TOKI-094 concern 6). Check, with the clock of TOKI-008 and a fixed zone: an item of 09:22 today reads `09:22`; an item of yesterday reads the day word and the time; an item of last year reads the year as `formatDate` does; a time in another zone follows the context; a search of the feature finds no `toLocale*` and no `Intl.DateTimeFormat`.
- R12. The press of an item. A press (or Enter or Space) does two things, in this order: (a) for an unread item in the main list it calls `markRead({ itemIds: [id] })` (R13 gives the store method; the item is read at once on the screen, AC-109, TS-171), and (b) it opens what the item points at: for `mention`, `thread`, `reaction` and `message_deleted` it calls `open({ conversationId, messageId, parentId })` of `useOpenMessage()` (TOKI-068 R1), which opens the conversation at the message or, when `parentId` is not null, the thread panel at that reply (F13, TOKI-068 R6); for `added_to_channel` it navigates to `/c/{conversationId}` with one push; for `role_changed` it opens nothing. The press of a row in the "Snoozed" view opens the same and marks nothing read (the server skips a snoozed item, TOKI-065 R18). The press is the whole of what this ticket sends when a person opens a message; what else reads an item by itself is concern 3. The screen never calls `openThread` itself: a thread item points at the reply (TOKI-065, "What the later tickets get"). Check: with a fake hook and a fake store: a mention press calls `markRead` with the id and `open` with the three values once; a reply item gives `parentId`; a read item sends no `markRead`; `added_to_channel` navigates and opens no message; `role_changed` calls neither `open` nor navigate but still marks read; a press in the snoozed view sends no `markRead`; the order is read first, open second; no import of `openThread` in the feature.
- R13. Done with Undo. Each row of the main list has an `IconButton` with the check icon and the name `t("activity.row.done")` ("Mark done", the prototype's `aria-label`). A press calls `markDone(id)` through `useUndo().run` of TOKI-093 with the action `markActivityDone` (`actionPatterns`: pattern `undo`, owner TOKI-066, already in the table), the key `markActivityDone:{id}`, the text `t("activity.toast.done")` and: `perform`, which removes the item from the view and the map at once (and moves the summary by R6) and then calls `api.activity.done(id)`, restoring the item and rethrowing when the request fails so that `useUndo` shows `common.error.actionFailed`; `undo`, which calls `api.activity.reopen(id)` and puts the held copy back in the view by the rule of R8 (its state as it was, read or unread; the server leaves `read_at` alone, TOKI-065 R19). No confirmation is shown (D-069, AC-112). The Toast and its 5 seconds belong to TOKI-013 and `useUndo` adds no timer; the store keeps the removed item for the Undo until the toast is gone, the person leaves the page, or `reset()`; a `reopen` that answers 404 (the server removed the item for good, TOKI-065 R19, or deleted it with its message) shows `common.error.undoFailed` and the row stays out. While offline the press is blocked with the explanation of D-079 (concern 7). Check: Done removes the row and lowers the badge before the fake request answers, shows the toast with the text and an Undo action, and no dialog; Undo calls `reopen` and the row is back at the place its sort time gives, read or unread as before; a failing `done` restores the row and shows the error toast; a 404 `reopen` shows the undo error and leaves the row out; two Done presses on two rows keep two toasts; no `window.confirm` or dialog is rendered (TS-177); the toast is gone after the 5 seconds of the fake clock and a reload lists no done item (TS-178; the server's part is TOKI-065 R19).
- R14. Snooze. Each row of the main list has an `IconButton` with the clock icon and the name `t("activity.row.snooze")` ("Snooze", the prototype's `aria-label`) that opens a `Menu` of TOKI-014 anchored to it, titled `t("activity.snooze.title")` ("Snooze until", the prototype's `.mt`), whose body is `QuickTimeMenu` of R22. A choice calls `snooze(id, at)`: the store removes the item from the main view, adds it to the snoozed count and sets `snoozedUntil` at once (R6), then calls `api.activity.snooze(id, new Date(at).toISOString())` and, on success, applies the returned state by R5; on failure it restores the item and shows `common.error.actionFailed`. A success shows a plain toast `t("activity.toast.snoozed", { when })` ("Snoozed until {when}", the prototype's text) with `when` through `formatDateTime`. There is no Undo: Unsnooze (R15) is the way back (concern 9). The choices are computed when the menu opens, so a menu opened at 14:59 and chosen at 15:01 sends a time that is still later than now; the server refuses a time not later than its now with 400 `validation_failed` (TOKI-065 R20), and the screen then shows `common.error.actionFailed` and restores the row. While offline the press is blocked (concern 7). Check, with the clock of TOKI-008: at 10:00, choosing "In 1 hour" removes the row from All, shows "Snoozed · 1", sends `until` 11:00 as an ISO UTC string, and the toast says when; the same item is listed under the chip Snoozed with the "back" text (TS-179, the server's wake being TOKI-065's); a failing request restores the row; a 400 shows the error and restores the row; the menu is not offered for a snoozed row.
- R15. Unsnooze. A row in the "Snoozed" view has, in place of Done and Snooze, one `Button` with the text `t("activity.row.unsnooze")` ("Unsnooze", the prototype's) that calls `unsnooze(id)`: the store moves the item to the main view as unread (`unread` true, `snoozedUntil` null, `backFromSnooze` false, as the server does, TOKI-065 R20) and calls `api.activity.unsnooze(id)`, applying the state on success and restoring on failure with `common.error.actionFailed`. The press of the button does not open the message. When it was the last snoozed item the chip is gone and the filter becomes `all` (R7). Check: Unsnooze returns the item to All, unread, with no label and the chip count one lower (TS-180); the press opens nothing; a failure restores the row in the snoozed view.
- R16. Mark all read. The button of R2 calls `markAllRead()`: the store sets every held unread item of the main list to read and `summary.unread` to 0 at once, calls `api.activity.read({ all: true })` and applies the returned states by R5; no row leaves the list (AC-109, TS-172). A success shows the plain toast `t("activity.toast.allCaughtUp")` ("All caught up", the prototype's). On failure the items return to unread and `common.error.actionFailed` is shown. The button is drawn also when nothing is unread (the prototype draws it always) and then changes nothing and sends no request. While offline the press is blocked (concern 7). ### The store in one picture
```
server --activity.upserted / activity.removed (numbered events)--> sync.registerEventHandler --\
server --activity.changed (the person's own marks, unnumbered)---> sync.registerLiveHandler  ---+--> reducer (version rule) --> view + summary --> page, badge
page   --press, Done, Snooze, Unsnooze, Mark all read--> optimistic change at once --> api.activity.* --> state answer --> reducer
connection online again / a state for an unknown item --> refresh the first page, read the summary
```
Two ways in, one rule: every change is applied only when its `version` is higher than the held one (R5 (a)), so the numbered event that another person causes, the notice of the person's own mark from another tab, and the answer to the person's own request give the same result in any order. A summary is read again after any of them, one request at a time, so the numbers on the chips and the badge equal the rows (D-074). An action changes the screen first and the server second, as D-069 asks for Done ("at once"), and a failed request restores what it changed.

## Data safety
- The store holds items (ids, a preview of message text, times) in memory only, for the signed-in person, and drops them at `reset()` (R3): nothing is written to storage, the address or a log.
- No text of a message or a preview is written to a log or sent anywhere but the person's own screen; the item reaches the screen only from the person's own routes and events (scope `user`, TOKI-065 R14, R24).
- The press sends an item id or a message id to the server and nothing else; nobody sees who has read or marked an item (D-048).

## Areas of concern
1. The name of the chip for the other kinds. The log requires a chip for every kind (D-071) and F27 and AC-110 put "added to a channel", "role changed" and "message deleted by an admin" under one chip besides All; the prototype has no such chip (its chips are All, Mentions, Threads, Reactions and Snoozed), and D-173 asks what the chip is called and is Open in the log. Not decided: the name. Proposal, not a decision: "Other", the name F27 gives, held in `activity.chip.other` (R7, R23) so that the answer is one text. The grouping of the three kinds under one chip follows F27 and AC-110, as TOKI-065 R2 does. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
2. Numbers on the chips. D-074 says a count on a chip always equals the rows shown, and the plan's line for this ticket speaks of "each count"; the prototype shows a number only on "Snoozed · n", and AC-113 names only that one. Not decided: whether All, Mentions, Threads, Reactions and Other also show a number. Proposal, not a decision: no number on them, as the prototype draws them; the summary already holds every count (TOKI-065 R17), so adding a number is one text change and one prop, and R8 already tests that each count equals its rows. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
3. What reads an item by itself. D-071 says an item "becomes done on its own once the thing it asks for is done"; AC-038 says that opening the mentioned message marks the item "done by itself" and the count falls; F13 says the item "becomes read" once the message is on screen; TS-171 and AC-109 say a pressed row stays listed and loses its dot. TOKI-065 concern 3 and TOKI-053 concern 8 ask the same and are open: whether the trigger is the message on screen, the message opened or the read pointer passing it, and whether the row leaves or only loses its dot. Not decided here either. This ticket builds what the log decides, the press (R12), and the store method `markMessageRead(messageId)` that sends `{ messageId }` (TOKI-065 R18); it wires no other trigger, so an item made while its message is already on screen at the bottom stays unread until it is pressed or the owner answers. Proposal, not a decision, following TOKI-065 concern 3: marking read only and never removing, with the trigger that TOKI-053 concern 8 settles; the wiring is then one call of `markMessageRead` from the place that settles it. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
4. Wording that the prototype does not contain. The prototype draws the sentences of mentions, thread replies and a reaction of one person (the texts marked "prototype" in "Texts"). It draws none for being added to a channel, a changed role or a message deleted by an admin, for a mention of the channel, for a reaction of several people, for the Done toast, for the loading and failure texts, or for the filters label; F22, F40, F42 and F13 give only the sense ("Lan added you to #name", "Lan changed your role to Admin", "mentioned the channel"). By D-139 each such wording is a proposal. Not decided: the final words in English and Vietnamese. Proposal, not a decision: the rows marked "proposal" in "Texts". Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
5. The time of an item from another day. Table A of TOKI-094 gives "Activity item time" the 24-hour time (`15:05`) and the prototype shows `09:22` for today and the word "Yesterday" for an older item, which is not in that table. Not decided: what an item of another day shows. Proposal, not a decision: `formatTime` for today and `formatDateTime` (the day word or date, then the time) for any other day, as R11 builds; the other reading is the time alone, which would hide the day. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
6. The press of an item with no message. Decided in this spec: an `added_to_channel` item opens `/c/{conversationId}` and a `role_changed` item opens nothing, and both are marked read (R12). Reason: F27 says a press "opens the conversation at that message, or opens the thread", and these two kinds have no message; TOKI-065 and TOKI-068 give the same reading; a role item has no place to go, and a channel the person was added to is the one place the item is about. A person who is no longer a member has no such item (TOKI-065 R12). Owner: Thanh Truong.
7. Disconnected. Decided in this spec: loaded rows stay readable, and Done, Snooze, Unsnooze and "Mark all read" each pass through `useOnlineGate` and are blocked with its one explanation (R13 to R16, R20); the press of a row opens the message whatever the connection and sends its mark-read only when the connection is up, with no message, so an item pressed offline stays unread until the next press or the owner's trigger (concern 3). Reason: D-079 blocks writes with an explanation, and opening a message needs no server (TOKI-068 R12); a mark-read blocked with a toast on every press would make reading offline unusable, and it is repaired by reading again. Owner: Thanh Truong.
8. The store, optimistic changes and the version rule. Decided in this spec: the store is in `packages/client-core` with its own `attachConnection`, applies numbered events and unnumbered notices by `version`, changes the screen before the server answers and restores on failure, and reads the summary again after every change, one request at a time (R3, R5, R6). Reason: it is the pattern of TOKI-053 (a tracker with its own connection and a state order), D-069 says Done happens at once, and TOKI-065 R23 says a client applies a state only when its version is higher and reads again for an item it does not hold. Cost: one more request after each change; the alternative, counting locally and trusting it, would let the badge drift from the rows (D-074). Owner: Thanh Truong.
9. Snoozed rows and no Undo for Snooze. Decided in this spec: a row in the Snoozed view has Unsnooze and no Done or Snooze (R15), and Snooze shows a plain toast with the time and no Undo (R14). Reason: the prototype draws exactly this (lines 1560 and 2418), AC-113 and TS-180 say Unsnooze is the way back, and Table A of TOKI-093 lists no Snooze row because it is neither destructive nor irreversible. Done on a snoozed item is possible on the server (TOKI-065 R19) and not offered here. Owner: Thanh Truong.
10. The owner of the time choices and the zone. Decided in this spec: the labels, `useQuickTimeChoices` and `QuickTimeMenu` are this ticket's and Later reuses them (R22), as TOKI-094 Scope and TOKI-067 concern 6 say. Which time zone "Today 15:00" and "Tomorrow 09:00" are read in is answered by concern 2 of the date-time spec (the zone set in the profile) and this ticket reads whatever `useDateContext()` gives; the copy of that hook that the code has today (`apps/web/src/account/use-date-context.ts`) reads the device's zone until the profile ticket gives the person a zone, so the two differ in the built code and one hook is the place to change. Owner: Thanh Truong.
11. The frame and the look. Decided in this spec: `SectionPage` gains an optional `toolbar` so the chips stay in place while the rows scroll, as the prototype's `.toolbar` does; the buttons use the tap-mode query of TOKI-052 R6 so that the shell, the message bar and this screen agree on what a touch screen is; the row class is `activity-item` so it does not meet the Later row's styles. Reason: the prototype draws the chips outside the scrolling body, its buttons are hover-only (a breach of D-067 that AC-115 and D-053 correct), and two features must not share a bare `.item`. Owner: Thanh Truong.
12. Page size and more rows. Decided in this spec: the screen asks for the server's default page (50, TOKI-065 R16) and loads the next page when the end is within 48 px or focus reaches the last row (R21). Reason: the log gives no number; the server's default is a bound and not a product rule (TOKI-065 concern 11), and 48 px is the threshold the other lists of the app use. Owner: Thanh Truong.
