# Activity (server) design

Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: draft for review (concerns 1 to 6, 9, 11 and 13 are open for the product owner)
Intent: docs/intents/activity-server.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-065

## Goal
A person cannot watch every conversation they belong to, so the server must keep, for each person, the things that need their attention: a message that names them, a reply in a thread they follow, a reaction to their message, an addition to a channel, a change of their role, and a message of theirs deleted by an admin. The facts exist already in the messages, mentions and reactions of the earlier tickets, and in the changes that `recordChange` carries; nothing turns them into a list.
This ticket builds that list on the server: the module `activity` with its tables, the rule of who follows a thread, the handler that creates, updates and removes items inside the transaction of the change that causes them, the routes to read the list and to mark, finish and snooze items, the numbered events that tell every open screen, the notice for the person's own actions, and the job that brings a snoozed item back on time.
The hard rules are the ones of D-071 and the journeys F13, F14, F15, F27, F40 and F42: nobody is notified of their own action, an item goes with its source, leaving a channel clears the person's items of it, a muted conversation still creates items, and no one can stop following a thread. Because an item is written in the transaction of its cause, it can never exist without it, and an event for it can never be sent for a rolled-back change.
The screen (TOKI-066) and the notification decision engine (TOKI-073) are built on this; "What the later tickets get" says what this ticket gives each.

## Scope
- In:
  - The tables `activity_items`, `activity_reactors` and `thread_followers`, their checks, keys, indexes and one migration (R1).
  - The shared shapes and constants in `packages/shared/src/activity/` (R2).
  - Who gets an item and when it is created, updated or removed, for each of the six kinds (R3 to R12).
  - The handler of changes, its place in the list of `recordChange`, and the shape of the change for the events (R13, R14).
  - The routes: the list, the summary, mark read, Done and its Undo, Snooze and Unsnooze (R15 to R20), and their errors and access rule (R24).
  - The job that wakes a snoozed item, the job that removes finished items, and the notice to the person's other devices (R21 to R23).
  - The wiring in `apps/server` (R26).
- Out (not now):
  - The Activity screen: chips and their names, the rows, the sentences, the empty state, the Done toast with Undo, the Snooze menu, "Mark all read", the badge in the navigation, and the client store and API methods. TOKI-066.
  - The toast, push and sound, Pause, Focus, the level of a conversation, the mute rule and the switch for mentions in muted conversations; also the toast "Back from snooze". TOKI-073 (decision) and TOKI-074 / TOKI-075 (delivery). This ticket creates items whatever those say (D-174, D-088).
  - Later (saved messages and reminders): TOKI-067. It has its own tables and routes and shares nothing with this ticket except the quick times of `@toki/shared`.
  - The preferences that the decision engine reads: TOKI-102.
  - The mention table and its writer (TOKI-050), the reaction table (TOKI-046), the thread summary (TOKI-045), the delete and edit routes (TOKI-047), the read pointer (TOKI-053): used as they are.
  - Text of any sentence. The server sends kinds, ids and data; the words are the screen's (NFR-05).
  - "Catch up on everything" (a later phase, D-053 note) and the items of kinds that belong to later phases (an approval request).

## Requirements
- R1. Three tables, in `apps/server/src/modules/activity/schema.ts`, one migration made with `pnpm db:generate --name activity_tables` (TOKI-019 conventions: UUIDv7 default of the id column, every key `ON DELETE RESTRICT` and written as SQL in the migration, no time column with a database default, `workspace_id` on every table). Exactly these columns. `activity_items`: `id` (uuid, primary key `activity_items_pkey`), `workspace_id`, `user_id` (the person the item is for), `kind` (`mention`, `thread`, `reaction`, `added_to_channel`, `role_changed`, `message_deleted`; check `activity_items_kind_check`), `actor_id` (not null: the person who did it; for a reaction item the latest reactor), `conversation_id` (null only for `role_changed`), `message_id` (null for `added_to_channel` and `role_changed`), `parent_id` (the root of the thread when the message is a reply, else null), `via_channel` (boolean, default false; true only for a mention by `@channel`), `role` (`member` or `admin`, set only for `role_changed`), `occurred_at` (the time shown and the sort time), `read_at`, `done_at`, `snoozed_until`, `woke_at` (all null or a time), `version` (bigint, default 1, raised by 1 by every statement that changes the row), `created_at`. A check `activity_items_shape_check` says which columns each kind needs and which it must leave empty (for example `role` only with `role_changed`, `message_id` with the four message kinds, `parent_id` with every `thread` item, `via_channel` only with `mention`). `activity_reactors`: `item_id`, `user_id` (the person who reacted), `emoji`, `created_at`; primary key (`item_id`, `user_id`, `emoji`). `thread_followers`: `workspace_id`, `conversation_id`, `root_id`, `user_id`, `created_at`; primary key (`root_id`, `user_id`). Foreign keys: (`message_id`, `conversation_id`) to (`id`, `conversation_id`) of `messages` through `messages_id_conversation_id_key` (TOKI-036), the same pair on `thread_followers` for `root_id`, (`conversation_id`, `workspace_id`) to `conversations`, `user_id` and `actor_id` to `users`, `item_id` to `activity_items`. Unique indexes, named: `activity_items_message_key` on (`user_id`, `message_id`) where `kind in ('mention', 'thread')`; `activity_items_reaction_key` on (`user_id`, `message_id`) where `kind = 'reaction'`; `activity_items_deleted_key` on (`user_id`, `message_id`) where `kind = 'message_deleted'`. Other indexes: `activity_items_user_idx` on (`user_id`, `occurred_at` desc, `id` desc) where `done_at is null`; `activity_items_source_idx` on (`message_id`) where `message_id is not null`; `activity_items_conversation_idx` on (`user_id`, `conversation_id`); `activity_items_done_idx` on (`done_at`) where `done_at is not null`; `thread_followers_member_idx` on (`conversation_id`, `user_id`). Check: after the migrations the three tables are empty and have these columns, checks, keys and indexes (read from the catalog); an insert with a kind outside the list, a `role_changed` with a `message_id`, a `mention` with no `message_id`, a `via_channel` on a reaction item and a second `mention` or `thread` item for the same person and message are each refused by the named constraint; a reaction item and a mention item for the same person and message can both exist only when they are of the two groups of the unique indexes; `pnpm db:generate` reports no change after the migration.
- R2. `packages/shared/src/activity/` (exported from the package index) holds: `ACTIVITY_KINDS` and its type; `ACTIVITY_FILTERS` (`all`, `mentions`, `threads`, `reactions`, `other`, `snoozed`) and `activityFilterOfKind(kind)`, which maps `mention` to `mentions`, `thread` to `threads`, `reaction` to `reactions` and `added_to_channel`, `role_changed` and `message_deleted` to `other` (F27, AC-110); the strict Zod schemas `ActivityItemSchema`, `ActivityItemStateSchema`, `ActivitySummarySchema`, `ActivityListQuerySchema`, `ActivityListResponseSchema`, `ActivityReadRequestSchema`, `ActivitySnoozeRequestSchema` and `ActivityEffectSchema` of Design; the schemas of the two events and of the notice (R14, R23), and the effect `cause` (R13); and the numbers `ACTIVITY_PAGE_DEFAULT` (50), `ACTIVITY_PAGE_MAX` (100), `ACTIVITY_PREVIEW_MAX` (200 code points) `ACTIVITY_REACTORS_SHOWN` (3), `ACTIVITY_PURGE_BATCH` (500) and `ACTIVITY_SNOOZE_MAX_DAYS` (366), each a bound on size or time and not a product rule (concern 11). Check: schema tests accept each valid object and refuse an added key, a missing key, a kind outside the list, a filter outside the list and a time that is not ISO 8601 UTC; `activityFilterOfKind` returns the filter above for every kind and no kind maps to `all` or `snoozed`; a type test imports each name from `@toki/shared`.
- R3. Who can be given an item. Every item made by R4 to R9 and R11 passes one function, `selectRecipients(client, conversationId, candidateIds, actorId)` (`client` is a `DbClient`, so it runs in the transaction of the change) of `modules/activity/recipients.ts`, which returns the candidates that: are not the actor (D-071: your own action never notifies you), have a member row in the conversation (D-058: people outside the conversation are not notified), and whose account is `active` (a deactivated person cannot sign in, F41). It is sorted by user id, the order in which every statement of this ticket locks rows (R25). It reads the members with `listMemberIds` of `conversations` (R26 changes its first parameter from `Db` to `DbClient`; the code of the function stays, and a `Tx` is accepted) and the status with `getAccountsByIds` of `members`, each through its `index.ts`, one statement each whatever the number of people. A mute, a level or any preference does not remove a candidate (D-174). Check: a table test with the actor, a non-member, a person who left, a deactivated person and an active member returns only the member; a candidate list of 199 people costs two statements; a person who muted the conversation, or whose level is `none`, is returned; `listMemberIds` called with a transaction sees a member row inserted in that same transaction and not yet committed (the case of `members_added`, R9).
- R4. A mention gives an item. For `message_created` and `message_edited`, with `change.mentionDelta` (TOKI-050 R9) and `change.message`, the handler creates one `mention` item, with `message_id` the message, `parent_id` its root for a reply, `actor_id` the author of the message, `occurred_at` `change.at` and `read_at` null, for each person in `mentionDelta.added` that R3 returns, and, when `mentionDelta.channelAdded` is true, for every member of the conversation that R3 returns (the members are read when the item is made: a person who joined later is not told for an old message, TOKI-050 Design) with `via_channel` true. A person named by `@channel` and by name gets the personal item (`via_channel` false). Only a stored message that is not deleted makes items: a repeat, a refusal and a system line carry no delta and make none. Each item is inserted with `ON CONFLICT ... DO NOTHING` on `activity_items_message_key`, so one person has one item for one message (AC-036), except in one case: when the person already has a `thread` item for the message (a reply they follow, then an edit that names them), the conflict is `DO UPDATE SET kind = 'mention', via_channel = false, read_at = null, done_at = null, occurred_at = change.at, version = version + 1 WHERE kind = 'thread'`, so the mention wins on an edit as it does on a new reply (the proposal of concern 1; a snoozed item stays snoozed); the effect has `isNew` false and `cause` `converted`. In a direct message or a group `@channel` is plain text (TOKI-050 R5) and `channelAdded` is false, so nobody gets a `via_channel` item there. Check: "hi @Mai" in a channel gives Mai one item; the same change twice gives one row; a self-mention gives none; "@channel" in a channel of five gives four items with `via_channel` true; "@Mai @channel" gives Mai a personal item and three `via_channel` items; a mention of a person who is not a member gives none; an edit that adds @Sam gives Sam an item and gives Mai, already told, none; a reply by Sam under Mai's root gives Mai a `thread` item, and an edit of that reply that names Mai turns her item into a `mention` item, unread, with `isNew` false and `cause` `converted`, and she has one item; the actor of the change gets no item in every case.
- R5. An edit takes mentions back. For `message_edited`, for each person in `mentionDelta.removed` the handler removes that person's `mention` item of the message, whatever its state (read, done or snoozed) (F13, F16); when `mentionDelta.channelRemoved` is true it removes the `via_channel` items of the message. A person in `removed` who is still covered by `change.message.mentions.channel` being true and is a member keeps an item, which becomes `via_channel` true. When the edited message is a reply (`change.message.parentId` not null) and the person follows its thread (R6, R7), the item is not removed but becomes a `thread` item (`cause` `converted`, `isNew` false, state kept), by the rule of concern 1 (proposal, not a decision). For a root message (`parentId` null) the item is removed: a `thread` item always points to a reply (R1, `activity_items_shape_check` requires `parent_id` for `thread`), and F13 says the item goes. Check: "hi @Mai" edited to "hi" removes Mai's item, also when it is read and when it is snoozed (its wake job is cancelled, R20); "@Mai @channel" edited to "@channel" turns Mai's item into a `via_channel` item; the same edit of a reply for a person who follows its thread leaves a `thread` item (the two directions of concern 1 are tested: thread to mention in R4, mention to thread here); the same edit of a root message that Mai replied under removes the item and makes no `thread` item; an edit that changes no mention leaves every item as it is.
- R6. Who follows a thread (D-119, FR-34). A person follows the thread of a root message when they are the author of the root, or a row of `thread_followers` has them. The root author is read from the root row and needs no row. The rows are written by R7 only, and nothing deletes one except R12: there is no route and no setting that stops following, and no screen can offer it (AC-091). Check: no route of the module or of `messages` deletes a row of `thread_followers` (a test that calls every route of `activity` and `messages` with a populated `thread_followers` and checks that the row count is unchanged, and a search of `apps/server/src` that finds a `delete` on the table only in `removal.ts`, R12); the root author follows with no row.
- R7. A reply gives a thread item and makes followers. For `message_created` whose `change.message.parentId` is not null and whose message is of kind `user`, the handler (a) takes the followers of the root (R6), (b) removes from them the sender and every person who gets a mention item from R4 for this message (the proposal of concern 1: one item per person and message, the mention wins), (c) passes the rest through R3, (d) inserts one `thread` item for each, with `message_id` the reply, `parent_id` the root, `actor_id` the sender, and (e) afterwards inserts, with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, a follower row for the sender and one for each person in `mentionDelta.added` (the people mentioned in a reply follow, FR-34). A reply also sent to the channel is still a reply. A person mentioned only by `@channel` does not become a follower (concern 2). The same follower rows (e) are written for a `message_edited` of a reply, for the people in `mentionDelta.added`. A deleted root changes nothing: the thread stays (F14) and its followers still get items. Check, with the cases of TS-138 to TS-140: Mai replies to Sam's message, Sam (root author) gets one `thread` item naming Mai and Mai none; Sam then replies, Mai (earlier replier) gets one; Alex, who did none of these, gets none; a reply that mentions Lan makes Lan a follower and gives Lan a `mention` item and no `thread` item; Lan's next reply gives Sam and Mai items; a reply by Sam does not give Sam an item; a muted conversation still gets the item; a follower who left the conversation gets none; the reply's own change called twice makes one item per person.
- R8. A reaction gives one item for the message (D-163, F15). For `reaction_added` with `change.reaction`, when `messageAuthorId` is not the actor and R3 returns it, the handler upserts the item of kind `reaction` for (`messageAuthorId`, `messageId`): it inserts it when absent, with `actor_id` the reactor, `occurred_at` `change.at`, `read_at` null; and it inserts the row (`item_id`, actor, `emoji`) of `activity_reactors` with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`. When the item exists, it sets `actor_id` to the reactor, raises `version`, and, following the proposal of concern 4, sets `occurred_at` to `change.at`, `read_at` and `done_at` to null; a snoozed item stays snoozed. For `reaction_removed` the handler locks the item `FOR UPDATE` (R25), deletes the row of that actor and emoji; when the item has no reactor left it deletes the item, otherwise it sets `actor_id` to the most recent remaining reactor and raises `version`, and changes nothing else. Reacting to one's own message makes nothing (AC-037). Check: Mai reacts 👍 to Sam's message: one item with reactor Mai; Alex reacts 🎉: still one item, reactors Alex and Mai, emojis 👍 and 🎉, unread and first in the list; Mai removes 👍: Mai is out of the item's names; Alex removes 🎉: the item is deleted; Sam reacts to his own message: nothing; Mai with two emoji and one removed stays among the names; a reaction on a reply gives an item with `parent_id` the root.
- R9. Being added to a channel gives an item (F22). For `members_added`, each person in `change.userIds` that R3 returns gets one `added_to_channel` item with `conversation_id`, `actor_id` the person who added, `message_id` null, `occurred_at` `change.at`. A person who joins by themselves (`member_joined`), is put in #general by accepting an invitation, or returns by a restore gets none (your own action). A removal gives the removed person no item (decided by the product owner for the add-and-remove-people spec, concern 1). `channel_created` gives none: the log names no item for the people chosen in the create dialog (concern 5). Check: Minh adds Sam and Lan: two items, each with Minh as actor; a join and a removal give none; the creation of a channel with three first members gives none; adding the same person after a removal gives a second item.
- R10. A changed role gives an item (F40). `registerActivityListeners({ onMemberChanged, db, clock, recordChange })` registers one listener of `onMemberChanged` (TOKI-024 R40). For an event of `kind` `role_changed` with a `targetId` different from `actorId`, it opens a transaction, inserts one `role_changed` item for the target (`actor_id` the actor, `role` the value of `after.role`, `conversation_id` and `message_id` null, `occurred_at` the event's `at`) and calls `recordChange` once with the change `activity_changed` (R13). A deactivation, a reactivation, a role change of oneself and an event of another kind make no item. The event is published after the commit of the role change, so the item is written after it, in its own transaction (concern 9). Check: Lan changes Sam to Admin: Sam gets one item with role `admin`, Lan none; an Admin who demotes themselves gets none; a deactivation gives none; a listener that fails is logged with the ids and does not reach the route (as `publishMemberChanged` says) and the role change stands; with the real handler list, the item and its event are in one transaction (a rollback after `recordChange` leaves neither).
- R11. A deleted message takes its items and may give one (F13, F42, D-049). For `message_deleted` with `change.message`, `authorId`, `deletedAs` and `deletedById` (TOKI-047 R11) the handler first deletes, for every person, the items of kind `mention`, `thread` and `reaction` whose `message_id` is the deleted message (and the reactor rows of the reaction items), whatever their state, and then, when `deletedAs` is `"admin"` and R3 returns `authorId` (the admin's own message is `"author"`, so the admin gets none), inserts one `message_deleted` item for the author with `actor_id` `deletedById`, `message_id` the deleted message and `parent_id` its root for a reply. The item points to the line "Message deleted by an admin" (D-049). A delete by the author makes no item. The deletion of a root removes the root's own items only: the thread items of its replies stay (F14). Check, with TS-175 and TS-052: Minh deletes his own "@Sam please check": Sam's item is gone and the count falls; an Admin deletes Mai's message that carried a mention of Sam and a reaction by Alex: the items of Sam and of Mai (the reaction item) are gone and Mai gets one `message_deleted` item with the Admin as actor; an Admin who deletes their own message gets none; the deletion of a root keeps the thread items of its replies; a message deleted while the author left the conversation gives no item.
- R12. Leaving clears (D-133). For `member_left` and `member_removed`, for each person in `change.userIds` the handler deletes that person's `activity_items` whose `conversation_id` is the conversation (all kinds, with their reactor rows) and their `thread_followers` rows of it. A removal from a private channel and a leave that empties and archives a channel do the same. A person who joins again has no follower row and no items of the earlier time. `role_changed` items have no conversation and stay. Check, with TS-176: Sam leaves #launch: only the #general mention is listed and the unread count is 1; a removal clears the same; the follower rows of that person in that conversation are gone and those of another person are not; after Sam joins again a reply in a thread he once followed gives him no item unless he is its root author.
- R13. The handler and the shape of a change for the events. `createActivityHandler(deps)` of `modules/activity/handler.ts` is a `ChangeHandler` (TOKI-037 R18) that does R4 to R9, R11 and R12 for the change types named there and nothing for any other. It sits after the system-line handler and before the event handler in the list built by `buildChangeHandlers`, because it takes row locks and no lock may follow the counter lock of the event handler (TOKI-036 R18, TOKI-046 R8); the handler of TOKI-073 goes after it. It returns `{ activity }`, an array of `ActivityEffect`: `{ op: "upsert", userId, item: ActivityItem, isNew: boolean, cause }` for an item that was created or changed, where `cause` is `created` (`isNew` true), `reaction_added`, `reaction_removed`, `converted` (a change of kind, R4, R5) or `woke` (R21), so TOKI-073 can tell the wake from the others, and `{ op: "remove", userId, itemIds: string[] }` for items deleted. The work is this ticket's (the text that the merged specs must carry is in "Plan changes"): it changes `createRecordChange` of TOKI-037 so that a handler may return `{ message?, activity? }` (the existing `{ message }` stays valid) and the handlers after it read `change.activity`; `Change` gains the optional field `activity` and a third member of its union, `ActivityChange`: `{ type: "activity_changed", workspaceId, actorId, userIds, at, activity }`, with no `conversationId`, called by R10 and R21 and ignored by every other handler. A handler that finds nothing to do returns nothing. Check: with a recording handler after it, the send of a message with a mention leaves a `change.activity` of one `upsert` effect with `isNew` true and the item the 200 of the list route would return; a delete leaves a `remove` effect with the ids; a change that creates nothing leaves no `activity` field; the existing tests of `createRecordChange` pass; a type test checks the union; every effect has a `cause`, and a new reaction on an existing item, a removed reaction, a conversion and a wake give the four other causes.
- R14. The events. The event handler of TOKI-038 (R20) writes, after the events of the change itself, one event for each effect of `change.activity`: scope `user`, `userId` the person, kind `activity.upserted` with `data` `{ item }`, or `activity.removed` with `data` `{ itemIds }`; one event for each effect, and the change `activity_changed` writes these and no other event. The handler of R13 makes at most one `upsert` effect for a person in one change (R4 to R9 each give a person at most one item, and R7 leaves out the people who get a mention item) and one `remove` effect for a person with all their `itemIds`, so `data` stays `{ item }` and no event holds two items. A message deleted by an admin gives its author a `remove` and an `upsert`: two effects of different ops, two events. The schemas of the two kinds join the registry of `packages/shared/src/realtime/events.ts` and a row for each is added to `kinds.ts` saying that neither changes any map; `EventChange` gains the optional field `activity?` of the shape above (TOKI-038 R20, "Rows of later tickets"). A user event is sent to every connection of that person and to nobody else (TOKI-038 R16) and is replayed to that person only (TOKI-039). Check, with the real handler: a mention writes one `activity.upserted` event, scope `user`, `userId` the mentioned person, and a person with two tabs receives it on both, a third person receives nothing; `@channel` in a channel of five writes four events; a delete writes one `activity.removed` for each person who lost items; the log has no event for a change that created nothing; a rollback after `recordChange` leaves no row and no event.
- R15. What an item says, and how it is built. `buildActivityItems(db, rows, readers)` of `modules/activity/build.ts` makes every `ActivityItem` that a route, an effect or an event holds, so the four are equal for one row. `ActivityItem` is the strict object `{ id, version, kind, actorId, conversationId, messageId, parentId, preview, viaChannel, role, reactions, occurredAt, unread, snoozedUntil, backFromSnooze }`. `preview` is the text of the message, cut to `ACTIVITY_PREVIEW_MAX` code points, for `mention` and `thread`, `null` for the others and for a message the reader does not find; it is read at the time of the build with `listMessageTexts(client, ids)`, a new reader of `messages` (one statement, `where id = any($1)`, a deleted message gives `null`), injected by `app.ts` (`activity` imports nothing of `messages`), so an edit shows in the next read and nothing copies the text. An edit of a message gives no `activity.upserted` event to the holders of its items: an open screen shows the old text until it reads the list again (the next event for that item, or a reload, shows the new one). `reactions` is `null` except for `reaction`, where it is `{ emojis, actors, actorCount }`: the emojis in use in the order of `REACTION_EMOJI` (TOKI-046), the latest `ACTIVITY_REACTORS_SHOWN` distinct reactors, newest first, and the number of distinct reactors. `occurredAt` is the sort time (R16); an item is *woken* when `snoozed_until` is set, `done_at` is null and `snoozed_until` is not later than the clock's now; the build gives the state the wake job would write (R21), so it does not depend on the job having run: `unread` is `read_at` null or woken; `snoozedUntil` is set only while `snoozed_until` is later than now; `backFromSnooze` is true when the item is woken, or `woke_at` is set and `read_at` is null (concern 6). The builder makes one statement for the reactors and one for the texts, whatever the number of rows. Check: a page of 50 items of the four reader kinds costs the page statement plus one for the reactors and one for the texts; an item built for an event equals the item of a later list read; an item that was read, then snoozed, with the clock moved past the time and no job run, is built with `unread` true and `backFromSnooze` true, and after the job runs the built item is the same; a text of 300 characters is cut to 200; an edited message shows its new text at the next read; the reactor names are the latest three and `actorCount` the total.
- R16. `GET /api/v1/activity` returns the person's items for one filter. Query (strict): `filter` (one of `ACTIVITY_FILTERS`, default `all`), `limit` (1 to `ACTIVITY_PAGE_MAX`, default `ACTIVITY_PAGE_DEFAULT`), `cursor` (opaque, from a previous answer). It answers 200 `{ asOf, items, nextCursor }` where `asOf` is the last event number at the snapshot, read first in the same `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` transaction as the rows (the helper `inReadSnapshot` of `apps/server/src/bootstrap/`, injected), so a client may apply the events with a higher number on top of the answer. The main list (`all`, `mentions`, `threads`, `reactions`, `other`) holds the items with `done_at` null that are not snoozed (`snoozed_until` null or not later than the clock's now), newest first by the sort time and then by id, where the sort time of an item is `occurred_at`, or the later of `occurred_at` and `snoozed_until` for an item that was snoozed and has come back (the wake time is the time the person chose, so the order does not depend on when the job ran, R21). (an item whose time has passed is built as in R15, unread and labelled, before the job runs.) `filter=snoozed` holds the items with `snoozed_until` later than now, the one that returns first at the top. ## Design
### What each change does
| Change (type) | Activity does | R |
|---|---|---|
| `message_created` (a main-list message) | `mention` items for `mentionDelta.added` and, for `channelAdded`, the members | R4 |
| `message_created` (a reply) | the same, then `thread` items for the followers, then follower rows | R4, R7 |
| `message_edited` | `mention` items for `added`; removes or changes them for `removed`; follower rows for a reply | R4, R5, R7 |
| `message_deleted` | removes the items of the message; `message_deleted` item for the author when `deletedAs` is `admin` | R11 |
| `reaction_added`, `reaction_removed` | one `reaction` item for the author, its reactors | R8 |
| `members_added` | `added_to_channel` item for each person added | R9 |
| `member_left`, `member_removed` | clears the person's items and follower rows of the conversation | R12 |
| `onMemberChanged` `role_changed` (listener) | `role_changed` item for the target | R10 |
| `activity_changed` (the wake job, the listener) | no row; carries the effect to the event handler | R10, R13, R21 |
| every other type | nothing | R13 |

### The rule of who is told
One rule, `selectRecipients` (R3), stands behind every kind: not the actor, a member of the conversation, an active account. It is applied to the people each kind names (the mentioned, the followers, the author of a reacted message, the people added, the author of a deleted message). Nothing in it reads a level, a mute, Pause or Focus: those decide a toast and a push (TOKI-073), not whether the person has the item (D-174, "the items are still created and counted in the Activity badge, with no toast, push or sound"). The counts and the badge are therefore the same for a muted conversation and an open one.
A person who is not a member of the conversation when the item is made gets none: people outside the conversation are not notified (D-058). A person who stops being a member loses their items of it at once (R12, D-133), so no item points to a place the person cannot open.

### Data model
`activity_items` is one table for the six kinds, because the list, the counts, the cursor and the three states (read, done, snoozed) are the same for all of them, and a person's items number in the hundreds. The columns that a kind does not use are null and the check of R1 says which. Reading a state:
- unread: `read_at` is null. Read: it is set. Done: `done_at` is set (the row is kept for Undo, R19, R22). Snoozed: `snoozed_until` is later than now. Back from snooze: `woke_at` is set and the item is unread.
- `version` rises by 1 with every change of the row (a state change, an update of a reaction item). The client keeps the higher of two versions of one item, so a notice and an event that arrive in either order give the same result (R23).
- `activity_reactors` keeps who reacted with what to the message of a `reaction` item, so the item can name "the latest names" and drop one person on removal without reading `message_reactions` (TOKI-046 owns that table; this ticket reads none of it). Rows are written from the `change.reaction` of each change.
- `thread_followers` keeps followers. The root author is not stored: it is read from the root row, so the first reply needs no row to find them. The blueprint's table list names `thread_followers`; it lives in this module because the rule is this ticket's (concern 8).
Tables of other modules are never read: messages through `listMessageTexts`, members through `getAccountsByIds`, conversations through `listMemberIds`.

### The item
`ActivityItem` (R15) holds ids and facts, no wording:
| Key | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `id`, `version`, `kind` | identity, change counter, one of the six kinds |
| `actorId` | who did it; for a reaction the latest reactor; the screen takes the name from the people it holds |
| `conversationId`, `messageId`, `parentId` | where it points. `messageId` is null for `added_to_channel` and `role_changed`; `parentId` is the root for a reply |
| `preview` | the start of the message for `mention` and `thread` |
| `viaChannel` | the mention was `@channel` ("mentioned the channel", F13) |
| `role` | the new role for `role_changed` |
| `reactions` | `{ emojis, actors, actorCount }` for `reaction` |
| `occurredAt` | the time shown, and the sort time |
| `unread`, `snoozedUntil`, `backFromSnooze` | the state |
How an item is opened, for the screen: a message item calls `open({ conversationId, messageId, parentId })` of `useOpenMessage()` (TOKI-068), which opens the conversation at the message, or the thread panel at the reply when `parentId` is set (F13: a mention in a reply opens the thread at that reply); an `added_to_channel` item opens `/c/{conversationId}`; a `role_changed` item opens nothing. A `message_deleted` item opens the conversation at the line "Message deleted by an admin".

### Events and the notice
| What | Kind | Scope | `data` | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| an item made or changed by a change | event `activity.upserted` | user | `{ item }` | in the transaction of the change (R14) |
| items deleted by a change | event `activity.removed` | user | `{ itemIds }` | same |
| a snoozed item woke | event `activity.upserted` | user | `{ item }` with `backFromSnooze` true | in the transaction of the wake job (R21) |
| the person's own mark, Done, Undo, Snooze, Unsnooze | live `activity.changed` | the person's connections | `{ states }` | after the commit of the route (R23) |
Two paths, on purpose: what other people cause is numbered and replayed after a lost connection (TOKI-039), because the change is already a numbered write and one more event costs no extra wait for the lock; the person's own marks are frequent, private to the person and repaired by reading the list, so they use the unnumbered notice, as the read pointer does (TOKI-053 R9). A person who has been offline reads the summary and the list again after the events are replayed.
A change that makes an item for many people writes one event for each person: a user event has one `user_id` (TOKI-036 R13). For `@channel` in a channel of 200 people that is 199 events in one transaction while the counter lock is held (concern 12).

### The jobs
- `activity.wake-snoozed` (R21): one job for each snooze, started at the chosen time, key `<itemId>:<ms>`, cancelled by `cancelByKey` on Done, Unsnooze and a new Snooze. The state is correct without it, because the list reads `snoozed_until <= now` as returned; the job makes the stored row agree, raises `version` and sends the event so that a screen that is open shows the item at the top and the notification engine can toast "Back from snooze".
- `activity.purge-done` (R22): hourly, deletes items done more than 24 hours ago.

### What the later tickets get

It applies `activity.upserted`, `activity.removed` and `activity.changed` by `version`, and reads `summary` again after any of them (one request at a time) so the counts equal the rows (D-074). - TOKI-102 (preferences). Nothing from this ticket depends on a preference: items are made for everyone (D-174).
- TOKI-068. The press of an item is the screen's (TOKI-066), because this ticket is the server; TOKI-068's table gives the press of Activity to "TOKI-065 and TOKI-066", and TOKI-045 R16 names this ticket as the caller of `useOpenThread`. ### Errors
- No session -> 401 `unauthenticated`. A foreign `Origin` -> 403 `origin_not_allowed`. A body that is not JSON -> 415.
- A malformed id, query, cursor or body, `until` not later than now -> 400 `validation_failed`.
- An item of another person, an unknown item, an item purged after Done -> 404 `not_found`.
- A listener, job or handler failure -> the transaction rolls back; a request answers 500 `internal_error`; a job retries (TOKI-064 R8).

## Data safety
- Items are the person's own: no route takes another person's id, an item of another person is 404, and no event or notice of this ticket is sent to anyone but the person it is for (scope `user`, R14, R23). Nobody sees who has read or marked an item: the rule for items is this spec's, in the same spirit as D-048 (no seen receipts for messages).
- `preview` is a copy of message text in an event, which stays in the log for the retention window (TOKI-036 R20). The `message.created` event of the same message holds the same text for everyone in the conversation, so this adds no new reader; a deleted message's text is emptied (TOKI-047) and a deleted message makes no preview.
- Leaving a channel removes the person's items and follower rows of it (R12), so no item keeps a pointer into a conversation the person cannot read.
- No log line holds a preview or message text (R28). A job payload holds ids only (TOKI-064 R7).

## Areas of concern
1. One item or two for a reply that mentions a follower. D-071 and FR-14 say a mention gives "exactly one item" and FR-34 gives each follower a thread item; no decision says what a person who is both gets. The item rows allow either: R7 and R4 use the unique index of `activity_items_message_key`. Not decided. Proposal, not a decision: one item per person and message, of kind `mention` (the more specific), so the person sees it under Mentions and not under Threads, and the count rises by one; when the mention is edited out of a reply and the person still follows the thread, the item becomes a `thread` item (R5), and when an edit names a person who has a `thread` item for the reply, it becomes a `mention` item (R4). The other reading is two items, one per kind: it needs the unique index to include the kind and removes the rule of R5. R4, R5 and R7 follow the proposal until the owner answers. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
2. The edges of the follow rule. FR-34 says everyone "mentioned in the thread" follows. Not decided: whether a person mentioned in the root message (not in a reply) follows, and whether `@channel` in a reply makes every member a follower. R6 and R7 build only what the words settle: the root author, every sender of a reply, and the people named by name in a reply (or an edit of a reply). Proposal, not a decision: a person named in the root also follows (one more follower row written when the root is sent, with the same rule), and `@channel` does not make followers (it is a flag, not a list of people, TOKI-050). Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
3. When an item becomes read by itself, and what "done on its own" means. F13 says the item "becomes read" once the message is on screen; AC-038 says opening the mentioned message marks the item "done by itself" and the count falls by one; D-071 says an item "becomes done on its own once the thing it asks for is done"; TS-171 expects the row to stay listed, without the unread dot, after it is pressed. Not decided: whether "done on its own" removes the row (as Done does) or only marks it read, and whether the trigger is the message on screen (F13), the message opened (AC-038) or the read pointer passing it (TOKI-053 concern 8 asks the same for the `@` dot). Proposal, not a decision: it only marks the item read and never removes it (Done stays the person's own act, FR-41), and the trigger is the one TOKI-053 concern 8 settles for the `@` dot. This ticket gives the route (`POST /api/v1/activity/read` with `messageId`, R18) and does not fix the trigger; TOKI-066 sends it. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
4. A new reaction on a read or done item. D-163 says the item is "updated with the latest names" and says nothing of its state. Not decided: whether a reaction that arrives after the person read the item, or marked it done, makes it unread again and moves it to the top. Proposal, not a decision: yes, it is unread, not done, first in the list, as a new reaction is new information (R8 follows it); a snoozed item stays snoozed. The other reading keeps the state and changes the names only. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
5. A channel created with people. F22 gives an "added you" item for adding people to a channel; F20 says the people chosen when the channel is created become members and names no item. R9 builds the item for `members_added` only. Not decided: whether the people put in a new channel by its creator also get the item. Proposal, not a decision: yes, the same item with the creator as actor, because a person put in a channel by someone else should hear it as they would after an add; it is one more case in R9 for `channel_created`, written with the same function. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
6. How long "Back from snooze" stays. F27 and FR-41 say an item that returns at the chosen time is labelled; the prototype clears the label when the row is pressed and not on "Mark all read". Not decided. Proposal, not a decision: the label shows while the item is unread (`backFromSnooze`, R15), so any way of reading it, "Mark all read" included, ends it; the prototype's narrower rule needs a second column and a rule for `read` with `all`. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
7. Events for other people's changes, a notice for the person's own marks. Decided in this spec: what other people cause is a numbered user event written in the transaction of the cause (R14), and what the person does themselves is the unnumbered notice (R23). Reason: the cause already holds the counter lock for its own event, so an item event adds no extra wait and is replayed after a lost connection; the person's own marks are frequent and a lock for each would queue every writer behind them (the reasoning of TOKI-053 concern 5). Cost: the two paths need the `version` rule of R23 on the client. Owner: Thanh Truong.
8. Followers are stored. Decided in this spec: a table `thread_followers`, written by the handler (R6, R7), the root author implied. Reason: the thread summary keeps only three repliers (TOKI-045), so followers cannot be read from it; reading every author of a long thread at each reply grows with the thread; and a stored set can be cleared when a person leaves (R12) and extended by a mention edit. Cost: one table and one write for each reply. TOKI-045 stores nothing (its Design, "What the later tickets get"). Owner: Thanh Truong.
9. The item for a changed role is written after the commit. Not decided. Proposal, not a decision: the listener of `onMemberChanged` (TOKI-024 R40) writes it in its own transaction after the commit of the role change (R10). Reason: the event of TOKI-024 is published after the commit and the route has no seam inside its transaction; adding one would change a merged ticket for one item. Cost the owner would see: if the process stops between the commit and the listener the item is not written, and the person never learns of the new role from Activity; the role change itself and the next load of the person's role are not affected (TOKI-024 concern 8). If the owner wants no loss, the role routes call `recordChange` inside their transaction and this listener goes. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
10. No change to the bootstrap answer. Decided in this spec: the badge and the chip counts come from `GET /api/v1/activity/summary` (R17), read by the screen after the bootstrap call; the bootstrap answer gains no field. Reason: the summary carries its own `asOf` for the replay of events, and the bootstrap transaction already grows with every ticket (TOKI-031 R14, TOKI-053 R8). Cost: the first paint of the badge waits one request. If the badge must be in the first answer, `buildActivitySummary` of this module is the function to add there. Owner: Thanh Truong.
11. Bounds. Not decided. Proposal, not a decision: a page is 50 items and at most 100, the preview is cut to 200 code points, an item names its latest 3 reactors, a purge runs in batches of 500, a snooze may be at most 366 days ahead, and a done item is kept 24 hours (R2, R20, R22). A page, a preview and a batch must have a size, 3 matches the three avatars the thread line shows (F14), and these are technical constants of `@toki/shared`. The 24 hours is the one the owner would see: the log says Undo lasts 5 seconds (D-069), and the server takes a Done back for 24 hours (a second device or a retry can do it); the 24 hours keeps Undo working with no copy of the item and keeps the table small. If the owner wants the server to refuse `reopen` after 5 seconds, the job removes done items sooner and R19 gains a time check. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
12. The cost of a change that tells many people. Decided in this spec: one numbered user event for each person given an item (R14). For `@channel` in a channel of 200 people that is 199 events in one transaction while the counter lock is held (TOKI-036 "How the event number stays free of gaps"). Reason: a user event has one `user_id`; the alternative, one conversation event that lists the people, would send the list to everyone and put a person-specific fact on the conversation's audience. Cost: the lock is held longer for such a message; `@channel` in a big channel asks for confirmation (D-041, TOKI-050) and is rare, and TOKI-089 measures the 150 ms target with it. If it fails the measure, the fix is a batch writer in `realtime`, not a change here. Owner: Thanh Truong.
13. Which people get items. Not decided. Proposal, not a decision: only active accounts that are members get an item (R3), because a deactivated person cannot sign in and cannot open the place (F41). Cost the owner would see: a person reactivated later finds none of the items of the time they were away, while D-094 says that accepting the new invitation reactivates the account "with its history". Reason for the proposal: it needs no extra state. The other reading creates items for a deactivated member and keeps them until reactivation. The keeping of Done for Undo is concern 11. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
