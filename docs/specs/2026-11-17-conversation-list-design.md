# Conversation list design

Date: 2026-11-17 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/conversation-list.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-033

## Goal
A signed-in person needs to see the conversations they take part in, see which have something new, and open one. After TOKI-015 the shell has a column for the list and after TOKI-032 the store holds the conversations already grouped, filtered and named, but nothing draws them and a conversation has no address.

After this change the sidebar shows the Starred, Channels and Direct messages groups with collapse, the All, Unread and Mentions filters and a search on the name; the Direct messages heading has the '+' of D-086 and D-154; a '+ Browse channels' row ends the list; a starred conversation shows a star after its name (D-143); a filter that matches nothing has its own empty state; every conversation has the address `/c/:id`; the app opens on the conversation last viewed on the device; and the collapsed groups and the chosen filter are remembered on the device; a conversation that opens is shown in the list, so a filter, a search or a collapsed group that would hide it is changed (D-076, R34). The list is a presentational component in `ui-web`; `apps/web` wires it to the store, the router and the device's storage.

Groups and rows
- R4. `groups` is an array of `{ id, label, collapsed, rows, keepWhenEmpty?, action? }` with `id` one of `conversationGroupIds`. They are drawn in the order of the array. A group is drawn when it has one row or more, or when `keepWhenEmpty` is true; otherwise nothing of it is drawn. A drawn group is `div.cgroup[data-group=<id>]` holding `div.sech` and `div.crows` with one row each, in the order of `rows` (the list sorts nothing). A drawn group always holds `div.crows` with an id; when collapsed it has the attribute `hidden` and holds no row, so `aria-controls` never points at nothing. The heading is a `button.sec` with `type="button"`, `aria-expanded` equal to `!collapsed`, `aria-controls` the id of `div.crows`, a `DownIcon` and a `span` with `label`; the class `fold` when collapsed (the arrow turns, Table A). A press calls `onToggleGroup(id)`. A collapsed group holds no row, so no row of it is in the Tab order. Check: DOM test with three groups, one empty without `keepWhenEmpty` (not drawn), one empty with it (heading and an empty `div.crows`), one collapsed (`hidden`, no row, `aria-controls` equal to the id of that element); a press; the keyboard (Enter and Space on the heading).
- R5. A group with `action = { label, onPress }` draws, in `div.sech`, after `button.sec` and not inside it (a button cannot hold a button), an `IconButton` (`icon="plus"`, size `sm`, `label` from `action.label`) with the class `sec-add`. A press calls `action.onPress`. Without `action` no such button is drawn. Check: DOM test; `button.sec-add` has `button.sec` as its previous sibling and no ancestor that is a `button`.
- R6. A row is `ConversationRowData`: `{ id, href, kind, name, isPrivate?, isStarred?, avatar?, unreadCount?, unreadLabel?, hasMention?, mentionLabel?, isMuted?, mutedLabel?, privateLabel?, starredLabel?, deactivatedLabel?, draftLabel?, current? }` with `kind` one of `channel`, `dm`, `group_dm` and `avatar` `{ name, hue, presence?, presenceLabel? }`. It renders an `a.cr` with `href`, in this order: `span.gl` holding the glyph (a `HashIcon` for a public channel, a `LockIcon` for a private one, an `Avatar` of TOKI-013 of size 24 with the optional presence for a `dm`, a `UsersIcon` for a `group_dm`); `span.nm` holding `name`, then `span.sr` with `privateLabel` for a private channel, with `mutedLabel` for a muted conversation, and a `Tag` (TOKI-012) with `deactivatedLabel` when given; when `isStarred` is true, after `span.nm`, a `span.star` holding a `StarIcon` and a `span.sr` with `starredLabel` (D-143: "starred glyph in the list"; where on the row is Decided in this spec, concern 5); the `draftLabel` in `span.draft` when given and `current` is not true; a `Badge` of variant `mention` showing `@` with `label` from `mentionLabel` when `hasMention`; a `Badge` showing `unreadCount` with `label` from `unreadLabel` when R7 says so. `current` sets `aria-current="page"`. The class `un` and the class `mutec` are set by R7. A plain left click calls `onOpenRow(row, event)` and cancels the browser's navigation; a click with Ctrl, Meta, Shift or Alt, a middle click and a click when `onOpenRow` is missing are left to the browser, as for the shell's items (TOKI-015 R5). The rows are links so that a conversation can be opened in a new tab and copied as an address (D-089). Check: DOM test for each kind and each optional prop, and click tests with `user-event`, the four modifiers included.
- R7. The look of a row follows the fields and nothing else. A row is unread when `unreadCount` is above 0 and `isMuted` is not true: it gets the class `un` (bold name) and shows the count Badge, as given, with no cap (TOKI-012 concern 4). A muted row (`isMuted`) gets the class `mutec` (grey name) and shows neither bold nor count (D-103). `hasMention` shows the `@` Badge whether the row is muted or not: whether it is set for a muted conversation is the producer's rule (D-088, TOKI-054), not this component's. A count of 0, a negative number, `NaN` or a missing value draws no count. Check: a table test over the eight combinations of unread, muted and mention, and one over 0, -1, `NaN`, 1 and 100.

The wiring in `apps/web`
- R15. `apps/web/src/conversation-list/ConversationListPane.tsx` is the one place that turns the store into `ConversationListProps`. It reads, from the store of TOKI-032, what "What the list reads" (Design) names (`getList` and the lookups) and nothing else; it holds no grouping, sorting, filtering or naming of its own, so the rows come in the order the store gives and a test of the pane with a fake store that returns a fixed order draws that order. The group ids and labels are `starred`, `channels`, `direct` and the texts of R27. The rows carry `isStarred` from the store's row (TOKI-032 R21) to the row of R6. The name of a row is the store's `name`: for a group direct message it is the full `displayName` of the other members joined with `, ` (`Alex Phạm, Thu Hoàng`), and the tests and the browser fixture use those names until TOKI-032 concern 12 (short names) is decided; the pane does not shorten a name, and a long one is cut by the ellipsis of Table A. A conversation is in exactly one group, the one the store puts it in (a starred channel is in Starred only, F09, D-143). Check: a test with a fake store of 3 groups draws them in order; the same conversation never appears twice.
- R16. The wiring decides which groups are kept when empty. `keepWhenEmpty` is true for the `direct` group when the filter is All and the search is empty, and false in every other case, so a person with no direct message still sees the heading and its '+' (concern 2). Starred and Channels are never kept when empty (F09: an empty group is not shown). Check: a test with a store that has no direct message: the heading is drawn with filter All and an empty search, and not with the filter Unread or with a search typed; a store with only #general draws Channels and no Starred.
- R17. Filter and search. The pane keeps `filter` (`all`, `unread`, `mentions`; default `all`) and `query` (default empty) and asks the store for the list with them (R15). A press on a chip sets the filter; typing sets the query. `empty.onAction` (the button 'Clear filter') sets the filter to `all` and the query to empty. The search is not remembered (R18). R34 also sets the filter and the search. Check: component test with a fake store: each chip, typing, the clear button; the empty state appears when the store returns no row and goes when cleared.
- R18. Remembered on the device (F09: "Collapsed groups and the filter are remembered per device"). The pane stores the filter under the key `conversationList.filter` and the collapsed groups under `conversationList.collapsed` in `platform.storage.device` (TOKI-018), as one JSON text each: the filter as the string `all`, `unread` or `mentions`, the collapsed groups as an array of group ids, each with the id of the signed-in person (`{ "userId": …, "value": … }`). On start the pane reads them; a missing key, text that is not valid JSON, a value outside the lists, or a `userId` that is not the signed-in person's gives the default (filter `all`, no group collapsed), so a second person who signs in on the same device does not inherit the first one's. A write that the storage refuses (it returns `false`) leaves the state in memory and shows no error. Check: tests with a fake storage for each case: stored and read back; invalid JSON; unknown value; another person's value; a refusing storage.
- R19. A press on a group heading toggles that group in the collapsed set, writes it (R18) and the list updates at once. A group that is collapsed stays collapsed when a conversation changes or a new one arrives, and when the filter changes. A collapsed group still counts for R11: rows that a collapsed group holds are rows. The one exception is R34: a conversation that opens and would be hidden by its collapsed group expands that group. Check: component test.
- R20. Opening a row. The pane gives `onOpenRow` the router's `navigate({ to: "/c/$conversationId", params })`, a push (not a replace), so the browser's Back button returns to the place before (D-089). The push carries `state: { fromList: true }` only when the press is made at the address `/` (a narrow window showing the list), so `fromList` means that the previous history entry is `/`; a press made at another address (another conversation, after a window changed from wide to narrow) carries no flag (R21, `onBack`). `href` of each row is the same address, `/c/<id>`. The current row (`current`, `aria-current="page"`) is the one whose id is the `$conversationId` of the router's current location; a person at another section has no current row. Check: component test with a memory router: a click at `/` navigates with the flag, a click at `/c/a` navigates without it, and `aria-current` moves; the browser test of R30 presses a row, then Back.
- R21. The route `/c/$conversationId` (child of `appLayoutRoute`, TOKI-015 R30; `src/routes/conversation.tsx`, with a `validateSearch` that returns no search parameter, which TOKI-035 R8 extends with `panel`) resolves the id with `hasConversation` of the store. The route draws nothing in the main area while the store's `status` is `idle` or `loading` (the first load: `loading` is only while no data is held, TOKI-032 R10) and does not redirect then; during a refresh (`isRefreshing`) the status stays `ready`, so the page and the list stay drawn. When `status` is `failed` the main area stays empty until the Retry of TOKI-082 (a failed first load, Scope). Once `ready`: (a) the id is a conversation of the list: the main area draws `ConversationPage` with that conversation, and the id is written as the last viewed (R22). (b) The id is not in the list and was never in it during this session (a link, an old bookmark, a channel that is not joined or does not exist, an archived channel): the route draws `UnjoinedView` (the seam below) with `channelId` the id and these three callbacks. `onJoined(id)` needs nothing from the route, because the store now holds the conversation and the route draws (a). `onMember(detail)` adds the id to `memberChecked` (a set kept by the layout route, cleared with the store's reset, R32) and calls `refresh()` once; when the store then holds the conversation the route draws (a); otherwise (an archived channel that the person belongs to) the id is in `memberChecked`, so the route draws the not-found view of TOKI-006 directly, does not draw `UnjoinedView` again and does not call `onMember` again, until TOKI-101 decides (concern 13). `onNotFound()` draws the not-found view of TOKI-006 (later the shared page of TOKI-082, D-131). No message of a conversation that is not in the list is requested by this route (D-116). (c) The id was in the list during this session and no longer is (the person left it, it was archived, the person was removed): the route replaces itself with the address of the fallback of R22 and shows no error (F23: "the view moves to #general"). "Was in the list during this session" is the set `seen` that the layout route keeps in a ref: at every change of the store while `status` is `ready` it adds every conversation id the store holds, and it is emptied with `memberChecked` at `reset()` and at a change of person (R32). `UnjoinedView` is `apps/web/src/conversation/UnjoinedView.tsx`, a file of this ticket with the props `{ channelId, onJoined, onMember, onNotFound }`; in this ticket its body draws the not-found view of TOKI-006 and nothing else; TOKI-034 replaces the body with `UnjoinedChannel` (its R16, the same four props) and keeps the file and the props, as TOKI-035 does with `ConversationPage`, so this ticket imports nothing of TOKI-034. `ConversationPage({ conversationId, onBack })` is a placeholder: it draws one element in `main` with `data-testid="conversation-page"` and the conversation's name in an `h1`; TOKI-035 (the header and the Details panel), TOKI-041 and TOKI-043 replace its body and keep its file, its props and its test id. `onBack` is given by the route: when the current history entry has `state.fromList` true (set by R20, so the previous entry is `/`) it calls `history.back()`, otherwise it navigates to `/` with `replace: true`; so the Back button of the header ends on the list on a narrow screen and never leaves the app for a conversation reached from the list. TOKI-101 (archive and restore, its R13) asks to add one branch to this route, in this file and ahead of (b) and (c): an id whose held `getChannelDetail(id)` is an archived channel that the person belongs to draws its archived page, and is neither sent to the not-found view of (b) nor replaced by the fallback of (c). This ticket does not build that branch: F09 and F23 send a person whose conversation was archived or left to #general, which (c) does, and whether an archived channel keeps the person on its address is not decided (concern 13; TOKI-101 concern 6). It is TOKI-101's change to this file, made in its own pull request if the owner decides for it; nothing here blocks it, because the route reads the store only through `hasConversation`, `seen` and `memberChecked` and the branch needs no field of this ticket. The tests of (b) and (c) below use ids for which the store holds no detail of an archived channel that the person belongs to, so they stay as written when the branch lands, and TOKI-101 adds its own cases. The limit: after a window changed from wide to narrow, a conversation opened from another conversation carries no flag, and the header Back replaces it with `/` (the list), leaving the earlier conversation under it in the history. Check: tests with a memory router and a fake store for (a), (b) with each of the three callbacks (a test that counts `refresh()` calls: `onMember` for an id that the refresh does not add ends in the not-found view after one `refresh()` and one `onMember`), (c) with an id in `seen` and with an id that was never seen, a store that has not loaded, a failed store, a refresh in progress that keeps the page, and both ways of `onBack` including a press with no flag.
- R22. Last viewed and the opening address. `apps/web/src/conversation-list/last-viewed.ts` stores the id under `conversationList.lastViewed` in `platform.storage.device`, as `{ "userId", "value" }` (R18's rules for a missing, invalid or another person's value). ### The routes and the shell
