# Conversation list design

Date: 2026-11-17 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/conversation-list.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-033

## Goal
A signed-in person needs to see the conversations they take part in, see which have something new, and open one. After TOKI-015 the shell has a column for the list and after TOKI-032 the store holds the conversations already grouped, filtered and named, but nothing draws them and a conversation has no address.

After this change the sidebar shows the Starred, Channels and Direct messages groups with collapse, the All, Unread and Mentions filters and a search on the name; the Direct messages heading has the '+' of D-086 and D-154; a '+ Browse channels' row ends the list; a filter that matches nothing has its own empty state; every conversation has the address `/c/:id`; the app opens on the conversation last viewed on the device; and the collapsed groups and the chosen filter are remembered on the device. The list is a presentational component in `ui-web`; `apps/web` wires it to the store, the router and the device's storage.

## Scope
- In:
  - `packages/ui-web/src/conversation-list/`: `ConversationList` (header, filters, groups, rows, empty state, the Browse channels row) and its CSS, carried over from the prototype where the prototype is right and changed where it is not (Table A, Design, "What the prototype does, measured").
  - `apps/web/src/conversation-list/`: the wiring of the list pane to the store of TOKI-032 (reading, loading and resetting it); the routes `/` and `/c/$conversationId`; the device's memory of the filter, the collapsed groups and the last viewed conversation; the colour of a person's avatar; the two dialogs of TOKI-034 opened from the list (R25).
  - `apps/web/src/conversation/ConversationPage.tsx`, the placeholder page that the route draws in the main area until TOKI-035, TOKI-041 and TOKI-043 fill it (R21), and `apps/web/src/shell/shell-slots.tsx`, the way a route gives the shell its side panel (R26).
  - One change to `apps/web/src/routes/app-layout.tsx` of TOKI-015: it passes `list`, `chatOpen` and `workspace` to `AppShell` (R24). One change to the index route of TOKI-015 R30 (R23).
  - The namespace `conversationList` in both catalogs of `packages/i18n` (R27).
  - Tests: unit tests, and one browser test file that runs the real list in the shell with stubbed network answers (R30).
- Out (not now), each with its owner:
  - Grouping, ordering, filtering by All, Unread and Mentions, the search match, the naming of a conversation (the other person's name for a DM, the people's names for a group) and the conversation data itself: TOKI-032. This ticket draws what the store gives and keeps the order it gives.
  - The unread count, the @ mark and the latest message of a conversation: the read pointer (TOKI-053) and TOKI-054 supply the values; until then every conversation has no count and no mention and the Unread and Mentions chips show their empty state (concern 4). Which ticket draws the count on a row: this one, from the fields in "What the list reads" (R7); the rules that fill those fields (muted, mention in a muted conversation, D-088) are TOKI-054's.
  - The 'Draft' label of a row: TOKI-051 (`.draft`, TOKI-012 table of pieces). The row has an optional prop for it (R6).
  - The presence dot of a direct message avatar: TOKI-055. The row has an optional presence prop (R6).
  - Hiding a new direct message from the other person until its first message (F25): not built here; its owner is not decided (concern 3). The list draws every direct message the store gives.
  - Browse channels, the people picker, the view of a channel that is not joined and what they do: TOKI-034 (`BrowseChannelsDialog`, `NewMessageDialog`, `UnjoinedChannel`). This ticket draws the three controls that open the dialogs, holds their open state and places the view (R21, R25).
  - The conversation header (the Back button of a narrow screen, the star, the topic), the Details panel, leaving a channel, Star and Mute: TOKI-035; this ticket gives the page the `onBack` that the header calls (R21). Closing an open panel with Back before leaving the conversation: TOKI-035 for Details, TOKI-045 for a thread, TOKI-057 for a profile (concern 15). The messages and the composer: TOKI-041 and TOKI-043.
  - The strip 'Offline: showing saved messages' above the list, the loading skeleton and the Retry of a failed first load: TOKI-082. The list has a `notice` slot for the strip (R3).
  - The account card, the Home badge and the other badges: TOKI-099 and TOKI-054. The workspace name and head count under it come from this ticket (R24).
  - The command palette, which also finds conversations: TOKI-071.
- Not in phase 1, so not drawn: the green dot of a Meet in progress (`.live`, Meet is phase 4, D-006), the 'Guest' tag on a direct message (Guest portal is phase 5, D-152), the prototype's pencil icon (D-155) and any control that creates a channel (D-084).

- R2. - R3. `ConversationList` renders a fragment (the `aside.convs` is the shell's, TOKI-015 R3) of, in this order: `div.ch` holding an `h2` with `title` and, only when `browse` is given, an `IconButton` (TOKI-012) with `icon="hash"` and `label` from `browse.label`, size `sm`; the node `notice` when given; `div.cfilter` holding the search field (R9) and the filter chips (R10); and `div.clist` holding the groups (R4), the empty state (R11) and, last, the Browse channels row (R8). `ref` goes to the `div.clist`; `className` and the other attributes of that element pass through. Groups and rows
- R4. `groups` is an array of `{ id, label, collapsed, rows, keepWhenEmpty?, action? }` with `id` one of `conversationGroupIds`. They are drawn in the order of the array. A group is drawn when it has one row or more, or when `keepWhenEmpty` is true; otherwise nothing of it is drawn. A drawn group is `div.cgroup[data-group=<id>]` holding `div.sech` and, when not collapsed, `div.crows` with one row each, in the order of `rows` (the list sorts nothing). The heading is a `button.sec` with `type="button"`, `aria-expanded` equal to `!collapsed`, `aria-controls` the id of `div.crows`, a `DownIcon` and a `span` with `label`; the class `fold` when collapsed (the arrow turns, Table A). A press calls `onToggleGroup(id)`. A collapsed group draws its heading only and no row, so no row of it is in the Tab order. Check: DOM test with three groups, one empty without `keepWhenEmpty` (not drawn), one empty with it (heading only), one collapsed; a press; the keyboard (Enter and Space on the heading).
- R5. A group with `action = { label, onPress }` draws, in `div.sech`, after `button.sec` and not inside it (a button cannot hold a button), an `IconButton` (`icon="plus"`, size `sm`, `label` from `action.label`) with the class `sec-add`. A press calls `action.onPress`. Without `action` no such button is drawn. Check: DOM test; `button.sec-add` has `button.sec` as its previous sibling and no ancestor that is a `button`.
- R6. A row is `ConversationRowData`: `{ id, href, kind, name, isPrivate?, avatar?, unreadCount?, unreadLabel?, hasMention?, mentionLabel?, isMuted?, mutedLabel?, privateLabel?, deactivatedLabel?, draftLabel?, current? }` with `kind` one of `channel`, `dm`, `group_dm` and `avatar` `{ name, hue, presence?, presenceLabel? }`. It renders an `a.cr` with `href`, in this order: `span.gl` holding the glyph (a `HashIcon` for a public channel, a `LockIcon` for a private one, an `Avatar` of TOKI-013 of size 24 with the optional presence for a `dm`, a `UsersIcon` for a `group_dm`); `span.nm` holding `name`, then `span.sr` with `privateLabel` for a private channel, with `mutedLabel` for a muted conversation, and a `Tag` (TOKI-012) with `deactivatedLabel` when given; the `draftLabel` in `span.draft` when given and `current` is not true; a `Badge` of variant `mention` showing `@` with `label` from `mentionLabel` when `hasMention`; a `Badge` showing `unreadCount` with `label` from `unreadLabel` when R7 says so. `current` sets `aria-current="page"`. The class `un` and the class `mutec` are set by R7. A plain left click calls `onOpenRow(row, event)` and cancels the browser's navigation; a click with Ctrl, Meta, Shift or Alt, a middle click and a click when `onOpenRow` is missing are left to the browser, as for the shell's items (TOKI-015 R5). The rows are links so that a conversation can be opened in a new tab and copied as an address (D-089). Check: DOM test for each kind and each optional prop, and click tests with `user-event`, the four modifiers included.
- R7. The look of a row follows the fields and nothing else. A row is unread when `unreadCount` is above 0 and `isMuted` is not true: it gets the class `un` (bold name) and shows the count Badge, as given, with no cap (TOKI-012 concern 4). A muted row (`isMuted`) gets the class `mutec` (grey name) and shows neither bold nor count (D-103). `hasMention` shows the `@` Badge whether the row is muted or not: whether it is set for a muted conversation is the producer's rule (D-088, TOKI-054), not this component's. A count of 0, a negative number, `NaN` or a missing value draws no count. Check: a table test over the eight combinations of unread, muted and mention, and one over 0, -1, `NaN`, 1 and 100.
- R8. The Browse channels row is `button.cr.browse` (`type="button"`) with a `PlusIcon` in a `span.gl` and the text of `browse.label` in a `span.nm`; it is the last child of `div.clist`, after every group and after the empty state; a press calls `browse.onPress`. With no `browse` neither this row nor the header button of R3 is drawn: the component draws no control it was given no handler for. Check: DOM test with and without `browse`.

Search and filters
- R9. `search = { value, label, placeholder, onChange }` draws a `Field` of variant `search` (TOKI-012 R15 (b)) with a `SearchIcon`, `type="search"`, `aria-label` equal to `label`, the placeholder, the controlled `value`, and `onChange` called with the new text on every change. The field is drawn in every look and every width; the Calm look hides it from 720 px by CSS (Table A, concern 1). Check: DOM test; typing three characters calls `onChange` three times with the growing text.
- R10. `filters = { label, value, options, onChange }`, with `options` an array of `{ id, label }`, draws a `ChipGroup` (TOKI-012) named by `label` holding one `Chip` of size `sm` for each option, in order. The chip whose id equals `value` has `aria-pressed="true"` and every other `aria-pressed="false"`; a press on a chip calls `onChange(id)`. The chips show no number: a count on a chip would have to equal the rows shown (D-074) and the prototype has none. Check: DOM test for each value, and a press.
- R11. `empty = { title, description, actionLabel, onAction }` is drawn in `div.clist`, in place of the groups, when the number of rows of all groups is 0. It is an `EmptyState` (TOKI-013, `icon="search"`, `action` a `Button` of size `sm` with `actionLabel` calling `onAction`). When the rows are not 0 it is not drawn. The empty state is centred in the area of `div.clist` above the Browse channels row (D-074, TOKI-015 R15: `div.clist` is a flex column with `min-height:0` and the empty state has `flex:1`). Check: DOM test; the browser test reads that the centre of the empty state's content is within 1 px of the centre of that area, at 360, 720 and 1100 px, in both looks.

Style
- R12. `conversation-list.css` holds exactly the selectors and declarations of Table A, in the media blocks the table names, and no other rule. The media conditions are `(min-width: 720px)`, `(max-width: 719px)` and `(min-width: 720px) and (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`; there is no `@container` query and no `!important`. Every colour is `var(--…)` or `transparent`; no colour literal; every `var(--name)` is one of the 32 properties of TOKI-009 R10. Narrow is the window under 720 px (TOKI-015 R16). Check: a CSS test with `readCss` of TOKI-012 R27, with the table typed in.
- R13. The '+' of R5 is reachable and visible as D-154 says. On a window of 720 px or more with a pointer that hovers (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`) the button has `opacity:0` and takes `opacity:1` while its heading row is hovered or has focus inside (`:hover`, `:focus-within`) and while the button has `:focus-visible`; in every other case (touch, a window under 720 px) it has `opacity:1`. It is never `display:none` or `visibility:hidden`, so it stays in the Tab order and a keyboard user reaches it and sees it. Nothing else of the list is hover-only (D-067): every other `:hover` rule of Table A changes only `background` or `color`. Check: the CSS test of R12 reads the rules; the browser test runs once with a mouse (a window of 1100 px, `hover: hover`) and reads `opacity` before the pointer is over the heading (0), over it (1), and after Tab brings focus to the button (1), and once with touch emulation (`hasTouch`, `isMobile`, a window of 1100 px and one of 360 px) and reads 1 with no pointer over.
- R14. On a window under 720 px, every control the list draws is at least 40 px tall: a row 52 px (the prototype's value), a chip 40 px (TOKI-012 R8), the header button, the heading button `.sec` (40 px; the prototype's 31.4 px is changed here, Table A) and the '+'. At 360 px by 560 px, in both looks, with 40 rows, the list has no horizontal scroll (`scrollWidth` not above `clientWidth` of `div.clist`) and the name of a row is cut with an ellipsis, not wrapped. The wiring in `apps/web`
- R15. `apps/web/src/conversation-list/ConversationListPane.tsx` is the one place that turns the store into `ConversationListProps`. It reads, from the store of TOKI-032, what "What the list reads" (Design) names (`getList` and the lookups) and nothing else; it holds no grouping, sorting, filtering or naming of its own, so the rows come in the order the store gives and a test of the pane with a fake store that returns a fixed order draws that order. The group ids and labels are `starred`, `channels`, `direct` and the texts of R27. A conversation is in exactly one group, the one the store puts it in (a starred channel is in Starred only, F09, D-143). Check: a test with a fake store of 3 groups draws them in order; the same conversation never appears twice.
- R16. The wiring decides which groups are kept when empty. `keepWhenEmpty` is true for the `direct` group when the filter is All and the search is empty, and false in every other case, so a person with no direct message still sees the heading and its '+' (concern 2). Starred and Channels are never kept when empty (F09: an empty group is not shown). Check: a test with a store that has no direct message: the heading is drawn with filter All and an empty search, and not with the filter Unread or with a search typed; a store with only #general draws Channels and no Starred.
- R17. Filter and search. The pane keeps `filter` (`all`, `unread`, `mentions`; default `all`) and `query` (default empty) and asks the store for the list with them (R15). A press on a chip sets the filter; typing sets the query. `empty.onAction` (the button 'Clear filter') sets the filter to `all` and the query to empty. The search is not remembered (R18). Check: component test with a fake store: each chip, typing, the clear button; the empty state appears when the store returns no row and goes when cleared.
- R18. Remembered on the device (F09: "Collapsed groups and the filter are remembered per device"). The pane stores the filter under the key `conversationList.filter` and the collapsed groups under `conversationList.collapsed` in `platform.storage.device` (TOKI-018), as one JSON text each: the filter as the string `all`, `unread` or `mentions`, the collapsed groups as an array of group ids, each with the id of the signed-in person (`{ "userId": …, "value": … }`). On start the pane reads them; a missing key, text that is not valid JSON, a value outside the lists, or a `userId` that is not the signed-in person's gives the default (filter `all`, no group collapsed), so a second person who signs in on the same device does not inherit the first one's. A write that the storage refuses (it returns `false`) leaves the state in memory and shows no error. Check: tests with a fake storage for each case: stored and read back; invalid JSON; unknown value; another person's value; a refusing storage.
- R19. A press on a group heading toggles that group in the collapsed set, writes it (R18) and the list updates at once. A group that is collapsed stays collapsed when a conversation changes or a new one arrives, and when the filter changes. A collapsed group still counts for R11: rows that a collapsed group holds are rows. Check: component test.
- R20. Opening a row. The pane gives `onOpenRow` the router's `navigate({ to: "/c/$conversationId", params })`, a push (not a replace) with `state: { fromList: true }`, so the browser's Back button returns to the place before (D-089). `href` of each row is the same address, `/c/<id>`. The current row (`current`, `aria-current="page"`) is the one whose id is the `$conversationId` of the router's current location; a person at another section has no current row. Check: component test with a memory router: a click navigates and `aria-current` moves; the browser test of R30 presses a row, then Back.
- R21. The route `/c/$conversationId` (child of `appLayoutRoute`, TOKI-015 R30) resolves the id with `hasConversation` of the store. (a) The id is a conversation of the list: the main area draws `ConversationPage` with that conversation, and the id is written as the last viewed (R22). (b) The id is not in the list and never was in this session (a link, an old bookmark, a channel that is not joined or does not exist, an archived channel): the route draws `UnjoinedChannel` of TOKI-034 (R16 there) with `channelId` the id; `onJoined(id)` needs nothing from the route, because the store now holds the conversation and the route draws (a); `onMember(detail)` calls `refresh()` of the store once and, when the store then holds the conversation, the route draws (a), and otherwise (an archived channel that the person belongs to) draws the not-found view of TOKI-006 until TOKI-101 decides (concern 13); `onNotFound()` draws the not-found view of TOKI-006 (later the shared page of TOKI-082, D-131). No message of a conversation that is not in the list is requested by this route (D-116). (c) The id was in the list during this session and no longer is (the person left it, it was archived, the person was removed): the route replaces itself with the address of the fallback of R22 and shows no error (F23: "the view moves to #general"). The route draws nothing while the store's `status` is `idle` or `loading`, and does not redirect then. `ConversationPage({ conversationId, onBack })` is a placeholder: it draws one element in `main` with `data-testid="conversation-page"` and the conversation's name in an `h1`; TOKI-035 (the header and the Details panel), TOKI-041 and TOKI-043 replace its body and keep its file, its props and its test id. `onBack` is given by the route: when the current history entry has `state.fromList` true (set by R20) it calls `history.back()`, otherwise it navigates to `/` with `replace: true`, so the Back button of the header and the browser's Back end on the list on a narrow screen and never leave the app for a conversation reached from the list. Check: tests with a memory router and a fake store for (a), (b) with each of the three callbacks, (c), a store that has not loaded, and both ways of `onBack`.
- R22. Last viewed and the opening address. `apps/web/src/conversation-list/last-viewed.ts` stores the id under `conversationList.lastViewed` in `platform.storage.device`, as `{ "userId", "value" }` (R18's rules for a missing, invalid or another person's value). The fallback, used by R21 (c) and R23, is the stored id when it is a conversation of the list, otherwise the conversation #general (`getGeneralId()` of the store, the entry with `isGeneral`), and #general is always in the list (D-034). Check: unit tests: the stored id in the list; the stored id not in the list (left, archived) gives #general; nothing stored gives #general; another person's value gives #general; a refusing storage leaves #general.
- R23. The index route `/`. On a window of 720 px or more it replaces itself with `/c/<fallback>` (R22) once the store has loaded, and shows nothing until then. On a window under 720 px it does not redirect: Home is the list (journeys F08, F38; D-035 says the app opens on the conversation last viewed, F08 gives the narrow case): the shell shows the list pane (`derivePane`, TOKI-015 R4, with `chatOpen` false). A window that changes from under 720 px to 720 px or more while at `/` redirects at that moment; from 720 px or more to under, at `/c/<id>`, stays on the conversation. It replaces the page of TOKI-015 R30 that showed "Toki" in `data-testid="app-root"`, so the sample test of TOKI-008 R11 and the browser test of TOKI-015 R30 are changed by this ticket to stub the answers of `GET /api/v1/auth/session` and `GET /api/v1/bootstrap` (R30). Check: component tests with `useNarrow` stubbed and a fake store; the browser test of R30 at 1100 px (arrives at `/c/<general id>`) and at 360 px (stays at `/` and shows the list).
- R24. `AppShellRoute` (TOKI-015 R30) is changed here. (a) It renders `ConversationListPane` as the `list` of `AppShell` for every section (the Calm sidebar shows it on every section, the Focus layout on Home only, TOKI-015 Table F). (b) It sets `chatOpen` to true when the path starts with `/c/`. (c) It sets `workspace` to `{ name, detail }` from `getWorkspace()` and `getPeople()` of the store: `name` the workspace name and `detail` the head count text of R27 for the number of people whose `status` is `active` (concern 8); with `status` `idle` or `loading` it sets no `workspace`. (d) It reads the side panel from the slots of R26. (e) It runs the store's life (R32). Until the store is `ready` the pane draws its header, the search field and the chips, and `div.clist` with `aria-busy="true"` and no row; when `status` is `failed` it draws the same and no row (the skeleton and the Retry are TOKI-082). Check: component test of the layout route with a fake store: before the load, after, with a deactivated person in `getPeople()` (not counted); `chatOpen` at `/` and at `/c/x`.
- R25. Opening Browse channels and the people picker. The pane draws the header button and the Browse channels row (R8) and the '+' of the Direct messages heading (R5), and holds the open state of the two dialogs of TOKI-034: `BrowseChannelsDialog({ open, onClose, onOpenConversation, returnFocusRef })` opened by the header button and the Browse channels row, and `NewMessageDialog({ open, onClose, onOpenConversation, returnFocusRef })` opened by the '+'. `returnFocusRef` is a ref to the control that opened the dialog (the one pressed, so focus returns to it, TOKI-034 R25); `onClose` closes the dialog; `onOpenConversation(id)` navigates to `/c/<id>` with a push, as R20, and closes the dialog. Check: component test with the dialogs replaced by stand-ins that record their props: each of the three controls opens the right one, `returnFocusRef.current` is the pressed control, `onOpenConversation` navigates and closes, and a second press after closing opens again.
- R26. The side panel slot. `apps/web/src/shell/shell-slots.tsx` exports `ShellSlotsProvider` (mounted by `AppShellRoute` around the shell) and `useShellPanel(panel)`, where `panel` is `null` or `{ label, content }` (`label` the accessible name of the panel, `content` the node, normally a `SidePanel` of TOKI-015 R11). A route or page that calls it sets the panel for as long as it is mounted and clears it when it unmounts or when it passes `null`; the last call wins. `AppShellRoute` gives the value to `AppShell` as `panel` and `panelLabel` (TOKI-015 R3). - R27. Texts. `apps/web` translates the pane with `useTranslation` of `@toki/i18n/react`. The title of the pane and the name of the landmark use the existing key `nav.conversations` (the name of a place lives in `nav`, TOKI-017 rule 3). This ticket adds the namespace `conversationList`, one file per language, with the keys of Table B, and the check `pnpm --filter @toki/i18n check` still prints `no problem`. A row's labels (`unreadLabel`, `mentionLabel`, `mutedLabel`, `privateLabel`, `deactivatedLabel`) and the head count are translated in the wiring and passed down (R2). Check: the i18n check; a test renders the pane in `en` and in `vi` and reads the three group headings, the chip names and the search placeholder; a test that no key of Table B is missing in a language.
- R28. A person's avatar colour. `apps/web/src/people/person-hue.ts` exports `personHue(personId)`, a pure function that returns an integer from 0 to 359 and the same integer for the same id every time, on every device (FNV-1a 32-bit over the UTF-8 bytes of the lower-cased id, modulo 360). The direct message row uses it for the other person's avatar. Other screens use the same function (the people picker of TOKI-034, the account card, the profile): this ticket builds it, as the first to need it. Check: unit tests: the same id twice gives the same number; the result is an integer in 0..359 for 1000 random ids; two ids that differ only in letter case give the same number; the function has a fixed expected output for three ids.
- R29. The list follows the store. A conversation that arrives in the store (a direct message started from the people picker, a channel joined from Browse channels, #general after sign-in) appears in the list without a reload, in the group and place the store gives; one that leaves it (left, archived, removed) disappears; a change of a person's star or mute moves the row. The pane subscribes to the store through one hook and re-renders only when the list it asked for changes. ### The routes and the shell
- The routes are code-defined (TOKI-006 R3): `src/routes/conversation.tsx` exports `conversationRoute` (path `/c/$conversationId`, parent `appLayoutRoute`) and `src/routes/index.tsx` keeps the index route with the component of R23. TOKI-045 adds `/c/$conversationId/t/$messageId` as a child of the first one; nothing here blocks it.
- `ConversationListPane` is rendered once by `AppShellRoute` and stays mounted while the person moves between sections and conversations, so the scroll position of `div.clist`, the search text and the filter survive a move to Activity and back, on a wide and on a narrow screen (on a narrow screen the pane is hidden by `visibility`, not unmounted, TOKI-015 R22). A reload starts with the remembered filter and groups (R18) and an empty search.
- On a narrow screen a press on a row pushes `/c/<id>`; the shell derives `data-chat-open` from the path (R24) and shows the main pane; the header's Back button (TOKI-035) and the browser's Back both return to `/`, which shows the list (R23). On a window of 720 px or more `/` never stays on screen: it is replaced at once (R23), so Back from the second conversation opened goes to the first, not to a blank `/`.
- `ConversationPage` is the seam for TOKI-035, TOKI-041 and TOKI-043: they fill it, they do not move the route. TOKI-035 adds the search parameter `panel` to the route (its R8).

### Where each choice is made
- Decided in this spec (technical): the list is a presentational component in `ui-web` and the wiring is in `apps/web`, as for the shell (TOKI-015, "Where the shell gets its data"), so the list is drawn in the preview and the fixtures without a server; rows are links; a heading and its '+' are siblings; `personHue` is FNV-1a over the id (any fixed function would do, this one is short, has no dependency and is the same on a phone); the device's memory is two JSON values with the person's id inside them (R18); the shell slots (R26) so that a route can give the shell its panel without the shell knowing routes.
- No virtualisation: all conversations are in the list in phase 1 and D-117 says to revisit past 500. Decided in this spec: the rows are plain elements; a list of 500 conversations (the size of TOKI-031 R9) renders in the unit test of R15 with no error, and the first measurement against D-113's targets (switching to an opened channel under 100 ms, 60 frames per second scrolling) is taken in the browser test with the 500-row fixture and recorded in the pull request, not asserted (concern 17).
