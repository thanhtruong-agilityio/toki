# Entry points on narrow screens design

Date: 2026-11-26 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/narrow-entry-points.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-092

## Goal
A person on a phone needs the same reach as a person on a laptop: search, People, Preferences, Admin (for an Admin), Set status, Pause notifications, Focus time and Sign out (D-053, D-067). Under 720 px the shell draws neither the sidebar nor the account card (TOKI-015 Table G), and it draws the More tab only when it is given a sheet (TOKI-015 R6), so today none of these has an entry point on a phone.
After this change `apps/web` gives the shell a More tab and a sheet. The sheet lists Search, the sections of the shell marked `more`, and an account section made of the entries that the account menu registry holds. A chosen row closes the sheet first and then does its work, so focus is back on the More tab before the bar can be hidden (GAP-39).

## Scope
- In:
  - `apps/web/src/shell/MoreSheet.tsx`, `use-more-sheet.ts`, `more-rows.ts` and `more-sheet.css`: the sheet, the state of the More tab, the list of rows and its styles.
  - The edit of `apps/web/src/routes/app-layout.tsx` that passes `moreTab` to `AppShell` and mounts the sheet beside `AccountHost` (R1).
  - One edit to `apps/web/src/account/account-entries.tsx` (TOKI-099): a registered item carries the id it was registered under (R10).
  - `e2e/more-sheet.spec.ts` and the helper `e2e/support/more-sheet.ts` that the phone runs of other browser tests use (R15, R16).
- Out (not now):
  - The state and the commands behind the rows: Set status (TOKI-056), Pause and Focus time (TOKI-076), the palette (TOKI-071), Sign out (TOKI-099). The sheet adds no state and no request for them.
  - The Sheet layer itself, its focus rules and its look (TOKI-014), and the More tab (TOKI-015). The People and Preferences pages and their section rows (TOKI-079, TOKI-078); the Admin page (TOKI-025).
  - The search field at the top of the narrow Home list (TOKI-071 R15 b) and the account card of the wide layouts.
  - The twelve end-to-end flows (TOKI-090): they call the helper of R15; their steps are theirs.
  - Rows for Later and Files (they are tabs of the bar, TOKI-015 R6), Calendar, Tasks, Mail and Meet (later phases, TOKI-015 Scope).
  - A badge on the More tab (TOKI-015 R6: none until a screen gives one).
  - The More tab while a chat is open: the shell hides the bar then (journey F38, TOKI-015 R22), and Back returns to the list.

## Requirements
- R1. Wiring. `ShellBody` in `apps/web/src/routes/app-layout.tsx` calls `useMoreSheet()` (R9) and, while a person is signed in, passes `AppShell` `moreTab = { label: t("nav.more"), open, onOpen, ref }` and renders `<MoreSheet />` as a child of the shell next to `AccountHost`, so the sheet is inside the shell's toast provider, as `AccountHost` is. With no signed-in user it passes no `moreTab`, so the bar has no dead tab. Pressing the More tab sets `open` to true and the sheet shows. Check: a render of the real `AppShellRoute` with a signed-in Member at a narrow `matchMedia` finds `button.nt` named "More" with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`; a press makes `role="dialog"` named "More" appear and the button's `aria-expanded` read `true`; with the state `signedOut` there is no More button.
- R2. The sheet. `MoreSheet` draws the `Sheet` of TOKI-014 with `title` `t("nav.more")` (the prototype's title, line 2155), `closeLabel` `t("common.close")`, `returnFocusRef` the ref of the More tab, and `onRequestClose` that closes it for each reason (`escape`, `outside`, `close`); there is no form guard, the sheet holds no typed text. It draws no row other than those of R3. Check: a render with a stand-in list finds the dialog named "More"; Esc, a press outside and a press on the close button each close it and put focus on the More button; the sheet holds no `input`.
- R3. The rows and their order. `buildMoreRows(sections, entries)` in `more-rows.ts` is a pure function that returns, in this order: (a) one `search` row; (b) one `link` row for each section of `sections` that has `more: true`, in the order of the list; (c) the account rows, kept apart from (a) and (b) as one group named by `t("account.menu.label")` (R13). With the list of TOKI-015 and the rows of TOKI-079, TOKI-025 and TOKI-099 that gives Search, People, Admin, Preferences, then the account rows. A section with `tab: true` and no `more` (Home, Activity, Later, Files) has no row. `MoreSheet` is given the list `ShellBody` already builds for the shell (`navSections`, which `visibleSections(role)` has filtered), so it reads no second source. Check: a table test of `buildMoreRows` with the rows of the list in order, the tab-only rows and a row added by a stand-in later ticket (`more: true`): the ids come out in the order above and the tab-only rows are absent.
- R4. Admin for an Admin only. The sheet shows the Admin row only when the list it receives holds it, and `visibleSections` gives the row `roles: ["admin"]` (TOKI-015 R36), so a Member's sheet has no Admin row and no entry for it. Check: a render of the real `AppShellRoute` with a Member and then an Admin finds no "Admin" button and then one; the Member's sheet lists exactly Search, People, Preferences and the account rows.
- R5. Search. The `search` row has the icon `search` and the label `t("nav.search")` (the shell's own word). Choosing it closes the sheet and then calls `open({ returnFocusRef })` of `useOpenPalette()` (TOKI-071 R1) with the ref of the More tab. The sheet keeps no palette state. Check: with a stand-in `useOpenPalette` that records, choosing Search records one call whose `returnFocusRef` is the More tab's ref and, at that moment, no `role="dialog"` is in the document.
- R6. Pages. A `link` row has the icon and label of its section (`row.icon`, `t(row.labelKey)`, the same values the sidebar draws) and is a `button` with `aria-current="page"` when its section is the current one. Choosing it closes the sheet and then calls `router.navigate({ to: row.to })`. There is no Home row, so the Home rule of the shell's `onNavigate` (TOKI-033 R23) does not apply. Check: choosing People, Admin and Preferences each ends on `/people`, `/admin` and `/prefs` in a memory router, and on `/people` the People row alone has `aria-current="page"`.
- R7. The account rows. `MoreSheet` reads `useAccountEntries()` of TOKI-099 R3 and draws, in the order the registry gives, every entry except the one whose id is `preferences` (R10, concern 2): an item as one row with its `icon`, `label`, `variant` and `disabled`; a group as a heading with its `heading` text and one row for each of its items, each with its `icon`, `label` and `disabled`. Choosing a row closes the sheet and then calls the item's `onSelect` with the click event of the press; this holds for Set status (TOKI-056 R16), the two groups of TOKI-076 R17 (Pause options, "Resume now", the Focus options, "End focus now") and every entry a later ticket registers. An entry that no ticket has registered has no row, so there is no dead row. Check: with the registry holding stand-ins for `setStatus`, a `pause` group of two options, a `focus` group of two options, `preferences` and `signOut`, the account rows are Set status, the heading "Pause notifications" with its two options, the heading "Focus time" with its two options, and Sign out, with no second Preferences row; choosing an option calls its `onSelect` once with a click event; with only `preferences` and `signOut` registered the account rows are Sign out alone.
- R8. Sign out keeps the sheet open while it runs. The `signOut` entry is the one row that does not close the sheet first: choosing it calls its `onSelect` at once, and while `useSigningOut()` of TOKI-099 R7 is true the row is disabled, has `aria-busy="true"` and reads `t("account.signingOut")`; when the call ends in `"done"` the layout is unmounted by the guard and the sheet with it; when it ends in `"failed"` or `"blocked"` the row has its label again, the sheet is still open and the toast or the explanation of TOKI-099 R7 and TOKI-093 R17 shows above it. The reason is that the wait of TOKI-099 R7 is shown on the card, which a phone does not draw, so a sheet that closed first would show nothing for up to 20 seconds (concern 3). Check: with a stand-in `signOut` whose promise the test settles: a press calls it once and the sheet stays; the row reads "Signing out…" and is disabled; settled with `"failed"` the row reads "Sign out" and the sheet is open; a second press while pending calls nothing more.
- R9. Close first, then run. `useMoreSheet()` in `use-more-sheet.ts` holds `open`, the ref of the More tab and `choose(run)`. `choose` stores `run`, sets `open` to false and returns; an effect of the hook's host runs the stored function once, after the commit in which the sheet has closed, so the Sheet has unmounted its dialog and given focus back (TOKI-014 R13, R33) before `run` starts, and a dialog or palette that `run` opens finds no other layer open and records the More tab as its opener. `run` is never called while the sheet is open, and never twice for one choice. Check: a test whose `run` records `document.querySelector('[role="dialog"]')` and `document.activeElement` finds `null` and the More button; two rapid presses on one row call `run` once.
- R10. One registry id for each item. In `apps/web/src/account/account-entries.tsx`, the entry the registry keeps for a registered item gets `id` equal to the id it was registered under when the caller gave none (`{ ...current, id: current.id ?? id }`), so `useAccountEntries()` returns items that can be told apart; a group already carries the `id` its caller gave. The menu of TOKI-099 R5 is unchanged. This is the only edit to a file of another ticket besides R1, and the pull request names it. Check: the registry test of TOKI-099 gains a case in which `useAccountEntries()` returns `preferences` and `signOut` items with those ids; the menu's tests still pass.
- R11. The window grows. While the sheet is open and `useNarrow()` of TOKI-015 turns false (the window reaches 720 px), the sheet closes, with no row run and no focus given to the More tab, which is no longer visible. Check: a render with a controllable `matchMedia` opens the sheet, turns the query false and finds no dialog and no call of any row.
- R12. Touch and size. Every row is a `button` that is at least 48 px high (the prototype's `min-height:48px`, line 2155) and as wide as the sheet's body, with the icon, the label in a `span` that grows, and the text left-aligned; a group heading is a block of 12 px text, weight 600, colour `--muted` (the prototype's heading of its lists, line 2150), not a control. The account group is separated from the rows above it by a 1 px `--line` rule. No rule hides or shows a row on hover or focus. The sheet holds its own scroll when it is taller than the window less 48 px (TOKI-014 Sheet), so at 360 by 560 px with an Admin and the registry of TOKI-056 and TOKI-076 complete (eleven rows and two headings) the last row can be reached by scrolling. All colours and lengths come from tokens, no literal colour. Check: the browser test of R16 reads the rectangles.
- R13. Texts. The sheet adds no text and no key. It uses `nav.more`, `nav.search`, `nav.people`, `nav.preferences`, `nav.admin`, `common.close`, `account.menu.label` (the accessible name of the account group, `aria-label` on a `div role="group"`), `account.signingOut` and the labels and headings that the entries bring, so each already has its Vietnamese. `ui-web` is not edited. Check: `pnpm i18n:check` exits 0 with no change to a catalog; a search of the three new files for a quoted English word that is not a key finds none; a render in Vietnamese shows the Vietnamese of each word of this list.
- R14. Nothing else is owned here. The sheet makes no request, holds no store and starts no timer. It never reads the status, the Pause or the Focus state: their rows are the registry's. Check: a search of the new files for `fetch`, `client`, `setTimeout` and `timers` finds none.
- R15. A helper for the browser tests. `e2e/support/more-sheet.ts` exports `openMoreSheet(page)`, which presses the More tab and waits for the dialog named "More", and `chooseInMoreSheet(page, name)`, which presses the row with that accessible name and waits for the dialog to go (except for Sign out, whose sheet stays until the page changes). The phone runs of the end-to-end flows (TOKI-090) call these two functions for Search, Preferences, Admin and Sign out and write no selector of their own. Check: the helper's own test runs both against the page of R16.
- R16. ## Design
### The sheet, row by row
| Row | Source | Icon | On choose |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Search | fixed | `search` | close, then `open({ returnFocusRef })` of the palette |
| People, Admin (Admins), Preferences | `navSections` with `more: true` | the section's | close, then `router.navigate({ to })` |
| Set status | registry `setStatus` | `smile` (TOKI-056) | close, then its `onSelect` (opens the status dialog) |
| Pause notifications: options, Resume now | registry `pause` group | `bell-off`, `bell` (TOKI-076) | close, then its `onSelect` (online gate, command, toast) |
| Focus time: options, End focus now | registry `focus` group | `bolt` (TOKI-076) | close, then its `onSelect` |
| Sign out | registry `signOut` | none (TOKI-099 concern 9) | its `onSelect` at once; the sheet stays until the page changes |

The registry's `preferences` entry has no row, because the Preferences row of the section list is the same page (concern 2).

### How the pieces meet
- `ShellBody` already holds `navSections`, the router and the signed-in user. It calls `useMoreSheet()` and gives the shell the More tab and the sheet the list. Nothing in `ui-web` changes: the shell's `moreTab` and `NavSection.more` were made for this (TOKI-015 R6, R9).
- `useMoreSheet()` returns `{ open, setOpen, tabRef, choose }`. The More tab's ref is the one `moreTab.ref` carries and the one the Sheet gets as `returnFocusRef`, because Safari and Firefox on macOS do not focus a button that is pressed with a finger or a mouse (TOKI-014 R13). It also watches `useNarrow()` (R11).
- `choose` stores a function and closes; the effect that runs it is in the host component, which renders after the Sheet's own effects in one commit. This is the order the palette uses for its own choices (TOKI-071 R9), so a dialog opened by a row, the status dialog or the palette, records the More tab as its opener and returns focus there.
- Rows are built in `more-rows.ts` from plain data and drawn by `MoreSheet.tsx`, so the order, the Member and Admin cases and the Preferences rule are tested without a DOM.
- The sheet sits inside `AppShell`, as a child, so a toast that a row causes (the pause started, the offline explanation) is drawn by the shell's provider above the bottom bar (TOKI-015 R23).

### Layout
```
apps/web/src/shell/
  MoreSheet.tsx       the sheet and its rows
  use-more-sheet.ts   open state, the tab ref, choose, the narrow watch
  more-rows.ts        buildMoreRows
  more-sheet.css      .msheet-row, .msheet-head, .msheet-rule
apps/web/src/routes/app-layout.tsx        moreTab and <MoreSheet /> (R1)
apps/web/src/account/account-entries.tsx  the id of a registered item (R10)
e2e/more-sheet.spec.ts, e2e/support/more-sheet.ts
```

## Areas of concern
1. The order of People, Admin and Preferences. The plan line and journey F38 list "Search, People, Preferences, Admin"; the shell's section list, which the prototype's own list follows (line 1199: people, admin, prefs) and which TOKI-079 and TOKI-099 insert their rows into, has People, Admin, Preferences. Decided in this spec: the sheet draws the `more` rows in the order of that list (R3). Reason: the sheet then has no list of its own to keep in step with the sidebar and the rail, a row added later by a ticket lands in one place for all three, and the journey names the members of the sheet, not an order that a test could check against a decision. If the owner wants Preferences before Admin, `buildMoreRows` sorts by a fixed list, one function and its table test change. Owner: Thanh Truong.
2. Preferences twice. The account menu of TOKI-099 has a Preferences entry (R4 there) and the sheet has a Preferences section row. Decided in this spec: the sheet draws the section row and skips the registry entry whose id is `preferences` (R7, R10), so the page is reached by one row, and the person never sees two rows with one word that lead to one page. To tell the entry apart the registry stamps the id on a registered item (R10); the alternative, a filter on the label, would break in Vietnamese. Owner: Thanh Truong.
3. Sign out and the closing rule. TOKI-099 concern 12 says the sheet closes when an entry is chosen, before the call, so that focus is back on the More tab before the bar hides; the plan line of this ticket says the sheet closes when a destination is chosen. Sign out is not a destination, the bar disappears with the layout when it succeeds, and TOKI-099 R7 shows the wait (up to 20 seconds) on the account card, which a phone does not draw. Decided in this spec: Sign out is the one row that calls its handler without closing the sheet and shows "Signing out…" on its own row (R8); every other row closes first (R9). It is the only place where this spec reads a word of TOKI-099 narrowly, and the pull request says so. If the owner wants the sheet closed first for Sign out too, the phone needs another place for the wait, for example a toast, and R8 is replaced by the close-first rule. Owner: Thanh Truong.
4. A sheet that outlives the narrow layout. The Sheet layer stays open if the window grows (TOKI-014 Sheet: its owner closes it). Decided in this spec: the sheet closes at 720 px (R11), because a sheet at the foot of a laptop window has no tab that opens it and no way to tell what it is for. Focus is not given back, since the More tab is not visible. Owner: Thanh Truong.
5. Who is signed in. The phone draws no account card, so nothing on a phone shows the person's name or current status; Set status opens a dialog that shows the current text, but a person who has set one and wants to read it has no place to look. The log (D-053, F38) lists the rows of the sheet and names no identity line. Not decided. Proposal, not a decision: the account group starts with one non-pressable line with the avatar, the name and `accountDetail` of TOKI-099 R2 (the status, otherwise the role), the same data the card draws, no new text; it adds one row of 56 px and one test, and R3 gains a `person` row. Without it the sheet is as R3 says. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
6. The Back button with the sheet open. The Sheet layer does not handle Back (TOKI-014 concern 2), the shell does not either (TOKI-015 concern 4), and this ticket adds no history entry for the sheet. On a phone the browser's Back from `/` with the sheet open leaves the page or goes to the previous address and the sheet is gone. Not decided; the log has D-089 for threads and says nothing about a sheet. Proposal, not a decision: leave it so, as for every layer, and decide once for all layers in TOKI-014's question. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
7. Plan changes this spec asks for, for the lead to apply (no other spec is edited here). Decided in this spec: the needs line of TOKI-092 (`TOKI-015, TOKI-056, TOKI-071, TOKI-076`) also gains TOKI-014 (the Sheet), TOKI-099 (the registry and Sign out), TOKI-017 (keys) and TOKI-008 (the browser runner); TOKI-079 and TOKI-078 are not needs, because the sheet draws the rows of the list that exist, but the phone checks of R16 for People and Preferences need their rows and wait for them. TOKI-090 needs TOKI-092 (its spec says so). TOKI-099 R3 and R4 are unchanged except R10 above. TOKI-071 R1 already gives `open({ returnFocusRef })`, TOKI-056 R16 the Set status entry and TOKI-076 R17 the two groups, so nothing is asked of those three. Owner: Thanh Truong.
