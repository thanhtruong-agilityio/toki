# Browse channels and the people picker design

Date: 2026-11-17 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/browse-channels-people-picker.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-034

## Goal
A person needs two screens that the conversation list only points at: one to find and join a channel, and one to start a direct message or a group. This work builds both as dialogs of TOKI-014, the view that a person sees when they reach a channel they have not joined, and the small chip that shows a chosen person. The channel rules (who sees which channel, what a non-member may read, the limit of 8 people in a group) are enforced by the server; the screens show what the server answers and refuse the ninth person before asking.

## Scope
- In:
  - `BrowseChannelsDialog`: search, the first 50 channels and more on scroll, name, topic and member count, "Join" or "Open", the "Archived" label, the empty state, the join.
  - `UnjoinedChannel`: the view of a public channel the person has not joined (name, topic, member count, "Join"), for an address that is not in the person's list.
  - `PeoplePicker` (search, chips, the list of people) and `NewMessageDialog` (the picker in a dialog with "Start"), with the form guard of TOKI-093.
  - `PersonChip` in `packages/ui-web`, with its CSS, tests and examples.
  - The CSS of the rows and lists of the two dialogs, and the texts of both in English and Vietnamese (namespaces `browse` and `picker`).
  - The one file in `apps/web` that reads the client and the store of TOKI-032 (R2).
- Out (not now):
  - The "Browse channels" row and the "+" on "Direct messages", the state that opens the dialogs, the route and address of a conversation, and what happens after `onOpenConversation`: TOKI-033. This ticket gives the dialogs and the view; TOKI-033 places them (Design, "Hand-offs").
  - The API client, the store and the people list: TOKI-032, from the answers of TOKI-029, TOKI-030 and TOKI-031. The server rules (who is listed, the limit, the key of a direct message): TOKI-029, TOKI-030.
  - The "Restore" button of an Admin beside an archived channel (F26): TOKI-101. How a member opens an archived channel that is not in their list (it is not in the bootstrap list, TOKI-031 R6): TOKI-101 (concern 2).
  - Creating a channel (Admin page, TOKI-098), adding people to an existing channel (TOKI-100): they may reuse `PeoplePicker` and `PersonChip`; this ticket builds neither screen.
  - The shared page for 403 and 404, loading skeletons, the Retry of a request with no answer after 10 seconds, the offline banner: TOKI-082. Until it lands these dialogs show the plain states of R10 and R14.
  - Presence dots and job titles in the people list: the bootstrap call carries neither (TOKI-031 R12); TOKI-055 adds the dot.
  - The command palette (TOKI-071), which can open Browse channels with a search text (R4).

Browse channels
- R4. `BrowseChannelsDialog({ open, onClose, onOpenConversation, returnFocusRef?, initialSearch? })` renders one `Modal` (TOKI-014 form (a)) with `title` `t("browse.title")`, `closeLabel` `t("browse.close.label")` and `returnFocusRef`, and in its body one `div.field` that holds one `input` with `aria-label` and `placeholder` `t("browse.search.label")`, `maxLength` 80 and the attribute `data-autofocus`, and below it the list of R6 to R10. `onOpenConversation(id)` is called by R11 and R12 and is supplied by TOKI-033. The dialog never closes itself: it calls `onClose()` for a close request of every reason (`"escape"`, `"outside"`, `"close"`), because a search text is not form content (concern 4, D-070, TOKI-093 concern 5), and after a join or an Open. `initialSearch` (default empty) is the starting text of the input, for the palette (TOKI-071). The input is a `type="search"`; it has no `form`.
- R5. Request. When the dialog opens, and each time the search text changes after 250 ms without a keystroke, it asks for the first page of `GET /api/v1/channels` through `api.channels.list({ search, limit: 50, cursor })` (Table N), with `search` the text trimmed of white space and left out when it is empty, and `limit` the value `CHANNEL_BROWSE_PAGE_DEFAULT` of `@toki/shared` (50, D-109, TOKI-029 R17). A new search replaces the list: the rows of the old text are not shown beside the new ones, and the answer of an earlier text that arrives after a later one is ignored. Reopening the dialog asks again: nothing is kept between openings (the Query settings `gcTime: 0` and `staleTime: 0` for this query). Decided in this spec: 250 ms, and TanStack Query's `useInfiniteQuery` (the client of TOKI-006) to page; the tests check the requests made and the rows shown, not the mechanism.
- R6. Rows. One `li.mrow` for each channel, in the order the server returns (by name in byte order, TOKI-029 R6), holding in this order: `span.gl` with the `Icon` `lock` for a private channel and `hash` for a public one; `span.grow` with `b` the channel name and, below it, `span.xs.muted.sub` with the topic (when it is not null) joined by " · " to the member count `t("browse.row.members", { count })`, and for a private channel the word `t("browse.row.private")` first, also joined by " · "; the label of R7 when the channel is archived; then the button of R7. The rows show only what the server sends for a person who is not a member of a public channel: name, topic and member count (D-116, TOKI-029 R7); no row shows a message, a description or a creator. The row has no `title` and no hover-only part.
- R7. The action of a row is `browseRowAction(channel)` in `browseRows.ts`, a pure function: `isMember` true gives `"open"` (also when archived: only members can open an archived channel, F26); `isMember` false and `archived` false gives `"join"`; `isMember` false and `archived` true gives `"none"` (the server refuses the join of an archived channel with 409, TOKI-029 R10). `"open"` renders a `Button` of TOKI-012 with `size="sm"` and `t("browse.row.open")`; `"join"` renders one with `size="sm"`, `variant="primary"` and `t("browse.row.join")`; `"none"` renders no button. An archived channel renders `Tag` of TOKI-012 with `t("browse.row.archived")` in front of the button (the label is the journey's wording, F21, F26; the choice of `Tag` is decided here). A test covers the six combinations of `isMember`, `archived` and `isPrivate` and the markup of each.
- R8. More on scroll. The list container `div.mlist` scrolls. When its `scrollTop + clientHeight` is within 48 px of its `scrollHeight`, or focus enters the last row, and the last answer has a `nextCursor` that is not null and no page is being asked, the next page is asked with that cursor (`useMoreOnScroll`). A page is never asked twice for one cursor. With `nextCursor` null nothing is asked. The rows of all pages are shown in order, each channel once. While a later page is asked the container has `aria-busy="true"`.
- R9. Empty. When the first page of a search has no channel, the list container holds one `EmptyState` of TOKI-013 with the `Icon` `hash`, `title` `t("browse.empty")` and `description` an empty string (TOKI-013 R15 requires the prop; the message is the title alone). The container then has the class `is-empty` (Table A) so that the message is centred in the list (D-074). A count is never shown beside the search. A test searches for `zzzz` with an answer of no channels and checks the message, the class, and that the empty block's centre is within 1 px of the container's centre in a browser (R27).
- R10. Loading and failure. While the first page of a search is on its way, the container has `aria-busy="true"` and holds one `p.muted` with `t("browse.loading")`. When the first page fails (any status, or no answer), it holds `p.muted` with `t("browse.error.load")` and a `Button` of `size="sm"` with `t("browse.error.retry")` that asks again; the dialog stays open and the search input keeps its text. A failure of a later page keeps the rows already shown and shows the same line and button after them. TOKI-082 replaces these plain states with its skeleton and its Retry (D-126).
- R11. Join. Pressing "Join" does, in this order: (1) `ensureOnline()` of TOKI-093 R17; when it returns `false` nothing is sent, the dialog and the row stay and the explanation toast of that helper is the only feedback; (2) while the request runs every "Join" button of the dialog has `disabled` and the pressed one has `aria-busy="true"` (D-111); (3) `conversations.joinChannel(id)` (Table N, `POST /api/v1/channels/{id}/join`), which stores the entry when the answer comes (R12); (4) on 200, whether `changed` is true or false, `onClose()` is called, then `onOpenConversation(id)`, then the toast `t("browse.joined", { name })` is shown with `show` of TOKI-013 (the prototype's "Joined #name", line 2311; no Undo: the log gives the join none). If the dialog was closed before the answer came, the store is still updated (by the command) and the toast still shown, and `onOpenConversation` is not called (the person may have gone elsewhere). A test closes the dialog while the request is held and checks all three.
- R12. The entry of a joined channel and of a new direct conversation is made by the store's commands `joinChannel(id)` and `openDirect(userIds)` (TOKI-032 R15, with the merge rule of its R13: a new channel entry has `kind` `channel`, `memberIds` null, the level `mentions` that `effectiveNotificationLevel` gives, no mute and no star, D-046). No file of this change builds a conversation entry: a test reads the files of `apps/web/src/features` (test files excepted) and fails on an object with a key `notificationLevel` or `memberIds`. After a join the conversation is in the store (`hasConversation(id)`), which is what lets TOKI-033 show it.
- R13. Join and Open failures. "Open" asks nothing: it calls `onClose()` and then `onOpenConversation(id)`. A join that fails any other way (a status other than 200 and 409, or no answer) shows one line `t("common.error.actionFailed")` (TOKI-093 R24) with `role="alert"` above the list, keeps the dialog and the rows, and enables the buttons. `classifyApiError` of TOKI-032 R8 says which screen D-131 leads to; the shared page that D-131 gives 403 and 404 (`no_access`) is TOKI-082's, and until it lands these statuses show the same line (concern 6).
- R14. Nothing creates a channel (D-084). With a session of an Admin and of a Member, the dialog's only buttons are the close button, the row buttons of R7 and the Retry of R10; no element holds the words of a create action and no source file of the dialog calls a create request. A test lists every `button` and `a` of the open dialog for both roles and compares it with that list, and a test reads the files of `features/channels` and fails on `createChannel` and `POST` to `/channels` without a path after it.
- R15. - R16. `UnjoinedChannel({ channelId, onJoined, onMember, onNotFound })` is the view of a channel the person has not joined, for a conversation address that is not in the person's list (TOKI-033 decides when it is shown, Design, "Hand-offs"). It asks `api.channels.get(channelId)` (`GET /api/v1/channels/{id}`, TOKI-029 R7) and, for the answer: (a) a `ChannelSummary` with `isMember` false and `archived` false: it renders `h2` with the icon of R6 and the name, `p` with the topic when it is not null, `p.muted` with the member count `t("browse.row.members", { count })`, and a primary `Button` `t("browse.row.join")` that follows R11 steps 1 to 3, calls `onJoined(id)` after the store is updated, and shows the toast of R11; (b) a summary with `isMember` false and `archived` true: the same without the button and with the label of R7; (c) `isMember` true (the person joined elsewhere, or is a member of an archived channel): nothing of the channel is drawn and `onMember(detail)` is called once; (d) status 404 or 403: `onNotFound()` is called once and nothing of the channel is drawn (the server answers 404 for a private channel and for an id that does not exist alike, BR-10, TOKI-029 R1). People picker
- R17. `PersonChip({ name, hue, removeLabel, onRemove })` renders `span.pchip` holding an `Avatar` of TOKI-013 (`name`, `hue`, `size={20}`), `span.nm` with the name and an `IconButton` of TOKI-012 with `size="sm"`, `icon="x"`, `label` equal to `removeLabel` and `onClick` equal to `onRemove`. It holds no text of its own (R3). `ref` points at `span.pchip`; `className` is added after the component's own; owned attributes follow TOKI-012 R2. `removeLabel` and `onRemove` are required. A long name is cut with an ellipsis and the chip never wider than its parent (Table B).
- R18. `PeoplePicker({ people, selected, onChange, max, limitMessage, searchLabel, emptyText, removeLabel, inputRef? })` is a controlled component. `people` is the list of candidates, each `{ id, displayName, hue }`; `selected` is the list of chosen ids in the order pressed; `onChange(ids)` receives the new list; `max` is the most chosen ids (default 7); the texts come in as props, already translated (`removeLabel(name)` returns the label of one chip). It renders one `div.field.pk` holding the chips (`PersonChip`, in the order of `selected`) and then one `input` (`aria-label` `searchLabel`, `data-autofocus`), then, under it, `div.mlist.people` holding `ul` with one `li` for each candidate whose name matches the text (R19), each `li` holding one `button.mrow` with `type="button"`, `aria-pressed` true when the person is chosen, an `Avatar` of `size={30}` and `span.grow` with the name. Pressing a row that is not chosen adds its id at the end of `selected` and clears the search text; pressing a chosen row removes it; pressing the remove button of a chip removes it. Focus stays on the control that was pressed, except that after the removal of a chip by its button focus moves to the search input (the pressed button is gone). A search with no match renders `EmptyState` with `emptyText` in the list (D-074), centred like R9.
- R19. Matching and window. `matchPeople(people, text)` in `matchPeople.ts` keeps the people whose `displayName` contains the trimmed text, ignoring case and diacritics: both sides are normalised to NFD, combining marks `U+0300` to `U+036F` are removed, `đ` and `Đ` become `d`, and the result is lower-cased. An empty text keeps everyone, in the order given. Decided in this spec: the names are Vietnamese and a person types "nguyen" for "Nguyễn"; the rule is one function and changes in one place. It never throws. The list shows the first 50 matches (D-109) and 50 more each time the container is scrolled within 48 px of its end or focus enters its last row (R8's `useMoreOnScroll`), until all are shown; a new text starts again at 50 and scrolls the container to the top. Check: 120 candidates show 50, then 100, then 120; "nguyen" finds "Nguyễn Văn An" and "dao" finds "Đào Thị Mai"; "mai" finds neither a person without it nor itself twice.
- R20. The limit. When `selected` already holds `max` ids and a person who is not chosen is pressed, nothing is added and `limitMessage` is shown in one `div` with `role="alert"` directly under the field (D-104: the message is under the field), until the next change of `selected` or of the text. The rows stay pressable (a chosen row can still be removed). `NewMessageDialog` gives `max` the value `DIRECT_CONVERSATION_MAX_OTHERS` (7) and `limitMessage` `t("picker.limit", { count: GROUP_DM_MAX_PEOPLE })` (8), both of `@toki/shared`: eight people counting the person who starts (D-017, D-086, BR-36). Check: seven people are chosen with no message; the eighth shows "Up to 8 people, counting you", is not added, and the "Start" request sent afterwards holds seven ids.
- R21. `NewMessageDialog({ open, onClose, onOpenConversation, returnFocusRef? })` renders one `Modal` (form (a)) with `title` `t("picker.title")`, `closeLabel` `t("picker.close.label")`, `returnFocusRef`, a body that holds `PeoplePicker` and a footer with two `Button`s: `t("common.cancel")` (TOKI-093 R24) and `t("picker.start")` with `variant="primary"`. The candidates are `usePeople()` (`getPeople()` of the store of TOKI-032, the `members` of the bootstrap answer, TOKI-031 R5, in its order: `lower(displayName)` in byte order, deactivated people included) that are `active` and are not `useCurrentUserId()`: a deactivated person is not listed and the person themselves is not offered (F25, TOKI-031 R19; messaging yourself is refused by the server with 400 `direct_with_self`, TOKI-030 R5). No request is made to list people: the list is the one already in the store (TOKI-030 Hand-offs), and it is never filtered by the server. `onClose()` is called after a successful start and, through the guard of R23, by a close that is allowed. The dialog holds its chosen ids and its search text in its own state, which is cleared each time it opens.
- R22. Start. "Start" has `disabled` while no person is chosen and while its request runs (D-104, D-111). Pressing it does, in this order: (1) `ensureOnline()`; when `false`, nothing is sent and the dialog stays; (2) `conversations.openDirect(userIds)` (Table N, `POST /api/v1/direct-conversations`, TOKI-030 R2) with the chosen ids in the order pressed, as the store holds them (lower case), the signed-in person not included (the server adds the caller, TOKI-030 R5); one id gives a direct message and two to seven give a group, which the server decides; the screen sends no kind and no key; (3) on 200 or 201 the command has stored the entry of the answer (`DirectConversationEntry`, TOKI-030 R18, R12) and resolves `{ id, created }`; then `onClose()` is called and then `onOpenConversation(id)`. A 200 (the same people already had it) is handled as a 201, with no other text (D-076: the screen shows the conversation; F25: the same set of people always leads to the same conversation). No toast is shown. The dialog states no kind of conversation in its title or its button, whatever the number of chips.
- R23. Errors of Start. A 409 `person_deactivated` with `details.userIds` removes those ids from `selected`, asks the store to read the people again (`conversations.refresh()`, Table N) and shows `t("picker.error.deactivated")` with `role="alert"` under the field; the dialog stays open and the buttons are enabled (D-131: a 409 is a fresh copy). A 400 `group_too_large` shows the message of R20 and keeps the chips (not reachable from a correct screen; the screen sends no more than seven ids, so this is a safeguard and `max` is not read from `details`, TOKI-030 Hand-offs). A 404 `not_found` or 403 `forbidden`, a 400 `direct_with_self` or `validation_failed`, a 5xx or a failure with no answer shows `t("common.error.actionFailed")` with `role="alert"`, keeps the chips and enables the buttons (the shared page of D-131 for 403 and 404 is TOKI-082's, concern 6). Every error keeps the typed text and the chosen people.
- R24. Form guard. `NewMessageDialog` uses `useFormGuard` of TOKI-093 with `hasContent` equal to `hasFormContent({ people: selected })`, `formName` `t("picker.title")` and `onClose` the dialog's close. Its `onRequestClose` is the `Modal`'s, its `requestClose` the "Cancel" button's, its `trackFocus` is put on the element that wraps the picker, and its `discardDialog` is rendered beside the `Modal`, not inside it. So with no person chosen, every close request closes the dialog at once; with one or more chosen, an outside press does nothing, the close button and "Cancel" open the discard question, and Esc does what `GUARD_ESCAPE` says (TOKI-093 R21; "ask" today). The text typed in the search does not count (concern 4). A successful start closes the dialog by `onClose` without the guard. A test covers each reason with zero and with two people chosen, and the discard dialog's two buttons (TOKI-093 R22), and checks that the discard question names "New message" through its `target`.
- R25. Focus. When either dialog opens, focus is in its search input (`data-autofocus`, TOKI-014 R11). When it closes it returns to the element of `returnFocusRef` (TOKI-014 R13), which TOKI-033 passes: the "Browse channels" row or the "+" button. Tab never leaves the dialog while it is open (TOKI-014 R12). ## Design
### What the prototype does, and what this spec does
Measured by reading `docs/blueprint/prototype/index.html` (line numbers are that file's).

| Behaviour | Prototype | This spec |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Browse channels | A modal with a search field, a list (public channels and the private ones you are in), name, topic, "N members", "Join" or "Open" (2156-2160) | The same content, plus the "Archived" label (F21), paging (D-109), the empty state (D-074), the private word (R6) |
| Browse list height | `max-height:360px` with `overflow:auto`, inline (2158) | Table A `.mlist` |
| Archived channels | None in the data | Labelled, "Open" for members only (R7) |
| Join | Closes the modal, opens the channel, toast "Joined #name" (2311) | R11 |
| New message | One search over people and channels; the first press opens a conversation (2139-2144) | D-086: people only, chips, "Start" (R18 to R22). The prototype's `max-height:320px` list is Table A |

### Flows
1. Browse: the owner opens the dialog; the first page is asked; typing asks again after 250 ms; scrolling near the end asks the next page; "Join" asks the join, updates the store, closes and opens the channel; "Open" closes and opens.
2. A link to a public channel the person has not joined: TOKI-033's route finds no such conversation in the store and renders `UnjoinedChannel`; "Join" does R11 and calls `onJoined(id)`, which the route uses to show the channel with its history (D-029); 404 and 403 go to `onNotFound`.
3. Picker: the owner opens the dialog; the person types, presses people (chips appear), presses "Start"; the server decides between a direct message and a group; the store gets the entry; the conversation opens.

### Errors
- First page of Browse fails -> the line and Retry (R10); join fails -> the line of R13; 409 on join -> the list is asked again.
- Start fails -> R23; a deactivated person -> removed from the chips; the eighth person -> R20.
- The connection is down -> `ensureOnline()` blocks Join and Start with its explanation (TOKI-093 R17, D-079); the list of Browse already shown stays readable.
- A dialog closed while a request runs -> R11.
