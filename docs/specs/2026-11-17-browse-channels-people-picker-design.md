# Browse channels and the people picker design

Date: 2026-11-17 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/browse-channels-people-picker.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-034

## Goal
A person needs two screens that the conversation list only points at: one to find and join a channel, and one to start a direct message or a group. This work builds both as dialogs of TOKI-014, the view that a person sees when they reach a channel they have not joined, and the small chip that shows a chosen person. The channel rules (who sees which channel, what a non-member may read, the limit of 8 people in a group) are enforced by the server; the screens show what the server answers and refuse the ninth person before asking.

## Scope
- In:
  - `BrowseChannelsDialog`: search, the first 50 channels and more on scroll, name, topic and member count, "Join" or "Open", the "Archived" label, the empty state, the join.
  - `UnjoinedChannel`: the view of a public channel the person has not joined (name, topic, member count, "Join"), for an address that is not in the person's list.
  - `PeoplePicker` (search, chips, the list of people) and `NewMessageDialog` (the picker in a dialog with "Start"), with the form guard of TOKI-093.
  - `PersonChip` in `packages/ui-web`, with its CSS, tests and examples.
  - `ConversationStarters`, the component that holds which of the two dialogs is open and registers their openers in the registry of TOKI-033 (R30).
  - The CSS of the rows and lists of the two dialogs, and the texts of both in English and Vietnamese (namespaces `browse` and `picker`, and the key `common.close`).
  - The one file in `apps/web` that reads the client and the store of TOKI-032 (R2).
- Out (not now):
  - The "Browse channels" header button and row and the "+" on "Direct messages" (the three controls), the registry of their openers (`ListActionsProvider`, `useRegisterListActions`, `useListActions`), the route `/c/$conversationId` and its address, the seam `UnjoinedView`, the hook `useConversationSelector` and the function `personHue`: TOKI-033 (its R5, R8, R21, R25, R28, R29). TOKI-033 draws each control only while an opener is registered. This ticket gives the dialogs, the view and `ConversationStarters`, which holds the open state, mounts the two dialogs and registers the two openers (R30); it adds one line to `AppShellRoute` and replaces the body of `UnjoinedView` (R16). Decided in this spec: this ticket needs TOKI-033 and TOKI-033 does not need this one (Design, "Hand-offs").
  - The grey system line "Lan joined" that F21 shows after a join: nothing in this ticket writes it; who does is not decided (TOKI-029 concern 4, the seam `recordChange`), and no requirement here changes with the answer.
  - A "Join" in the header or the Details panel of a channel the person has not joined: there is none. Such a channel has no header, because it is not in the list; it is drawn by `UnjoinedChannel` (R16), and Browse has its own "Join". TOKI-035 names this ticket for "Join"; this ticket adds nothing to the panel, and the line of TOKI-035 is for TOKI-035's pull request to reconcile (concern 7).
  - The API client, the store and the people list: TOKI-032, from the answers of TOKI-029, TOKI-030 and TOKI-031. The server rules (who is listed, the limit, the key of a direct message): TOKI-029, TOKI-030.
  - The "Restore" button of an Admin beside an archived channel (F26): TOKI-101. How a member opens an archived channel that is not in their list (it is not in the bootstrap list, TOKI-031 R6): TOKI-101 (concern 2).
  - Creating a channel (Admin page, TOKI-098), adding people to an existing channel (TOKI-100): they may reuse `PeoplePicker` and `PersonChip`; this ticket builds neither screen.
  - The shared page for 403 and 404, loading skeletons, the Retry of a request with no answer after 10 seconds, the offline banner: TOKI-082. Until it lands these dialogs show the plain states of R10 and R14.
  - Presence dots and job titles in the people list: the bootstrap call carries neither (TOKI-031 R12); TOKI-055 adds the dot.
  - The command palette (TOKI-071), which can open Browse channels with a search text (R4).

- R5. Request. When the dialog opens, and each time the search text changes after 250 ms without a keystroke, it asks for the first page of `GET /api/v1/channels` through `api.channels.list({ search, limit: 50, cursor })` (Table N), with `search` the text trimmed of white space and left out when it is empty, and `limit` the value `CHANNEL_BROWSE_PAGE_DEFAULT` of `@toki/shared` (50, D-109, TOKI-029 R17). A new search replaces the list: the rows of the old text are not shown beside the new ones, and the answer of an earlier text that arrives after a later one is ignored. Reopening the dialog asks again: nothing is kept between openings (the Query settings `gcTime: 0` and `staleTime: 0` for this query). Decided in this spec: 250 ms, and TanStack Query's `useInfiniteQuery` (the client of TOKI-006) to page; the tests check the requests made and the rows shown, not the mechanism.
- R6. Rows. ### Flows
1. Browse: the owner opens the dialog; the first page is asked; typing asks again after 250 ms; scrolling near the end asks the next page; "Join" asks the join, updates the store, closes and opens the channel; "Open" closes and opens.
2. A link to a public channel the person has not joined: TOKI-033's route finds no such conversation in the store and renders `UnjoinedChannel`; "Join" does R11 and calls `onJoined(id)`, which the route uses to show the channel with its history (D-029); 404 and 403 go to `onNotFound`.
3. Picker: the owner opens the dialog; the person types, presses people (chips appear), presses "Start"; the server decides between a direct message and a group; the store gets the entry; the conversation opens.

### Errors
- First page of Browse fails -> the line and Retry (R10); join fails -> the line of R13; 409 on join -> the list is asked again.
- Start fails -> R23; a deactivated person -> removed from the chips; the eighth person -> R20.
- The connection is down -> `ensureOnline()` blocks Join and Start with its explanation (TOKI-093 R17, D-079); the list of Browse already shown stays readable.
- A dialog closed while a request runs -> R11.

- The screens show only what the server sends for the person: a non-member of a public channel gets name, topic and member count (D-116, TOKI-029 R7); a private channel the person is not in never reaches the client (BR-09, BR-10).
- The picker lists names that are already in the store; it sends only ids. It stores nothing in the browser and logs no name, topic or id.
- A search text is sent to the server and is not logged by the server (TOKI-029 R20).
- Tests use made-up names and generated ids.
