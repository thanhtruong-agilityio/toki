# Add and remove people in a channel design

Date: 2026-11-17 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/add-remove-people.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-100

## Goal
A member of a channel needs to bring other people in, and an Admin who is a member needs to take a person out. The server already has both requests (TOKI-029) and the Details panel already lists the members (TOKI-035); no screen uses the requests. This work adds the "Add people" control with its dialog, and the "Remove" control on a member row with its confirmation. It builds no new picker and no new confirmation: it joins the people picker (TOKI-034), the confirmation and the discard guard (TOKI-093) and the member list (TOKI-035), and it decides who is offered which control.

## Scope
- In:
  - `AddPeopleDialog`: a `Modal` of TOKI-014 with the people picker of TOKI-034, "Cancel" and "Add", the guard of TOKI-093, the request and its errors.
  - The "Add people" button and the "Remove" button of a member row, the rule that offers each, and the one confirmation for a removal (`ConfirmAction`, action `removeFromChannel` of TOKI-093).
  - The two edits this needs in the files of TOKI-035 (R16): `PeopleSection.tsx` and `useChannelMembers.ts`.
  - The namespace `membership` (13 keys) in English and Vietnamese, one small CSS file, and the tests.
- Out (not now):
  - The routes and their rules (who may add or remove, #general, an archived channel, the repeat): TOKI-029 R11, R13 and the permission functions of TOKI-027. This ticket only calls them and does not offer what they refuse.
  - The picker, the chip, `matchPeople` and the 50-row window: TOKI-034 (`PeoplePicker`, `PersonChip`). The confirmation, the guard, the online gate and `actionPatterns`: TOKI-093. The panel, the members list and its search: TOKI-035. The client and the store: TOKI-032. This ticket uses them as they are and changes only what R16 lists.
  - Archive and Restore of a channel, which also go into the Details panel: TOKI-101.
  - The system lines ("Lan added Minh"), the Activity item ("Lan added you to #name"), the live update of another person's open screen and push: no ticket owns them yet (TOKI-029 concern 4, the seam `recordChange`; TOKI-040 applies events). Nothing here writes a line (concern 1).
  - A person added or removed in a group DM: there is no route and no control (AC-058, BR-36).
  - Removing oneself: leaving is its own action (TOKI-035). The server refuses a self-removal with 409 `self_target` (TOKI-029 R13).
  - Inviting a person who has no account: TOKI-025.

## Design
### What the prototype does, and what this spec does
Measured by reading `docs/blueprint/prototype/index.html` (line numbers are that file's).

| Behaviour | Prototype | This spec |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Members in the Details panel | `Members · N` and one pressable row for each person (line 1443) | The section of TOKI-035; this work adds a button under the heading and a button at the end of each row |
| Add a person to a channel | None: the panel has no control for it. The only "add" in the panel is "Invite a client" under Guests (1445, phase 5) | "Add people" and its dialog (R5 to R10); the flow is F22, which is wording and behaviour accepted as the baseline (D-091) |
| Remove a person from a channel | None | "Remove" on a row and a confirmation (R5, R11 to R13) |
| A dialog with chosen items | New message dialog opens a conversation on the first press (2142); no chips | The picker with chips of TOKI-034 (D-086), used here with another list and limit |
| Confirmation | An inline line under the message or under Leave (1369, 1452) | A dialog that names the person (TOKI-093 R4, R5) |

### Who owns what in the Details panel
TOKI-035 owns the panel, the Members section, its rows (`.mrow`) and `useChannelMembers`; its Hand-offs name this ticket for "Add people (and Remove, for an Admin) as controls under Members". This ticket therefore owns the two controls, their dialogs, their rules and their texts, and edits `PeopleSection.tsx` and `useChannelMembers.ts` as R16 says and nothing else of the panel. TOKI-101 adds Archive and Restore to the panel's section list and edits a different part of it. TOKI-034 owns the picker and the chip that the Add dialog uses.

### The flow
```
Members section  ── "Add people" ──> AddPeopleDialog ── Add ──> addMembers ──> close, reload list and count
                 └─ row "Remove" ──> ConfirmAction (names the person) ── Remove ──> removeMember ──> close, reload list and count
offline: ensureOnline() false ──> nothing sent, explanation toast
```

### Errors
- Offline -> nothing is sent, the explanation toast (R8, R12; D-079).
- A chosen person was deactivated -> the line and the chips are corrected (R9).
- A stale screen (409) -> the channel is read again, the dialog closes, one toast (R9, R13).
- The person is no longer a member or an Admin (403, 404) -> the shared page of D-131 through `readFailure` (R9, R13).
- A server or network failure -> the line under the picker for Add; the toast and an open dialog for Remove.
