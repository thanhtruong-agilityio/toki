# Pause and Focus time design

Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: draft for review (concerns 1, 3, 4 and 15 are open for the product owner)
Intent: docs/intents/pause-focus-time.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-076

## Goal
A person who has to concentrate must be able to make Toki quiet for a while and trust that it stays quiet on every device, ends on time even when every tab is closed, and tells them afterwards what they missed. Focus time must also show Do not disturb to colleagues and put the status "Focusing" on the person, and give back the earlier status at the end.
This ticket builds the whole path: the stored state, the routes that start, extend and end it, the job that ends it on time, the answer to "is this person held at this moment" that the notification decision engine (TOKI-073) and Later (TOKI-067) ask, the count behind the summary, the event that tells every device, the client store, the account menu entries, the strip at the top of the main area, the countdown on the account card, the summary toast, the two palette actions and the line under a direct message header.

## Scope
- In:
  - Shared lengths, schemas and the event payload (R1).
  - The table, the time rules and the three routes that write (R2 to R6), the order of locks and the settling of a period that has expired but not yet been ended (R4), the one procedure that ends a period (R7) and the end job (R8).
  - The reads: the person's own state, the hold answer for TOKI-073, the active Focus periods for presence, and the line of a direct message's other person (R9, R10).
  - The change, the event and the calls to presence (R11, R12), wiring and boundaries (R13), and what is logged (R14).
  - The client: API methods, the store, the account menu entries, the strip and its class `.strip`, the account card countdown, the toasts, the palette actions and the direct message line (R15 to R22), the texts (R23) and what does not change (R24).
- Out (not now):
  - Who gets a toast, push or sound, and the table of notices that were held: TOKI-073. This ticket gives it `HoldPort` and reads `countHeldNotices` (R9, R7).
  - Reminders, their timer and `listRemindersFiredBetween`: TOKI-067. This ticket asks whether a reminder is held through the same port and reads the count of reminders that fired.
  - Setting a status by hand, `setMemberStatus` and `getMemberStatus`: TOKI-056. This ticket calls them.
  - The presence state and the dot: TOKI-055. This ticket calls `setDoNotDisturb` and `restoreDoNotDisturb`.
  - The Pause section of the Preferences screen: TOKI-078; the entries of the More sheet on narrow screens: TOKI-092. Both read the hooks of R16 and R17 and the registry of TOKI-099; they add no state.
  - The toast component and `useToasts`: TOKI-013 (R20 uses them). The notice sink and the sound: TOKI-075, whose concern 10 says the toasts of this ticket (start, extension, end, summary) do not pass through the sink and play no sound. The offline strip and the other tones of `.strip`: TOKI-082; the update strip: TOKI-104.
  - Calendar alerts and the "In a Meet" state (phase 4).

## Requirements
Shared
- R1. `packages/shared/src/quiet/` (exported from the package index) holds `lengths.ts` and `schemas.ts`. `lengths.ts`: `QUIET_KINDS` equal to `["pause", "focus"]`; `PAUSE_LENGTHS` equal to `["30m", "1h", "tomorrow"]` (30 minutes, 1 hour, until tomorrow 08:00, F33); `FOCUS_LENGTHS` equal to `["25m", "1h"]` (F33); `QUIET_LENGTH_MS` `{ "25m": 1,500,000, "30m": 1,800,000, "1h": 3,600,000 }`; `QUIET_REASONS` equal to `["started", "extended", "replaced", "ended_early", "ended"]`; and `PAUSE_TOMORROW_TIME` `"08:00"`. No `demo` choice exists (D-145). `schemas.ts`, all strict: `StartPauseBodySchema` `{ length, timeZone? }` (`timeZone` required when `length` is `tomorrow` and valid by `isValidTimeZone` of TOKI-094); `StartFocusBodySchema` `{ length, statusText }` (`statusText` follows the text rule of `SetStatusBodySchema`, TOKI-056 R2: trimmed, 1 to `STATUS_TEXT_MAX` code points by `statusTextLength`, no control character); `QuietPeriodSchema` `{ endsAt }` (an ISO 8601 string); `QuietStateSchema` `{ pause: QuietPeriod | null, focus: QuietPeriod | null }`; `QuietSummarySchema` `{ count: integer >= 0 }`; `QuietChangedDataSchema` `{ kind, reason, pause, focus, summary: QuietSummary | null }` (the payload of R11; `pause` and `focus` are the state after the change); `QuietNoticeSchema` `{ number: integer >= 0, kind, reason, summary: QuietSummary | null }` (one change as the answer of a request reports it, R16 (b)); `QuietResponseSchema` `{ state, number: integer >= 0, changes: QuietNotice[] }` (`number` is the number of the last quiet event the request wrote, or the last event number read when it wrote none; `changes` lists the changes the request wrote, in order: none, one, or two when it first ended an expired period, R4); `PeerQuietResponseSchema` `{ until: ISO 8601 string | null }`. Check: schema tests accept each valid object and refuse an added key, a length outside its list (`25m` for a Pause, `30m` for a Focus, `demo`), `tomorrow` with no zone or with `Mars/Phobos`, a statusText of `""`, of spaces, of 101 code points and with `\n`, a reason outside the list, a negative count, a negative `number` and a `changes` item with no `kind`; a test names and reads each constant: the four tuples `QUIET_KINDS`, `PAUSE_LENGTHS`, `FOCUS_LENGTHS` and `QUIET_REASONS`, the three numbers of `QUIET_LENGTH_MS` (`25m`, `30m`, `1h`) and the string `PAUSE_TOMORROW_TIME`; the export-list test of `packages/shared` lists the names.

Server: data and time
- R2. The table `quiet_periods` (a Drizzle table in `modules/quiet/schema.ts`, not exported from `index.ts`, one forward-only migration `<number>_quiet_periods`, TOKI-005): `user_id` uuid not null, `workspace_id` uuid not null (foreign keys to `users(id)` and `workspaces(id)` written as SQL, `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, TOKI-019 R22), `kind` text not null with a check in (`pause`, `focus`), `ends_at` timestamptz not null, `held_since` timestamptz not null, `earlier_text` text null, `earlier_clear_at` timestamptz null, `focus_text` text null; primary key (`user_id`, `kind`), so a person has at most one Pause and one Focus; an index on (`ends_at`). No time column has a database default; the server writes the time from the clock (TOKI-008 R19). Checks: `ends_at > held_since`; a `pause` row has the three Focus columns null; a `focus` row has `focus_text` of 1 to 100 characters; `earlier_text` is null or 1 to 100 characters; `earlier_clear_at` is null when `earlier_text` is null. `held_since` is the start of the unbroken stretch of quiet that this row belongs to (R4, R5, R7). `earlier_text` and `earlier_clear_at` hold the status the person had when Focus started (null when there was none); `focus_text` is the text this ticket wrote as the status. A row whose `ends_at` has passed is never read as active (R9) and is ended by the job, or by the next request of the person, whichever comes first (R4, R7). Check: a migration test reads the columns, keys, checks and index from the catalog; each refused row (two rows for one person and kind, a Pause with a `focus_text`, a Focus without one, `ends_at` equal to `held_since`, a 101-character text) is refused by the database; `db:generate` reports no change.
- R3. The time rules are pure functions in `modules/quiet/time.ts`, taking the clock's `now` as a number: `pauseEndsAt(length, now, timeZone?)` returns `now + QUIET_LENGTH_MS[length]` for `30m` and `1h` and `atLocalTime(now, timeZone, 1, PAUSE_TOMORROW_TIME)` of TOKI-094 (R1) for `tomorrow`, and `null` when that is null; `focusStartEndsAt(length, now)` returns `now + QUIET_LENGTH_MS[length]`; `focusExtendedEndsAt(currentEndsAt, length)` returns `currentEndsAt + QUIET_LENGTH_MS[length]` (the reading that TS-064 requires, concern 2; the cap of concern 15 would be one line here). None reads a clock or a global. Check, with fixed numbers: 10:00 gives 10:30, 11:00 and, in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`, 08:00 of the next local day; at 23:30 `tomorrow` is 08:00 of the day after the local date and not 08:00 of the same date; on a day the zone's clock is set forward `tomorrow` still gives a later instant; Focus of 25 minutes started at 10:00 and extended by 1 hour gives 11:25; a search of `time.ts` finds no `Date.now(` or `new Date(`; a Focus with 50 minutes left and 25 chosen is longer, never shorter (TS-064).

Server: routes that write
- R4. `POST /api/v1/quiet/pause`, in the `/api/v1` scope (a valid session, TOKI-022 R24; the `Origin` rule of TOKI-022 R46 covers it), takes `StartPauseBodySchema` and answers 200 `QuietResponseSchema`: the state after the change, `number` and `changes` (R1). A body that fails the schema, or a `tomorrow` whose instant is null, is 400 `validation_failed`. Every write of this module (R4 to R6 and the job of R8) runs in one transaction that takes its locks in one order: accounts first (TOKI-024 R39), then the person's quiet lock, then the counter last (TOKI-056 R6, TOKI-036 R18). In this order: (1) `lockAccount(tx, userId)` (R13) takes the account row `FOR SHARE` and gives its status; a start by a deactivated account is 401 `account_deactivated`, and an end for one is allowed (R7, concern 13); (2) `lockPerson(tx, userId)` (`modules/quiet/lock.ts`) takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on a key built from the user id: one lock for both rows of the person, taken also when the person has no row, because a row lock on a missing row locks nothing; so two first requests of one person, a Pause start against the end of a Focus, and a start against the end job each wait for the other and the primary key never fails; (3) settle: when a row of the person has `ends_at` not above `now` (its job has not run yet, for instance while the worker is stopped), `endPeriod` (R7, reason `ended`) ends it first, in this transaction, so the status is given back, its event and its summary are made once, and the new period never reads the status or the `held_since` of a period that is over; (4) `ends_at` is worked out (R3); (5) the row is written with `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (user_id, kind) DO UPDATE`: when the person has no Pause row (after step 3) a new row with `held_since` equal to the `held_since` of the Focus row of the person, or `now` when there is none, and reason `started`; when one runs, the new `ends_at`, its `held_since` kept, and reason `replaced` (concern 6); (6) the end job is moved: `jobs.cancelByKey(endPeriodJob, "<userId>:pause", { tx })`, then `jobs.enqueue(endPeriodJob, { userId, kind: "pause", endsAt }, { startAfter, key, tx })` (TOKI-064 R11, R12, R14); (7) `recordChange` once with the change of R11, as the last write, then `getLastEventNumber` for `number`. Why a second request replaces the first: the button is disabled while its request runs (D-111), so a double press is one request; a request that comes while a Pause runs is a second choice, from another device or a retry after a lost answer, and the last request wins (D-121); a retry of the same length moves the end by the seconds between the two, which is accepted. A request that settles an expired period writes two events, the end first and the start after it, with consecutive numbers, and `changes` lists both. Check, with the `TestClock`, a real database and the fake queue: a first request writes one row, one job whose `startAfter` is `endsAt` and key is `<userId>:pause`, one event, and answers the number of that event and one change; a second request while it runs leaves one row with the new `endsAt`, the first `held_since`, one cancelled job and reason `replaced`; a request after `ends_at` (the job has not run) first ends the old period (one `ended` event with its summary counted from the old `held_since` to the old `ends_at`) and then writes a new row with `held_since` equal to now, two events in order and `changes` of length 2; two sessions of one person that start a Pause together with no row both answer 200 and leave one row; a Pause start against the end of the Focus of the same person, on two connections, gives either a summary at the Focus end and a Pause with `held_since` now, or no summary at the Focus end and a Pause with the Focus's `held_since`, never a mix and never two summaries; an account deactivation holding the account lock against a start, on two connections, ends without a deadlock and the start is 401; a transaction that throws after the call leaves no row, job or event; `tomorrow` without a zone is 400; no session is 401.
- R5. `POST /api/v1/quiet/focus` takes `StartFocusBodySchema` and answers 200 `QuietResponseSchema`. In one transaction, steps (1) to (3) of R4 first (account lock, person lock, settle). Then: (4) when the person has an active Focus row it is extended: `ends_at` becomes `focusExtendedEndsAt` (R3), nothing else of the row changes, the status is not written again, and the reason is `extended` (F33, D-072 and TS-064: one period, longer; concern 2); (5) otherwise a new row is written: `earlier_text` and `earlier_clear_at` from `getMemberStatus(tx, userId)` of TOKI-056 (`{ text, clearAt }` or null), where a status whose `clearAt` is not above `now` counts as no status (its time passed and its job has not run; the bootstrap hides it too, TOKI-056 R9), `focus_text` the body's `statusText`, `ends_at` from `focusStartEndsAt`, `held_since` as in R4, reason `started`; (6) the end job is moved as in R4 with key `<userId>:focus`; (7) for a new row `setMemberStatus(tx, deps, { userId, text: statusText, clearAt: null, reason: "set" })` of TOKI-056 R6 is called (its own account lock is the second `FOR SHARE` of this transaction and changes nothing, and it writes its own change); (8) `recordChange` for R11 as the last write, then `getLastEventNumber` for `number`. The order of writes is: the rows and the job of this module, then the status (its `recordChange` takes the counter lock), then the quiet `recordChange`; so the status event has the smaller number and the quiet event the larger, and no step of this module takes a lock after the counter lock. After the commit the route calls presence (R12). Check: a first request writes the row with the earlier status of the person (a person with the status "Reviewing" that clears at 15:00, a person with no status, and a person whose status cleared at 09:00 and whose job has not run at 10:00 are three cases, the last giving `earlier_text` null), writes the status "Focusing" through `setMemberStatus` and answers the number of the quiet event; a second request while it runs leaves one row, the same `earlier_text`, a later `ends_at` (longer, also with 50 minutes left and 25 chosen) and no second status write; with the worker stopped past the end of a Focus whose person had "Reviewing", a new Focus first ends the old one (the status "Reviewing" is back, one `ended` event) and then stores "Reviewing", not "Focusing", as `earlier_text` (TS-064 after a stopped worker); a deactivated account is 401 and writes nothing; the status event and the quiet event of one start have consecutive numbers with the quiet one larger; two sessions of one person that start Focus together leave one row and one status write; a request with a statusText of 101 code points is 400.
- R6. `DELETE /api/v1/quiet/pause` and `DELETE /api/v1/quiet/focus` end that period now. Each runs steps (1) and (2) of R4 and answers 200 `QuietResponseSchema`. When the person has no such row it writes nothing and answers the present state, `number` the last event number read in the same transaction and no `changes`, so a device whose strip is out of date (a missed end event) is corrected by the answer (a retry after a lost answer is safe, D-111). Otherwise it calls `endPeriod` (R7) with reason `ended_early` (`ended` when the row had already expired), and after the commit a call that deleted a Focus row calls presence (R12). Check: two `DELETE` calls give one event and the second answers no `changes` and the last event number; the answer of a `DELETE` with nothing to end holds the state; ending a Focus restores the status (R7) and ends Do not disturb (R12); ending a Pause while a Focus runs leaves the Focus row and its strip; a `DELETE` of an expired row that its job has not ended gives reason `ended`; no session is 401.

Server: the end
- R7. `endPeriod(tx, deps, { userId, kind, reason, endsAt?, accountActive })` of `modules/quiet/end.ts` is the one procedure that ends periods; the routes of R4 to R6 (settling and ending) and the job of R8 call it, after steps (1) and (2) of R4, and `accountActive` is the answer of `lockAccount`. In order: (1) it deletes, `returning` the rows, the row of the person with the given `kind` (and, when `endsAt` is given, that exact `ends_at`) and every other row of the person whose `ends_at` is not above `now`; with no row deleted it writes nothing and returns `{ changed: false, number: null, change: null }`. Deleting every expired row in the same call is the rule that gives one summary when a Pause and a Focus end in one poll of the queue, in whichever order their jobs arrive: the first call ends both and cancels both jobs, and the second job finds no row. (2) It calls `jobs.cancelByKey` for the key of each deleted row. (3) When a Focus row was deleted it restores the status, only when `accountActive` is true: the person's present status is read with `getMemberStatus`; when it is the status this ticket wrote (its `text` equals the row's `focus_text` and its `clearAt` is null) it is replaced with the row's `earlier_text` and `earlier_clear_at` when `earlier_text` is not null and `earlier_clear_at` is null or later than `now` (F32, D-072, NR6 and the prototype's `endFocus`), and cleared (`text` null, reason `cleared`) otherwise; when the present status is anything else the person changed it during Focus and it is left alone; this is the one departure from the plain words of D-072 and NR6, and it is concern 4 (a status typed by hand with the same words and no clear time cannot be told from ours and is replaced). (4) It reads the rows of the person that remain; they are all active, because every expired row was deleted in step 1 (F33: Focus ending under a longer Pause leaves notifications paused). (5) When none remains it works out the summary `{ count }`, otherwise `summary` is null. `count` is `countHeldNotices(tx, userId, fromMs, toMs)` of TOKI-073 R10 plus the number of reminders that `listRemindersFiredBetween` of TOKI-067 R12 returns for the same bounds (`SummarySources`, R13), where `fromMs` is the least `held_since` of the deleted rows converted to milliseconds (every row of one unbroken stretch carries the stretch's start, R4 and R5) and `toMs` is the smaller of `now` and the greatest `ends_at` of the deleted rows, so a late job does not count what arrived after the end. (6) It calls `recordChange` for R11 as the last write, with the state that remains and `kind` `focus` when a Focus row was among the deleted ones and `pause` otherwise, and the given `reason` (`ended` for a row that was not ended early), then reads `getLastEventNumber`, and returns `{ changed: true, number, change: { number, kind, reason, summary } }`. Check: with the `TestClock` and fake sources, a Pause alone gives `count` of both sources added, with the lower bound included and the upper excluded; a Focus that ends while a longer Pause runs gives `summary` null, restores the status and leaves the Pause row; the Pause that then ends gives the summary from the Focus's start (the earlier `held_since`); a Pause that began 5 minutes after a Focus began and ended after it gives one summary at its end from the Focus's start; a Pause and a Focus that both end at 10:30 with the worker 1 second late give one event, one summary with `toMs` 10:30 and one restore, whether the Pause job or the Focus job is delivered first, and the second job writes nothing (the case of the review); a Focus ended when the earlier status had a `clear_at` before now gives a cleared status; a status the person set during Focus is left alone; a deactivated person gets the event and no restore; a row that is not there writes nothing; the change is recorded once and after the status write.
- R8. The end job. `endPeriodJob = defineJob({ name: "quiet.end-period", payload: z.strictObject({ userId: uuid, kind: enum, endsAt: ISO date-time }) })` is declared in `modules/quiet` and exported from its `index.ts`; its type is in `apps/server/src/job-types.ts` and its handler factory `createEndPeriodHandler(deps: JobDeps)` in `job-handlers.ts` (TOKI-064 R9). The payload holds an identifier, a kind and a time only (TOKI-064 R7). The handler runs in one `context.db.transaction`: steps (1) and (2) of R4, then `endPeriod` with reason `ended` and the job's `endsAt`; the named row is matched on that exact time, so an extended or replaced period, an ended one and a second delivery of the same job do nothing (an expired row of the other kind is still ended with it, R7). It reads no clock to decide (the job is handed over at `endsAt`, TOKI-064 R14); `context.clock` gives only `now`. The retry defaults of TOKI-064 R8 apply; an error message holds the user id and a code. The handler runs in the worker, which has no presence: the end of Do not disturb is carried by the time given at the start (R12). Check, on a jobs database (`createJobsTestDb`) with the library's `TestClock` at `DEFAULT_TEST_INSTANT`: a Pause of 30 minutes is a row after 29 minutes 59 seconds and gone, with one `ended` event, after a further `pollingSeconds` (2); a Focus extended by 1 hour before its first end keeps its row at the first end time (the first job is cancelled, and a forced delivery of it deletes nothing) and ends at the new one; the same job run twice gives one event; a Pause and a Focus ending in one poll give one event and one summary; the payload walk of TOKI-064 R7 passes; a Focus ended by the worker restores the status with the `JobDeps` of `buildJobDeps` on a worker-mode queue.

Server: reads
- R9. Reads of `modules/quiet`, exported from `index.ts`. (a) `readQuietState(client, userId, now)` returns the `QuietState` of the person from the rows whose `ends_at` is above `now`. (b) `GET /api/v1/quiet` (a valid session) answers 200 `QuietResponseSchema` with `Cache-Control: no-store`: the state, `number` (the last event number) and no `changes`; the last event number read by `getLastEventNumber` in the same read-only transaction (as TOKI-031 R10 does), so a client applies the answer and the events after it in order. (c) `createHoldPort()` returns `HoldPort` of TOKI-073 R10 (`listHeld(client, userIds, at)`): the ids among `userIds` that have a row of either kind with `ends_at` above `at` (milliseconds), in one statement for any number of people, and no statement for an empty list. It is the real port that `app.ts` and `buildJobDeps` pass in place of the stand-in of TOKI-073 and the one Later's `reminderNotifier.isHeld` reaches through it (TOKI-073 R11); so a reminder that falls due during Pause or Focus is held with the same rule as a message (D-072, D-063). It reads no table of another module, and every id it is given is already a member of the one workspace (D-004). (d) `listActiveFocus(client, now)` returns `{ userId, until }[]` for the Focus rows with `ends_at` above `now`, for R12. Check: the state of a person with a Pause that ended a second ago and a Focus that runs holds only the Focus; the answer number equals the last event number; `listHeld` holds a person with a Pause only, one with a Focus only and one with both, not one whose row ended 1 ms before `at`, not a person with no row, and not another person; its statement count is one for 1 and for 200 people; `listHeld` satisfies the type `HoldPort` (a type test in the composition test); a held person's mention writes no event and one `held_notices` row, run through the real handler of TOKI-073 with this port (TS-060).
- R10. The line under a direct message header needs the other person's end time and nothing else. `GET /api/v1/quiet/dm/:conversationId` (a valid session) answers 200 `PeerQuietResponseSchema`: `until` is the latest `ends_at` of the other person's active rows, or null. It is allowed only when `getConversationFacts` of TOKI-028 gives a `dm` of which the caller is a member, and the other person is the one member that is not the caller (`listMemberIds`); anything else, including a group, a channel, a conversation the caller is not in and an id that does not exist, is the same 404 `not_found`. The answer holds no kind, no length, no count and no status; it is for the one person on the other side of a direct message and for nobody else (concern 7). It sets `Cache-Control: no-store`. Check: the other person of a direct message gets `until` while the Pause runs and null after; a stranger, a group, a channel and an unknown id are 404 with the same body; the answer has no other key; a person asking about their own direct message with themselves is 404 (messaging yourself is not supported).

Server: change, event, presence, wiring
- R11. The change and the event. `Change` of `modules/conversations/changes.ts` gains the member `QuietChange`: `{ type: "quiet_changed"; actorId: string; workspaceId: string; userIds: readonly [string]; at: Date; kind; reason; pause; focus; summary }` with no `conversationId`, `actorId` and the one id in `userIds` the person, `at` `new Date(clock.now())`, and `pause`, `focus` and `summary` as in `QuietChangedDataSchema`. The union is built by several tickets (TOKI-056, TOKI-065, TOKI-067, TOKI-102); whichever of them is built later adds its member to the union it finds. The event handler of TOKI-038 (R20) gets one row: `quiet_changed` writes the event `quiet.changed`, scope `user` (the person), payload `QuietChangedDataSchema`, with `recordEvent` and no read of the database; `EventChange` gains the structural member; the registry of `packages/shared/src/realtime/events.ts` gains the kind and `kinds.ts` a row saying it changes no map. The system-line handler of TOKI-037, the Activity handler of TOKI-065 and the notification handler of TOKI-073 ignore this type. A request that settles an expired period writes two of these events, in order (R4). A user event goes to every connection of that person and to nobody else (TOKI-038 R16) and is replayed to that person (TOKI-039), so every device learns of every change, including a device opened later. Check: with the real event handler and fake sockets, a start writes one `quiet.changed` received by both tabs of the person and by nobody else; the payload passes the schema; a rolled-back change leaves no event; the registry test, the kinds test and the export-list tests pass; no `system` message exists after a change; the handlers of TOKI-037, 038, 065 and 073 compile with the wider union.
- R12. Presence. For Focus only (F33, F34: Pause alone never changes presence, TOKI-055 R6), after the commit of a start or an extension the route calls `setDoNotDisturb(userId, endsAtMs)` of TOKI-055 R6, and after the commit of an early end, or of any call that deleted a Focus row, `setDoNotDisturb(userId, null)` (a call that finds no row deletes nothing and makes no call); an end by the job makes no call, because the sweep of presence leaves Do not disturb at the time given at the start (TOKI-055 concern 11, the dot follows the time given at the start exactly and the status text, restored by the job, can lag it by up to 2 seconds, accepted there). When the app starts `app.ts` calls `restoreDoNotDisturb(entries)` with `listActiveFocus` (R9 (d)), so a restart does not drop a running Focus. Presence is memory of the app process and the worker has no access to it; this ticket adds no frame, route or function that sets a state by hand (F34, AC-129). ## Design
### How it fits
Two rows at most for a person, one for each kind, are the whole state. A request takes the account lock and the person's quiet lock, ends any period of the person that has expired, writes the row, moves the end job and records one change in one transaction; the change becomes one user event that every device of the person applies in order, and the answer of the request carries the same change, so the device that asked needs no event to learn what it did. The decision engine and Later never read the table: they ask `HoldPort.listHeld` (R9), which answers true for a person while any row has `ends_at` above the moment asked. The end is a job with the exact end time, so a restart, a late worker or a second delivery cannot end a period that was extended.

Flow of the end:
```
job (endsAt) or DELETE --> account lock, person lock --> endPeriod: delete the row (exact endsAt for the job) and every expired row of the person
                       --> Focus: restore the status if it is still ours
                       --> other row still active? no summary : count held notices + reminders fired from the least held_since to the latest end
                       --> recordChange(quiet_changed) --> event quiet.changed --> every device: strip off, summary toast
```

### The unbroken stretch and the summary
Pause and Focus run side by side. A row gets `held_since` of the other active row when it starts, so every row of one unbroken stretch carries the stretch's start. When the last row ends, the summary counts from the least `held_since` of the rows ended in that call to the greatest `ends_at` among them (or now when that is earlier). A row that expired but was not yet ended counts as part of the stretch until it is ended: a call that finds it ends it together with the row it came for, so one stretch gives one summary however late the worker is. A gap between two stretches, and a start made after the previous period has ended, begins a new `held_since`.
What is counted is what the engine says it held (TOKI-073 R10: one row for each notice that was allowed and then held) plus the reminders that fired in the stretch (TOKI-067 R12). A message that would not have notified (a plain message in a conversation at 'Mentions only', or a mention in a muted channel whose switch is off) is not counted, because it would not have been a notice at all; a plain message in a conversation at 'All messages' is counted and has no Activity item, so the count can be higher than the Activity list (concern 3). A reminder whose item was removed after it fired is no longer returned by Later (TOKI-067 R12), so the count can also be lower than what the person saw arrive; accepted. Held rows are kept 72 hours (TOKI-073 R10), so a summary counts at most the last 72 hours of a very long stretch (concern 11).

### Locks and order
Every transaction of this module takes its locks in one order: the account row `FOR SHARE` (accounts first, TOKI-024 R39), the person's quiet lock (`pg_advisory_xact_lock`), then the writes: the rows and the job of this module, the status through `setMemberStatus` (its `recordChange` takes the counter lock, TOKI-036 R18) and last the quiet `recordChange`. The quiet lock exists because a row lock on a missing row locks nothing; it serialises the two rows of one person across the routes and the job. A deactivation holds the account row `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, so a start waits for it and is refused, and an end that runs in the job for a deactivated person reads the status from the same lock and restores nothing.

### Errors
- A body that fails its schema, or `tomorrow` with no instant: 400 `validation_failed`.
- No session: 401. A deactivated account that starts a Pause or a Focus: 401 `account_deactivated` (R4; the account lock is the first step); the end of a running period for a deactivated person is allowed and restores no status (R7, concern 13).
- An end with nothing running: 200 with the present state, the last event number and no `changes` (R6).
- A direct message line for anything but a direct message of the caller: 404 `not_found`.
- A failing handler rolls the whole transaction back; the job is tried again by TOKI-064 R8, and an end with an already deleted row does nothing.

So R3 `focusExtendedEndsAt` adds the chosen length to the present end (25 minutes while 10 are left gives 35 minutes left), which also never shortens a Focus the person already asked for. The prototype has no extension (it replaces the period and loses the earlier status, the fault D-072 removes). The one thing the log leaves out, a cap on the total, is concern 15. 3. The summary when nothing arrived, when no device is open, and what the count means. Not decided. TS-063 says the app shows "no summary, or an empty one, consistently" and picks neither; D-072 says only that when it ends "one summary lists what arrived". A person with no device open at the end has no one to show a toast to, and the log does not say whether the summary waits. Read literally, the log says: always restore, whatever happened in between, and the prototype's `endFocus` does that. TOKI-056 concern 8 (merged) says the case of a status set during Focus is not in the log and is for this spec. Proposal, not a decision: it departs from the literal text in one case.
