# People screen design

Date: 2026-11-26 · Status: draft for review (concerns 1 and 2 are open for the product owner)
Intent: docs/intents/people-screen.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-079

## Goal
A person has no way to see who is in the workspace. The profile panel (TOKI-057) opens from a message, a member row or a conversation's Details panel, so a colleague the person has never talked to cannot be reached. This ticket builds the People page: the directory of active people as cards, a search by name or job title, the first 50 cards with more on scroll, an empty state, and the press of a card that opens that person's Profile panel at an address of its own. It reads the people the app already holds and makes no request. The cards follow a change of role or status that the app receives live; a person who joins the workspace appears at the next load or refresh of the store, not before (R17).
The rules that must hold are D-118 (initials on a colour), D-152 (no Owner), D-144 (local time from the person's own zone), D-123 (24 hours), D-109 (50 shown, more on scroll), F41 (a deactivated person is not listed), D-074 (an empty state centred in the content) and D-089 (an address, and Back closes the panel first).

## Scope
- In:
  - The route `/people` and its child `/people/p/$personId`, the section row, the page frame and the search field (R1, R2, R11).
  - Who is listed, the card, its presence, its local time (R3 to R6).
  - The search, the window of 50 with more on scroll, the empty state, the state before the people are loaded (R7 to R10).
  - The press of a card, the panel and Back (R11, R12), the keyboard and touch rules (R13).
  - The texts, the styles, the seam, the live changes and what does not change (R14 to R18).
- Out (not now), each with its owner:
  - The panel itself (identity block, Message, Set status, Edit profile, "Deactivated", the unknown-person message), its hooks `useOpenProfileAt` and `useCloseProfileAt`, and `useMinuteNow`: TOKI-057. This ticket mounts and uses them (R6, R11).
  - Presence data, the dot and the three words: TOKI-055. Status text and its dialog: TOKI-056. The card shows neither the status nor a city (R4); the status is shown by the panel that a card opens.
  - Where a person's job title and time zone come from and how they are saved: the ticket "Profile and preferences (server)" (no number yet), and the form that edits them: TOKI-078 (concern 3).
  - The More sheet that reaches People on a narrow screen: TOKI-092. This ticket gives the row its `more: true` (R1).
  - Loading placeholders, the Retry of a failed load and the shared "no access" page: TOKI-082. Where its pieces are not yet on main the page draws the plain state of R10; where they are, it uses them, with the store's error and its `load()` for Retry (R10).
  - The command palette's own choice of a person and its address: TOKI-071.
  - The Guest role and its rule that a guest does not see the directory (phase 5): nothing here knows a guest.
  - The Admin page's member table (TOKI-025): a different screen with its own rows and actions.

The list and the card
- R3. Who is listed. The cards are the people of the store (`getPeople()` of TOKI-032 R23, through the seam of R16) whose `status` is `active`, every one of them once, the signed-in person's own card included (AC-116), in the order the store gives them. A person whose `status` is `deactivated` has no card (F41). No request is made: the store already holds every person of the workspace (TOKI-031 R5). Nothing in the page knows an Owner or a Guest. Check: a store of Minh (admin), Sam, Mai and Alex gives four cards in the store's order, Sam's own included; Alex made `deactivated` gives three; a search for "Alex" then shows the empty state (TS-185); a spy on the client finds no call.
- R4. The card. Each card is an `li` of a `ul` (`aria-label` `t("nav.people")`) holding one `button.pcard` (`type="button"`) with, in order: (a) an `Avatar` of TOKI-013 of 46 px with `name` the person's display name, `hue` `personHue(id)` (TOKI-033 R28) and `presence` when R5 gives one: initials on a colour and no picture (D-118); (b) the name in bold, in a `b.pcard-name` that breaks anywhere (Table A); (c) the `Tag` `t("common.role.admin")` after the name when the person's `role` is `admin`, and nothing for a Member: no card carries "Owner" (D-152); (d) the job title in muted text on its own line when it is not empty after a trim, cut by an ellipsis when long; (e) a last row with the `PresenceDot` of TOKI-013 (no `label`: the word beside it gives the state) and its word when R5 gives them, a spacer, and the local time of R6 with the `clock` icon, in the mono face, when R6 gives one; when R5 and R6 give nothing (every real card until the server ticket lands) the row is not drawn, so it takes no gap. The name, the job title and the time are plain text: `<`, `&` and a link are shown as typed. The card shows no picture, no email address (TOKI-031: others' addresses are not returned), no status text and no city (the list of FR-42 is avatar, presence, name, Admin label, job title and local time). A long name or title never widens the card: the title is cut by `.ell` and the name breaks anywhere (`overflow-wrap:anywhere`), and the card and the grid column can shrink (`min-width:0`, `minmax(0,1fr)`, Table A). Check: markup tests of the order for an Admin, a Member, a person with no job title, one with no presence and no zone (no last row), one with a job title `<b>x</b>` (shown as text) and one with a 200-character name; `getInitials` output for "Mai Vũ" and for a one-word name; the hue equals `personHue(id)`; a scan of the card for `img`, `input` and an `@` address finds none; no text "Owner" in any fixture.
- R5. Presence. The page asks `usePresenceMap(ids)` of TOKI-055 R17 once, for the ids of the cards in the window of R8, and gives each card the state and the word of its person. The avatar's dot is decorative (no `presenceLabel`) and the last row of R4 (e) says the word, so a screen reader reads the state once. When the map has nothing for a person (no snapshot yet, TOKI-055 R17) the card has no dot and no word. The page writes no presence and has no control for it (decided in this spec: D-036 gives no Offline state and presence is worked out, TOKI-057 R20 says the same for the panel). Check: a fake map with `active`, `away` and `dnd` shows the three words; a person with no entry shows no dot and no word; a batch that changes a person outside the window does not render the visible cards again (a render counter); no control in the card sets presence.
- R6. Local time. When the person has a `timeZone` that `isValidTimeZone` of `@toki/shared` accepts, the card shows `formatTime(now, timeZone)` of TOKI-094 R1: `HH:mm`, 24 hours, never `AM` or `PM` (D-123), in the zone the person set and not the viewer's (D-144). `now` comes from one call of `useMinuteNow()` of TOKI-057 R6 in `PeoplePage`, handed down to the cards, so the whole list has one timer and the time changes at the next minute of UTC time. When the person has no zone, or `formatTime` returns `null`, the card shows no time and no clock icon (concern 2). `jobTitle` and `timeZone` are read through `DirectoryPerson` of `people/deps.ts` (R16; TOKI-057's widened type is local to its own `deps.ts` and not exported) (concern 3). The zone is passed to `formatTime` as an argument; no object literal with a `timeZone` property is built outside the tests (the lint rule of TOKI-080 R8 and R9 reads such literals). Check, with the `TestClock` and the fake `timers` of TOKI-008 and no real timer: at 10:42:30 UTC a person in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` shows `17:42` and after 30 s of the fake clock `17:43`; one in `Asia/Kolkata` shows `16:12`; `00:05` and `23:59` show two digits; a zone `Mars/Phobos` and a person with no zone show no time; the viewer's own zone (set by the `TZ` variable to `Pacific/Auckland`) changes nothing; the label read before the time is `profile.localTime` (R14); two hundred cards make one timer; unmounting leaves no pending timer.

Search and windows
- R7. Search. The toolbar holds one `Field` of TOKI-012 with `variant="search"` the `search` icon and the class `people-search` (Table A: the prototype's `max-width:340px; flex:1`, line 1633), `type="search"`, `aria-label` and `placeholder` `t("people.search.label")` ("Search people", the prototype's), `maxLength` 80 and no `data-autofocus`. The text is held by the page and filters at each keystroke, with no request and no wait. `matchDirectory(people, text)` in `apps/web/src/people/directory.ts` is pure: it trims the text and keeps the people whose display name contains it, or whose job title contains it, ignoring case; an empty text keeps everyone in the order given; a person with no job title is matched by name only; it never throws. It is the one place that decides a match, so concern 1 changes one function. The cards that remain keep the store's order. Check: "mai" keeps Mai only (TS-186); "market" keeps Mai, whose job title is "Marketing", and no one whose name and title lack it (TS-187); "MAI" and "mai" give the same cards; "  mai " gives the same as "mai"; a person with no job title is not matched by an empty-title false positive; the field's computed width is at most 340 px in a toolbar of 1000 px; an empty text keeps all; the function is the only one in the folder that compares a typed text with a name.
- R8. The first 50, more on scroll. The window starts at 50 cards (D-109, AC-118). When the end of the scrolling body is within 48 px, or focus enters the last card, and there are more matches than shown, the window grows by 50, until every match is shown. The hook is `useMoreOnScroll` (`apps/web/src/features/useMoreOnScroll.ts`, TOKI-034 R8), whose result goes to `SectionPage` as `bodyProps` and whose default row selector `li` finds the cards (R4), so this ticket passes no `rowSelector` and depends on no edit to the hook. The hook reacts only to a scroll or to focus, and on a tall or wide screen the first 50 cards can fit with no scrollbar, so the 51st person could never be reached. The page therefore also grows the window by 50 when there are more matches than shown and the body does not overflow (`scrollHeight <= clientHeight + 48`), after each render and on each resize of the window (the `resize` event, and a `ResizeObserver` of the body where the browser has one), until the body overflows or every match is shown. It reads the body through the ref that the hook returns and edits no file of the hook. A new search text starts the window again at 50 and scrolls the body to the top. The window is in memory over the list that R3 and R7 produced; nothing is asked of the server. Check: 51 people show 50 cards and, after one scroll of the body near its end, 51 (TS-188); 120 people show 50, then 100, then 120, and a further scroll changes nothing; focus entering the 50th card shows 100; a body whose `scrollHeight` equals its `clientHeight` (a fake layout) with 120 people reaches 120 with no scroll, and a body that overflows by more than 48 px stays at 50; a resize that makes the body tall enough grows the window; a new text shows at most 50 and the body's `scrollTop` is 0; the hook's existing tests of TOKI-034 still pass.
- R9. Empty. When no person matches the text, the body holds one `EmptyState` of TOKI-013 with `filtered` (a list emptied by a search is announced, TOKI-013 concern 11, D-074), the icon `users`, the title `t("people.empty.title")` ("Nobody matches", the prototype's, line 1635) and the description `t("people.empty.hint")` ("Try a name or a job title.", the prototype's), centred in the body (D-074; `.secbody` is a flex column with a height, TOKI-015 R15, and the empty state takes the free height, TOKI-013 R17). No count is shown. An empty text never gives this state: the signed-in person is always listed once the store is ready. Check: a search for `zzzz` shows the title and the hint, in an element with `role="status"`; in a browser at 360, 720 and 1100 px, in Calm and Focus, the centre of the empty block's content is within 1 px of the centre of the body.
- R10. Before the people are held. While the store holds no person, no card and no empty state are drawn (a list that is not loaded is not "Nobody matches"). When the store holds people, whatever its `status` (a refresh in progress, or a failed refresh), the page shows them. What takes the place of the cards depends on whether TOKI-082's pieces are on main when this ticket is built: (a) when `apps/web/src/states/` is on main, the page uses them and writes no stand-in (082 R13): `PageSkeleton` while the status is `idle`, or `loading` with no failure remembered (082 R8 (a)); and `LoadFailed` (`variant` `page`) when the status is `failed`, and also when it is `loading` after a failure, with `busy` in that case, so that the block and the skeleton do not flip during a retry (082 R8 (a), R7). `LoadFailed` gets `error` from `useLoadFailure()` of R16 and `onRetry` from `reloadStore()` of R16, which calls the store's `load()` (082 R13: Retry calls `conversations.load()`); the page passes no `onNoAccess`; (b) when it is not, the page draws nothing in the body but the empty element, with no text, and 082 replaces it (its R13 lists this row). In (b), and only for `idle` and `loading`, `bodyProps` carries `aria-busy="true"`; for `failed` with no person nothing is loading and nothing will change, so there is no `aria-busy`. Check: a fake store in each of the four statuses with no person shows no card and no `EmptyState`; `aria-busy` is set for `idle` and `loading` and absent for `ready` and `failed` (case b); with the states folder present, the skeleton shows for `idle` and for `loading` with no failure remembered, `LoadFailed` shows for `failed` and for `loading` after a failure (with `busy` and no skeleton), and one press of its Retry calls `load()` once (case a); with people held in `ready`, `loading` and `failed` the cards show.

### Where each field comes from
```
store person (TOKI-032 getPeople)     id, displayName, role, status       exists today
  + jobTitle, timeZone                "Profile and preferences (server)"  `DirectoryPerson` of `people/deps.ts` (R16) until then
presence map (TOKI-055)               state, word                         from the snapshot
clock + timers (TOKI-057 useMinuteNow) now, redraw at the next UTC minute
```
`PeoplePage` is a mapping of these into cards with no request of its own. The bootstrap person has four keys today and a strict schema (TOKI-031 R12), so before the server ticket lands no real person has a job title or a zone: the card then shows no job title and no time, and the search by job title finds nothing. The tests use fixtures that carry the keys, as TOKI-057 does.

### Decided in this spec
- Address. `/people/p/$personId`, a path child of `/people` with no component, the way TOKI-057 made the profile a path child of the conversation (its Design, "The address"): the panels are one slot, a path shows the person in the address and a copied link opens the same profile (D-089). The page is the parent's component and stays mounted under the child, which keeps the search text and the scroll.
- The window is in memory. The store holds every person (TOKI-031 R5), so "the first 50, more on scroll" (D-109) is a slice of an array the app already has, grown by 50 as TOKI-034 R19 does for its picker. A request for pages would add a server route and a second source of the same list for no gain.
- The directory includes the signed-in person (AC-116, the prototype), and pressing it opens the own profile with Set status (TS-190).

### Flows
1. Open: the person presses People; the page lists the cards, the first 50; the toolbar's field is empty.
2. Search: the person types; the cards that match stay; none gives "Nobody matches".
3. Open a profile: the person presses a card; the address becomes `/people/p/{id}`; the panel opens; the list, text and scroll stay; Back closes the panel.
4. Message: in the panel the person presses Message; the direct message opens; Back returns to People.
