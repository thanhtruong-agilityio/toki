# Search inside a conversation design

Date: 2026-11-23 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/conversation-search.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-072

## Goal
A person who has a conversation open can look for a message in it by a word or by the name of the sender, without scrolling. A magnifier in the conversation's header opens a search field under the tab row; the messages of this conversation that match are listed under it; pressing one closes the search and shows that message in the list (F19).
The prototype filters the list in place because it holds every message in memory. The real list holds a window, so the match is made by the server, on the Vietnamese index of TOKI-069 and with the permission rule of the message history (TOKI-037 R1): a conversation the person may not read is answered as one that does not exist, and the search never reads outside the one conversation it was asked about.
This ticket builds one route, one read of messages by author, one client method, the header button and the search area of the Messages tab. It does not build the palette's message search, the jump to a message or its flash.

## Scope
- In:
  - `GET /api/v1/conversations/{id}/search` with its query, its answer, the cursor and the permission steps (R1 to R5).
  - The one read of `messages` that finds the messages of one conversation by their author (R4), and the merge of its rows with those of `searchMessageIds` (TOKI-069 R9).
  - The shared shapes (R6) and the `api` client method (R7).
  - The magnifier button in the header, the state of the search, the search field, the result area with its states and the result row (R8 to R12), the texts (R13).
- Out (not now):
  - The index, the word rule and `searchMessageIds`: TOKI-069. The route `GET /api/v1/search`, `buildMessageEntriesByIds`, the shared limits and the cursor of messages: TOKI-070. This ticket uses them and does not change them.
  - Ctrl+K and its message group: TOKI-071 (see "Where this and the palette meet" in Design).
  - Showing a message from a result in the list, centring it and the flash: TOKI-041 (the opening at a message, `openAt` of the kind `jump`) and TOKI-068 (the flash). A reply opens in the thread panel of TOKI-045.
  - The search of Files (TOKI-063), the search of people, channels and the conversation list (TOKI-033, TOKI-034, TOKI-079).
  - Highlighting the matched words, stepping through matches with arrows, a keyboard shortcut for the search: not in the prototype and not in F19 (concern 1).
  - The page of an archived channel (concern 6).
  - Guests (phase 5, D-090, D-152).

## Requirements
- R1. One route, `GET /api/v1/conversations/{id}/search`, in the module `search` of TOKI-070 (a new file `conversation-routes.ts` of that module and one line in its `index.ts` that registers it; the route `GET /api/v1/search` and its files are not changed). It is registered in the `/api/v1` scope, so every request needs a valid session (TOKI-022 R24: 401, and 401 `account_deactivated`). After the session, in this order, as `GET .../messages` of TOKI-037 R1 does: (1) the schema of the path and of the query (R2): 400 `validation_failed`; (2) `getConversationFacts(db, actor.id, id)` of TOKI-028: `null`, and a private channel, a direct message or a group of which the actor is not a member, are 404 `not_found` with the same body as an unknown conversation, before any permission function runs; (3) `canReadConversation` of TOKI-027 R5: a person who is not a member of a public channel gets 403 `forbidden`; (4) the reads of R3 to R5. A member can search whatever the kind of conversation, whether it is archived, and whether the other person of a direct message is deactivated (all of them can be read, TOKI-027 R5, F26, F41). The route takes no body and no other parameter, answers with the header `cache-control: no-store`, and no code of it reads the role of the actor (TOKI-027 R16). Check: a table test sends to the route a request without a session (401, no read of `conversations`, `users` or `messages`), a deactivated account (401 `account_deactivated`), an invalid id (400), an unknown id (404), a private channel of others, a direct message of others and a group of others (404, the same body as the unknown id), a public channel the actor has not joined (403 `forbidden`), and each kind of conversation of the actor, an archived channel and a direct message with a deactivated person (200); the statement count of every refused case is 0 after the facts read of step 2; a source search finds no comparison with `"admin"` or `"member"` in the new file.
- R2. The query. `q` is required, one string, trimmed of white space at both ends (`String.prototype.trim`), and the trimmed text has `SEARCH_MESSAGE_MIN_LENGTH` (2, TOKI-070 R12) to `SEARCH_QUERY_MAX` (200) code points. `cursor` is optional, a non-empty string of at most `SEARCH_CURSOR_MAX` (512) characters. A repeated key, any other key, an empty or white-space-only `q`, a `q` of 1 code point, a `q` of 201, a `cursor` over the limit and a cursor that is not a messages cursor (R5) are 400 `validation_failed` with the path of the key in `details.issues` (the cursor with `invalid_cursor`, as TOKI-070 R8), and no read is made. The minimum of 2 is a proposal (concern 2): it is the minimum that F29 gives to the messages of Ctrl+K, and a one-letter prefix is the case that TOKI-069 R11 measures as slow. Check: a table test with `q` of 0, 1, 2 and 200 code points and of 201 (with 200 emoji, so that code points and UTF-16 units differ), a `q` of one letter followed by spaces (1, refused), a repeated `q`, an unknown key, a cursor of 512 and of 513 characters (513 refused before decoding): status, code and `details.issues[0].path`; the statement count of each refused case is 0 after the session.
- R3. What matches. A message of the conversation is a result when it is a `user` message that is not deleted and either (a) its text matches by the rule of TOKI-069 R7 (every word of `searchWords(q)` is the start of a word of the message, case and diacritics ignored), through `buildSearchTsQuery(q)` and `searchMessageIds` of TOKI-069 R9 with `conversationIds` the one id of the path; or (b) its author is an account whose `displayName` contains the trimmed query, both folded with `foldText` of `@toki/shared` (TOKI-050 R2, the fold of TOKI-070 R6): the accounts come from `listPeople(client, workspaceId)` of the `members` module and include the deactivated ones, whose old messages stay (F41), and the signed-in person's own name counts like any other (the prototype's filter does the same). When `buildSearchTsQuery(q)` is `null` (no word, for example `!!`), only (b) applies. A reply of a thread of this conversation is a result like any message (concern 4). A message of another conversation, of another workspace, a system line and a deleted message are never results (the index leaves the last two out, TOKI-069 R2, and the read of R4 checks them again). Check, on a fixture of two conversations of the actor and one conversation of another person, with the messages of TOKI-069 R12 and the name `Nguyễn Văn Đạt` written in the precomposed and the decomposed form: `nguyen` finds the messages whose text has `Nguyễn` and the messages sent by `Nguyễn Văn Đạt`, in the one conversation asked and in no other; `duong dan` finds `đường dẫn`; `Nguyễn` and `nguyen` give the same page; a message of the other conversation of the actor, a message of the conversation of another person, a deleted message and a system line that contain the word are never in the page; a reply is in the page with its `parentId`; a message of a deactivated person is in the page and the person's name finds it; `!!` gives an empty page and no read of `search_vector`; a name that matches no account and a word that matches no message give an empty page.
- R4. The read by author. `listMessageIdsByAuthors(client, input)` of `apps/server/src/modules/messages/author-search.ts`, exported by `index.ts` (no table is exported), takes `{ workspaceId, conversationId, authorIds, limit, before? }` and runs one statement over `messages` where `workspace_id`, `conversation_id` and `author_id = any($authorIds)` hold, `kind = 'user'`, `deleted_at is null` and, with `before`, `(created_at, id) < ($before.createdAt, $before.id)`, ordered by `created_at desc, id desc`, with `limit`, and returns `{ id, conversationId, createdAt }[]` as `searchMessageIds` does. `limit` is an integer from 1 to 50 and `authorIds` has 0 to 1,000 distinct ids; anything else throws before a statement is sent, and an empty list returns `[]` with none (a workspace with more than 1,000 accounts is outside D-113's 200 people, so the function refuses instead of cutting the list). It does not decide who may read the conversation: the route has checked it (R1). The route asks both reads for `limit` 21 and the same `before`, merges the two lists by (`createdAt`, `id`) newest first, drops a repeated id and keeps the first 21; the page is the first 20 and `nextCursor` is not null when a 21st exists. The text read of (a) and this one take the same `before`, so the merged list is the one a single query would give for rows written with a whole millisecond (TOKI-070 R8, TOKI-069 R9); the seed of TOKI-081 writes whole milliseconds. Check: a database test finds the rows of the given authors in the given conversation only, newest first with equal times broken by id, never a deleted message, a system line, a message of another conversation or of another workspace; `limit` 0 and 51, 1,001 ids and a repeated id throw with no statement; an empty list returns `[]` and sends none; the export test of the module lists the function and no table; a route test with 45 matching messages, some by text, some by author and some by both, reads three pages of 20, 20 and 5, each message once, in order, and the last `nextCursor` is null.
- R5. The answer and the cursor. 200 with `{ items: SearchMessage[], nextCursor: string | null }` and no other key; `SearchMessage` is the strict object of TOKI-070 R7 (`{ id, conversationId, conversationName, authorId, parentId, createdAt, text, textTruncated }`), built the same way: the entries come from `buildMessageEntriesByIds(db, workspaceId, [id], ids)` of TOKI-070 R9 for the first 20 ids of the merged list, an entry that is deleted, not a `user` message, or with no row is dropped (so a page can be shorter than 20 while `nextCursor` is not null, and the client asks again), the `text` is the first `SEARCH_TEXT_MAX` (300) code points and `textTruncated` says it was longer, and no reaction, file, pin or mention is read. `nextCursor` is the encoding of TOKI-070 R8 for messages, base64url of `[createdAtIso, id]` of the last id read among the first 20 (the position read and not the position shown, so a page whose items were all dropped still advances); the functions that encode and decode it are those of `cursor.ts` of the same module. A cursor made for names (three elements) or malformed is 400 `validation_failed` with `invalid_cursor`; a cursor made with another query is a position in this query's order and gives a valid page, which reveals nothing because every page is read from this one conversation. An empty result is 200 with `{ items: [], nextCursor: null }`. The page size is `SEARCH_PAGE_SIZE` (20) of TOKI-070 R12 (concern 3). Check: the body of every 200 is parsed with the schema of R6; 301 code points are cut to 300 with `textTruncated` true and 300 are not; an emoji at the cut is not split; a fake builder that returns no entry for the 20 ids gives an empty page with a `nextCursor` that the next call follows to the next ids; each malformed cursor, a names cursor and a cursor of 513 characters are 400 and never 500; a cursor past the end gives an empty page with `nextCursor` null.

## Design
### Why a route of its own
`GET /api/v1/search` of TOKI-070 has no conversation filter: its query takes only `q`, `kind` and `cursor` (R2, any other key is 400), it reads the messages of every conversation the actor belongs to (R7), it never answers 403 or 404 about data (R11 (a)), and it does not match the sender; its Scope and its "Contract for the tickets that follow" give the search in one conversation, which also matches the sender, to this ticket. Decided in this spec (concern 7): this ticket adds its own route and changes nothing of that one. A filter on that route would need a change of its strict query, of its answer for a refused case and of its permission steps (a single conversation follows the message history's 404 and 403, TOKI-037 R1, not the palette's "always 200"), and it would still need the author read.
```
GET /api/v1/conversations/{id}/search?q=nguyen[&cursor=...]
  session -> schema -> getConversationFacts (404) -> canReadConversation (403)
  (one READ ONLY, REPEATABLE READ transaction from here)
  S1 listPeople ------------> accounts whose folded name contains the folded q  -> authorIds
  S2 searchMessageIds ------> ids by text   (conversationIds: [id], limit 21, before)   [if buildSearchTsQuery(q)]
  S3 listMessageIdsByAuthors -> ids by sender (limit 21, before)                        [if authorIds not empty]
  merge by (createdAt, id) -> first 20 ids (+ the 21st tells nextCursor)
  S4 buildMessageEntriesByIds(db, ws, [id], ids) -> SearchMessage[] (text cut at 300)
```
The facts and the five reads run in one `READ ONLY` transaction at `REPEATABLE READ`, opened by the module's service itself with the database client as TOKI-070 R10 does, so a person removed from the conversation while the request runs gets nothing that the snapshot does not hold. The statements of a request are at most 5 (facts, S1 to S4), 0 for the reads when the schema or the facts refuse.

### Where this and the palette meet
| | Search inside a conversation (this ticket) | Messages group of Ctrl+K (TOKI-071 on TOKI-070) |
|---|---|---|
| Asks | one conversation | every conversation the person belongs to |
| Matches | the text and the sender's name | the text only (TOKI-070 R3, R7) |
| Answer | `{ items, nextCursor }` of `SearchMessage` | four kinds, each a page |
| Refused case | 404 or 403, as the history | 200 with an empty page |
| On screen | a field under the tab row, results in the Messages tab | the palette dialog |
| Opening a result | closes the search, then the list or the thread | closes the palette, then the same opening (TOKI-068) |
Both use the index of TOKI-069, the word rule, the order (newest first, proposal) and the cursor of messages, so the same words give the same messages in the order they have in both places, and both end in `onOpenMessage` or the thread address. Neither draws the other's rows: this ticket's result row has no conversation name (the person is in it), the palette's has one. If TOKI-071 builds a message result row, the ticket built second may reuse it; this ticket does not depend on it (concern 9).

## Data safety
- The route reads conversation messages and names and writes nothing. The answer is personal and is not cached (R1). The text of a search is in the query string: the log line of a request holds the method, the path without the query, the status and the duration (TOKI-004 R7), the route adds no log line, an unexpected error is a 500 `internal_error` from the module's `safe-error.ts` with no text of the query in the body, and a 404 or 403 body names no conversation (TOKI-070 R11 (b), (c), (d) apply to this route, TOKI-037 R23). Check: a forced failure of each read gives a body with no text of the query and a captured log with none; the body of a 404 for a private channel of others equals that of an unknown id.
- The permission rule is the steps of R1 and the single conversation id of the reads: no read is given an id of another conversation, and the entries are read with the conversation id again (TOKI-070 R9 b). The typed text never leaves the browser except as the query of this request; it is not kept in the address, the history or the storage (R8).

## Areas of concern
1. The form of the result: the list filtered in place, a list of results, or a highlight with stepping. Not decided. The prototype filters the message list and has no result to press, no highlight and no stepping (`msgsHtml`, line 1327 of `index.html`); F19 says the list keeps the matching messages and then "press a result: search mode closes, the view scrolls to that message and flashes it"; the plan line says "filters messages". A filter in place would need the full message row for every result (reactions, files, pins, a thread line) and a list that is a set of search results instead of a window of the sync engine (TOKI-041 reads windows only), which the search read does not give. Proposal, not a decision (R9 to R12): a list of results, one row each, drawn in the place of the list, and a press on a row shows the message in the list. If the owner wants the prototype's look, the rows of the area become the message component fed by a read with the entry readers of the message API, and R10 to R12 change. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
2. From how many characters it searches. Not decided. F29 gives 2 for the messages of Ctrl+K; F19 gives none and the prototype filters from the first character. Proposal, not a decision (R2, R10): 2 code points, the same minimum, because the word rule is a prefix and a one-letter prefix is the case that TOKI-069 R11 measures at 335 ms over 1,000,000 messages; below 2 a line asks for more and the list stays. If the owner wants 1, `SEARCH_MESSAGE_MIN_LENGTH` is not used by this route and the cost of concern 8 grows. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
3. How many results, their order and how more is asked. Not decided. D-112 gives 20 per kind and "more on request" for Ctrl+K; the prototype lists every match in the order of the list (oldest first). Proposal, not a decision (R4, R5, R11): 20, newest first as TOKI-070 R7 proposes, and a "Show more" button; the cursor works only for the newest-first order, so an answer to TOKI-069 concern 5 (the order of messages) changes the read, the merge and the cursor for both routes. A scroll that loads more by itself is the alternative. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
4. Replies of threads in the results. Not decided. The prototype keeps only messages with no parent (`!m.parent`); `searchMessageIds` returns replies (TOKI-069 R9) and TOKI-070 concern 4 asks the same. Proposal, not a decision (R3, R12): replies are results, marked "Reply", and a press opens the thread with the reply in view, because a person who looks for a word said in a thread would otherwise never find it. If the owner leaves them out, the condition is `parent_id is null or also_in_channel` in both reads and the rule of R12 for a reply goes. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
5. Matching the sender. Accepted: F19 says the list keeps the messages "sent by someone whose name matches", and D-091 accepts what the journeys do. The rule for the name is Decided in this spec: the same as the lists and the palette use for names (TOKI-070 R6), `foldText` on both sides and a contains test on the whole trimmed query, over every account including the deactivated ones whose old messages stay (F41), and the signed-in person's own name counts as in the prototype. Reason: one fold for every list; no second rule to keep equal. A text that has words and a name that contains the whole query are both matches (a union). Owner: Thanh Truong.
6. The page of an archived channel. Not decided. The page has its own header and no tab row (TOKI-101, TOKI-041 R3) and the log says its history "stays readable and searchable" (F26) without saying by which search. The route accepts an archived channel (R1) and Ctrl+K finds its messages (TOKI-070 R7). Proposal, not a decision (R8): no button on the archived page in this ticket; the context is absent there, and a later change would give `ArchivedHistory` the same two components. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
7. Own route or a filter on the search API. Decided in this spec: its own route, `GET /api/v1/conversations/{id}/search`, and no change to TOKI-070. Reason: Design, "Why a route of its own". The change request that was the alternative, a `conversationId` key on TOKI-070's query with the sender match added to its message kind, is left unmade; the one thing this route takes from that ticket's module is the files it already has. Owner: Thanh Truong.
8. How a text matches, and the cost. Not decided: they are concerns 4 and 11 of TOKI-069. The prototype's filter is a substring of the whole lower-cased text (`includes`), so `ieng` finds `tiếng` there; the proposal of TOKI-069 R7 matches word prefixes, so it does not. A person who searches one conversation may expect the prototype's reading more than one who uses Ctrl+K. This route follows whatever TOKI-069 decides and changes nothing itself; the cost of a very common short prefix is paid by the index read for the whole workspace and not for one conversation, so the minimum of concern 2 matters here as well. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
9. How a result opens, and who owns it. Decided in this spec: a root message through `onOpenMessage` of TOKI-041 R4 (the opening at a message, centred, `data-target`), a reply through the thread address of TOKI-045 R16 with `reply`; the flash is TOKI-068's and the opening is TOKI-041's, so this ticket draws no scroll and no flash. The jump removes the New divider of the conversation (TOKI-054 R10 (a), itself a proposal there, concern 6). Reason: one path to a message for Pins, Activity, the palette and this search. If TOKI-071 builds a result row for messages, the one built second may reuse it; neither is a need of the other. Owner: Thanh Truong.
10. What the list does while the results show. Decided in this spec: the list is not mounted while results are drawn, and mounts again when the search closes, opening at the end as at every opening (TOKI-041 R14, concern 4 there: no position is remembered). Reason: a virtual list that is hidden cannot measure its rows, and keeping it visible under the results would draw two lists. The cost is that closing the search with the cross returns to the latest message, not to where the person was. Owner: Thanh Truong.
11. Where the search state lives. Decided in this spec: in the page's context, not in the address, the history, the store or the storage, and cleared when the conversation changes or the Messages tab is left. Reason: the prototype keeps it in the page state and clears it at `openConv`; a typed text is not an address (the technical design keeps interface state in the address only where a copied link needs it); Back then leaves the conversation as TOKI-041 concern 2 says. Owner: Thanh Truong.
12. Technical choices the Blueprint leaves to the builder. Decided in this spec: the debounce of 250 ms and the abort of an earlier request (R10), up to 5 automatic requests for pages that came back empty (R10), the page of 21 reads and the merge of two lists (R4), one snapshot for the facts and the reads (Design), the text cut of 300 code points and the three-line clamp (R5, R12), the title of the empty state with the text as typed and not lower-cased as the prototype does (Table B), and 1,000 authors at most (R4). Reason: each is in the requirement that holds it; none is in the log. Owner: Thanh Truong.
13. Texts. Settled by D-139 (Accepted Assumption): every wording the prototype does not contain, and every Vietnamese text, is a proposal; Table B marks each one and a change is a catalogue edit with no code change. Owner: Thanh Truong.
