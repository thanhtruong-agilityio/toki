# Search inside a conversation design

Date: 2026-11-23 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/conversation-search.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-072

## Goal
A person who has a conversation open can look for a message in it by a word or by the name of the sender, without scrolling. A magnifier in the conversation's header opens a search field under the tab row; the messages of this conversation that match are listed under it; pressing one closes the search and opens that message through the one hook that opens a message, `useOpenMessage` of TOKI-068 (F19; concern 9).
The prototype filters the list in place because it holds every message in memory. The real list holds a window, so the match is made by the server, on the Vietnamese index of TOKI-069 and with the permission rule of the message history (TOKI-037 R1): a conversation the person may not read is answered as one that does not exist, and the search never reads outside the one conversation it was asked about.
This ticket builds one route, one read of messages by author, one client method, the header button and the search area of the Messages tab. It does not build the palette's message search, the jump to a message or its flash.

## Scope
- In:
  - `GET /api/v1/conversations/{id}/search` with its query, its answer, the cursor and the permission steps (R1 to R5).
  - The one read of `messages` that finds the messages of one conversation by their author (R4), and the merge of its rows with those of `searchMessageIds` (TOKI-069 R9). The read may need an index on `messages`, which would be a migration of this ticket (R4, "Checks left for the build" (1)).
  - The shared shapes (R6, with their own item, which has no conversation name) and the `api` client method (R7).
  - The magnifier button in the header, the state of the search, the search field, the result area with its states and the result row (R8 to R12), the texts (R13).
- Out (not now):
  - The index, the word rule and `searchMessageIds`: TOKI-069. The route `GET /api/v1/search`, `buildMessageEntriesByIds`, the shared limits and the cursor of messages: TOKI-070. This ticket uses them and does not change them.
  - Ctrl+K and its message group: TOKI-071 (see "Where this and the palette meet" in Design).
  - Opening a message from a result (the list at that message, the thread panel for a reply, the flash): TOKI-068 owns `useOpenMessage`, which uses the opening at a message of TOKI-041 (`openAt` of the kind `jump`) and the thread address of TOKI-045 (R16). This ticket calls the hook and draws no scroll, no flash and no address.
  - The search of Files, which is made by the server's list of shared items and not on data the client holds (TOKI-063 R3); the search of people, channels and the conversation list (TOKI-033, TOKI-034, TOKI-079). This ticket searches messages only; the Files & links tab of a conversation has no search field (TOKI-063 R13) and the field of this ticket does not search files.
  - Highlighting the matched words, stepping through matches with arrows, a keyboard shortcut for the search: not in the prototype and not in F19 (concern 1).
  - The page of an archived channel (concern 6).
  - Guests (phase 5, D-090, D-152).

## Requirements
- R1. One route, `GET /api/v1/conversations/{id}/search`, in the module `search` of TOKI-070 (a new file `conversation-routes.ts` of that module and one line in its `index.ts` that registers it; the route `GET /api/v1/search` and its files are not changed). It is registered in the `/api/v1` scope, so every request needs a valid session (TOKI-022 R24: 401, and 401 `account_deactivated`). After the session, in this order, as `GET .../messages` of TOKI-037 R1 does: (1) the schema of the path and of the query (R2): 400 `validation_failed`; (2) `getConversationFacts(db, actor.id, id)` of TOKI-028: `null`, and a private channel, a direct message or a group of which the actor is not a member, are 404 `not_found` with the same body as an unknown conversation, before any permission function runs; (3) `canReadConversation` of TOKI-027 R5: a person who is not a member of a public channel gets 403 `forbidden`; (4) the reads of R3 to R5. A member can search whatever the kind of conversation, whether it is archived, and whether the other person of a direct message is deactivated (all of them can be read, TOKI-027 R5, F26, F41). The route is made by `conversationSearchRoutes({ db, ...overrides })`, which takes the same kind of optional dependencies as `searchRoutes` of TOKI-070 R13 (the facts read, `listPeople`, the two id reads and the entry builder, each with the real one as default), so the checks of R4 and R5 can inject a fake builder and count reads. The route takes no body and no other parameter, answers with the header `cache-control: no-store`, and no code of it reads the role of the actor (TOKI-027 R16). Check: a table test sends to the route a request without a session (401, no read of `conversations`, `users` or `messages`), a deactivated account (401 `account_deactivated`), an invalid id (400), an unknown id (404), a private channel of others, a direct message of others and a group of others (404, the same body as the unknown id), a public channel the actor has not joined (403 `forbidden`), and each kind of conversation of the actor, an archived channel and a direct message with a deactivated person (200); the statement count of every refused case is 0 after the facts read of step 2; a source search finds no comparison with `"admin"` or `"member"` in the new file.
- R2. The query. `q` is required, one string, trimmed of white space at both ends (`String.prototype.trim`), and the trimmed text has `SEARCH_MESSAGE_MIN_LENGTH` (2, TOKI-070 R12) to `SEARCH_QUERY_MAX` (200) code points. `cursor` is optional, a non-empty string of at most `SEARCH_CURSOR_MAX` (512) characters. A repeated key, any other key, an empty or white-space-only `q`, a `q` of 1 code point, a `q` of 201, a `cursor` over the limit and a cursor that is not a messages cursor (R5) are 400 `validation_failed` with the path of the key in `details.issues` (the cursor with `invalid_cursor`, as TOKI-070 R8), and no read is made. The minimum of 2 is a proposal (concern 2): it is the minimum that F29 gives to the messages of Ctrl+K, and a one-letter prefix is the case that TOKI-069 R11 measures as slow. Check: a table test with `q` of 0, 1, 2 and 200 code points and of 201 (with 200 emoji, so that code points and UTF-16 units differ), a `q` of one letter followed by spaces (1, refused), a `q` of two combining marks (accepted by the schema, an empty page from R3), a repeated `q`, an unknown key, a cursor of 512 and of 513 characters (513 refused before decoding): status, code and `details.issues[0].path`; the statement count of each refused case is 0 after the session.
- R3. What matches. A message of the conversation is a result when it is a `user` message that is not deleted and either (a) its text matches by the rule of TOKI-069 R7 (every word of `searchWords(q)` is the start of a word of the message, case and diacritics ignored), through `buildSearchTsQuery(q)` and `searchMessageIds` of TOKI-069 R9 with `conversationIds` the one id of the path; or (b) its author is an account whose `displayName` contains the trimmed query, both folded with `foldText` of `@toki/shared` (TOKI-050 R2, the fold of TOKI-070 R6); when the folded query is empty (the trimmed query has 2 code points or more, but `foldText` removes all of them, as two combining marks do) no account matches, as TOKI-070 R6 says, because `includes("")` would be true for every name and return every message of the conversation: the accounts come come from `listPeople(client, workspaceId)` of the `members` module and include the deactivated ones, whose old messages stay (F41), and the signed-in person's own name counts like any other (the prototype's filter does the same). When `buildSearchTsQuery(q)` is `null` (no word, for example `!!`), only (b) applies. A reply of a thread of this conversation is a result like any message (concern 4). A message of another conversation, of another workspace, a system line and a deleted message are never results (the index leaves the last two out, TOKI-069 R2, and the read of R4 checks them again). Check, on a fixture of two conversations of the actor and one conversation of another person, with the messages of TOKI-069 R12 and the name `Nguyễn Văn Đạt` written in the precomposed and the decomposed form: `nguyen` finds the messages whose text has `Nguyễn` and the messages sent by `Nguyễn Văn Đạt`, in the one conversation asked and in no other; `duong dan` finds `đường dẫn`; `Nguyễn` and `nguyen` give the same page; a message of the other conversation of the actor, a message of the conversation of another person, a deleted message and a system line that contain the word are never in the page; a reply is in the page with its `parentId`; a message of a deactivated person is in the page and the person's name finds it; `!!` gives an empty page and no read of `search_vector`; a query of two combining marks (`U+0301 U+0301`) gives an empty page, no match of any account and no author read (the fold is empty, so no account is chosen); a name that matches no account and a word that matches no message give an empty page.
- R4. The read by author. `listMessageIdsByAuthors(client, input)` of `apps/server/src/modules/messages/author-search.ts`, exported by `index.ts` (no table is exported), takes `{ workspaceId, conversationId, authorIds, limit, before? }` and runs one statement over `messages` where `workspace_id`, `conversation_id` and `author_id = any($authorIds)` hold, `kind = 'user'`, `deleted_at is null` and, with `before`, `(created_at, id) < ($before.createdAt, $before.id)`, ordered by `created_at desc, id desc`, with `limit`, and returns `{ id, conversationId, createdAt }[]` as `searchMessageIds` does. `limit` is an integer from 1 to 50 and `authorIds` has 0 to 1,000 distinct ids; anything else throws before a statement is sent, and an empty list returns `[]` with none (a workspace with more than 1,000 accounts is outside D-113's 200 people, so the function refuses instead of cutting the list). It does not decide who may read the conversation: the route has checked it (R1). The route asks both reads for `limit` 21 and the same `before`, merges the two lists by (`createdAt`, `id`) newest first, drops a repeated id and keeps the first 21; the page is the first 20 and `nextCursor` is not null when a 21st exists. The text read of (a) and this one take the same `before`, so the merged list is the one a single query would give for rows written with a whole millisecond (TOKI-070 R8, TOKI-069 R9); the seed of TOKI-081 writes whole milliseconds. Check: a database test finds the rows of the given authors in the given conversation only, newest first with equal times broken by id, never a deleted message, a system line, a message of another conversation or of another workspace; `limit` 0 and 51, 1,001 ids and a repeated id throw with no statement; an empty list returns `[]` and sends none; the export test of the module lists the function and no table; a route test with 45 matching messages, some by text, some by author and some by both, reads three pages of 20, 20 and 5, each message once, in order, and the last `nextCursor` is null.
- R5. The answer and the cursor. 200 with `{ items: SearchMessage[], nextCursor: string | null }` and no other key; the item is `ConversationSearchMessage` (R6), the strict object `{ id, conversationId, authorId, parentId, createdAt, text, textTruncated }`: the `SearchMessage` of TOKI-070 R7 without `conversationName`. Decided in this spec: the name is dropped from this route's item, because the route is for one conversation, the person has it open and its name is in the header, so the row shows none (R12) and no read of the name is needed (the four reads after the facts stay four). The other keys are built as TOKI-070 R7 builds them: the entries come from `buildMessageEntriesByIds(db, workspaceId, [id], ids)` of TOKI-070 R9 for the first 20 ids of the merged list, an entry that is deleted, not a `user` message, or with no row is dropped (so a page can be shorter than 20 while `nextCursor` is not null, and the client asks again), the `text` is the first `SEARCH_TEXT_MAX` (300) code points and `textTruncated` says it was longer, and no reaction, file, pin or mention is read. `nextCursor` is the encoding of TOKI-070 R8 for messages, base64url of `[createdAtIso, id]` of the last id read among the first 20 (the position read and not the position shown, so a page whose items were all dropped still advances); the functions that encode and decode it are those of `cursor.ts` of the same module. A cursor made for names (three elements) or malformed is 400 `validation_failed` with `invalid_cursor`; a cursor made with another query is a position in this query's order and gives a valid page, which reveals nothing because every page is read from this one conversation. An empty result is 200 with `{ items: [], nextCursor: null }`. The page size is `SEARCH_PAGE_SIZE` (20) of TOKI-070 R12 (concern 3). Check: the body of every 200 is parsed with the schema of R6 and has no `conversationName`; 301 code points are cut to 300 with `textTruncated` true and 300 are not; an emoji at the cut is not split; a fake builder that returns no entry for the 20 ids gives an empty page with a `nextCursor` that the next call follows to the next ids; each malformed cursor, a names cursor and a cursor of 513 characters are 400 and never 500; a cursor past the end gives an empty page with `nextCursor` null.

## Design
### Why a route of its own
`GET /api/v1/search` of TOKI-070 has no conversation filter: its query takes only `q`, `kind` and `cursor` (R2, any other key is 400), it reads the messages of every conversation the actor belongs to (R7), it never answers 403 or 404 about data (R11 (a)), and it does not match the sender; its Scope and its "Contract for the tickets that follow" give the search in one conversation, which also matches the sender, to this ticket. Decided in this spec (concern 7): this ticket adds its own route and changes nothing of that one. A filter on that route would need a change of its strict query, of its answer for a refused case and of its permission steps (a single conversation follows the message history's 404 and 403, TOKI-037 R1, not the palette's "always 200"), and it would still need the author read.
```
GET /api/v1/conversations/{id}/search?q=nguyen[&cursor=...]
  session -> schema -> getConversationFacts (404) -> canReadConversation (403)
  (one READ ONLY, REPEATABLE READ transaction from here)
  S1 listPeople ------------> accounts whose folded name contains the folded q  -> authorIds
  S2 searchMessageIds ------> ids by text   (conversationIds: [id], limit 21, before)   [if buildSearchTsQuery(q)]
  S3 listMessageIdsByAuthors -> ids by sender (limit 21, before)                        [if authorIds not empty]
  merge by (createdAt, id) -> first 20 ids (+ the 21st tells nextCursor)
  S4 buildMessageEntriesByIds(db, ws, [id], ids) -> SearchMessage[] (text cut at 300)
```
The facts and the four reads run in one `READ ONLY` transaction at `REPEATABLE READ`, opened by this ticket's `conversation-service.ts` itself with the database client (`BEGIN READ ONLY ISOLATION LEVEL REPEATABLE READ`, then the reads, then `COMMIT`, not counted as statements), as TOKI-070 R10 does for its own route: TOKI-070 names no helper for it, and it is not the `inReadSnapshot` of `bootstrap`, which no other module imports, so nothing is asked of TOKI-070 here, so a person removed from the conversation while the request runs gets nothing that the snapshot does not hold. The statements of a request are at most 5 (the facts, then the four reads S1 to S4), 0 for the reads when the schema or the facts refuse.

## Data safety
- The route reads conversation messages and names and writes nothing. The answer is personal and is not cached (R1). The text of a search is in the query string: the log line of a request holds the method, the path without the query, the status and the duration (TOKI-004 R7), the route adds no log line, an unexpected error is a 500 `internal_error` from the module's `safe-error.ts` with no text of the query in the body, and a 404 or 403 body names no conversation (TOKI-070 R11 (b), (c), (d) apply to this route, TOKI-037 R23). Check: a forced failure of each read gives a body with no text of the query and a captured log with none; the body of a 404 for a private channel of others equals that of an unknown id.
- The permission rule is the steps of R1 and the single conversation id of the reads: no read is given an id of another conversation, and the entries are read with the conversation id again (TOKI-070 R9 b). The typed text never leaves the browser except as the query of this request; it is not kept in the address, the history or the storage (R8).

## Areas of concern
1. The form of the result: the list filtered in place, a list of results, or a highlight with stepping. Not decided. The prototype filters the message list and has no result to press, no highlight and no stepping (`msgsHtml`, line 1327 of `index.html`); F19 says the list keeps the matching messages and then "press a result: search mode closes, the view scrolls to that message and flashes it"; the plan line says "filters messages". Proposal, not a decision (R9 to R12): a list of results, one row each, drawn in the place of the list, and a press on a row shows the message in the list. 2. From how many characters it searches. Not decided. F29 gives 2 for the messages of Ctrl+K; F19 gives none and the prototype filters from the first character. Proposal, not a decision (R2, R10): 2 code points, the same minimum, because the word rule is a prefix and a one-letter prefix is the case that TOKI-069 R11 measures at 335 ms over 1,000,000 messages; below 2 a line asks for more and the list stays. The effect for the owner to see: a contains test on any part of the name with a minimum of 2 means that the query `an` matches `Lan`, `Hoàng` and `Anh` and returns all of their messages in this conversation; the prototype does the same.
