# New divider, badges and filters design

Date: 2026-11-23 · Status: draft for review (concerns 2 to 6 are open for the product owner)
Intent: docs/intents/new-message-divider.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-054

## Goal
After the read pointer (TOKI-053) the client knows, for every conversation, how many messages are unread, whether one mentions the person, and which message is the first unread. Nothing yet turns that into what a person sees as a whole. This work does it: the New divider and the opening at the first unread message, the @ mark of a row with the rule for a muted conversation, the Home number in the navigation, the unread count in the browser tab title, and the Unread and Mentions filters, which the device still keeps switched off.
The hard rule is that the divider is anchored by message id at the opening and never moves, whatever arrives later. The conversation page reads the first unread message once, in the first render that has the store ready, and keeps it for as long as the conversation page is mounted: through a tab round trip, until the conversation is left (R7 to R9). The second rule is that every number comes from one set of pure functions in `client-core` (technical design: what to show lives there), so the web interface and a phone interface cannot disagree (R1).

## Design
### Where the decisions live
Every number and every rule is a pure function in `packages/client-core/src/badges/`, called by the store (`getList`, `getBadges`) so that every reader of the store, the sidebar, the shell and the title, sees the same value. `apps/web` holds only wiring: one hook for the divider, one for the title, and one prop of the shell. That is the technical design's rule that "what to show" lives in `client-core`.

### The data it reads
- The slot of TOKI-032 R20, filled from the answers of TOKI-053 (R20) (`{ count, mention, firstUnreadId }`). `count` is the main-list messages of other people after the pointer, with no system line and no thread reply (TOKI-053 R4); `mention` is the @ mark, which a mention inside a thread reply also raises (F13).
- `isMuted` (TOKI-031 R7) and the level of the conversation entry (TOKI-031 R8).
- `mentionsInMuted`, set from outside (R2).

### The divider
The first unread message is in the store's slot, filled by TOKI-053, which empties it when the list has reported the bottom. The page must therefore read it at the opening, before the list has reported anything, and keep it. The hook keeps it in state; the page gives it to the list as the opening place (an `openAt` of kind `new`, dropped when the opening ends) and, separately, as `newFromId`, which it keeps while the conversation is viewed. The list draws the divider before that message and opens there. Because the list receives an id and not an index, a message loaded above it, a day divider or a hidden message cannot move it.

```
store slot unread[id].firstUnreadId --once--> useNewDivider --> newFromId
                                                                   |
       ConversationPage: openAt (dropped at onOpened), newFromId (kept) --+--> MessageList: New row before message, open there
store.getList / getBadges --> ConversationListPane rows ; AppShellRoute badge ; useTabTitle -> document.title
```

### The tab title
D-122 gives the form `(3) Toki` for mentions and direct messages and a dot for anything else. A mention is known as a mark and not as a number of messages, so the count of R1 (c) is a proposal: a direct message adds its unread messages and a channel with a mark adds one. The dot and the rule for when it shows are proposals too (concern 2, concern 3).

## Testing
- Written test-first. The rules of R1 are unit tests with tables and no store or DOM; the store changes (R2, R4) are store tests with fixtures of conversations; the hooks are tested with a fake store and a fake `document`; the page test (R8 to R10) uses the real list over the fake layout of TOKI-041 R20 (with the change request of R9 applied to the list), so the claim that the divider is drawn, kept (through `onOpened` and a tab round trip) and dropped is proved by what the list draws and not by a spy.
- No test waits for a real timer; none of this work uses time. The store and the clock are fakes (TOKI-008 controls the clock where one is needed).
- Each "shown" case has a "not shown" case next to it: muted with and without the switch, a divider with and without unread messages, a title with a count, with a dot and with neither.
- The browser test is R12. Fake names and counts only.

## Open questions from the intent
- Does the divider appear for messages that arrive while the conversation is open? Answered: no. The divider is anchored at the opening and stays put while the conversation is viewed (D-052; F10: "stays put until the conversation is left"); R7, R8, R9.
- Is a mention in a muted conversation counted in the Home badge? Answered: no. A muted conversation is left out of the Home badge, and the mark stays only on its row (journey "mention in a muted conversation": "Not counted in the Home badge"; F08); R1 (b).
- Does the Home badge add up messages or conversations? Answered: messages. F08 says "total unread", and a new message raises it by 1 (journey of a mention); R1 (b).
- What number does the tab title show: messages, conversations, or mentions? Not answered. Carried forward as concern 2.
- When does the tab title show the dot, and which character? Not answered. Carried forward as concern 3.
- Is a displayed number capped? Not answered. Carried forward as concern 4.
- Does the Mentions filter keep a muted conversation whose @ mark is hidden? Not answered. Carried forward as concern 5.
- When a conversation opens at a given message and also has unread messages, which one does the list open at? Not answered. Carried forward as concern 6.
- Does a muted conversation still get a divider? Answered: yes. Mute hides the badge and the bold and nothing else (D-103); the count is kept (TOKI-053 R19); R10 (b).

## Areas of concern
1. The rule for a muted conversation and the switch. The rule is Decided by D-088 (Confirmed, "a personal switch in Preferences, on by default"), D-103 and F09: no count, no bold, out of the Home badge, and the @ mark only while the switch is on. Where the value comes from is Decided in this spec: the store holds `mentionsInMuted`, default true, and a setter; the preference's own ticket (TOKI-102, through `bindMentionsInMuted`) calls it; the screen, TOKI-078, does not. Reason: the saved preference does not exist yet, and the default of D-088 is the value until it does. Owner: Thanh Truong.
2. What number the tab title shows. Not decided. D-122 says `(3) Toki` "counts mentions and DMs" and does not say whether it counts messages, conversations or mentions, nor whether a muted conversation counts. The data holds a mention as a mark and not as a number. Proposal, not a decision: the unread messages of unmuted direct messages and groups, plus one for each channel that shows an @ mark (R1 (c)); a muted direct message adds nothing; a muted channel adds one only while the switch for mentions in muted conversations is on. The other readings (the number of conversations, or the sum of all counts of Home) change `titleBadge` only. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
3. The dot of the tab title. Not decided. D-122 says "a dot for anything else" and gives neither the character nor what "anything else" is. Proposal, not a decision: the character `•` before the name, shown when the number is 0 and an unmuted conversation has unread messages; Activity and Later items are not looked at here, because their lists belong to other tickets. A page that also shows the Activity badge would need a source from TOKI-065. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
4. The largest number a badge shows. Not decided. The prototype draws the number as it is, the component has no cap (TOKI-012 concern 4) and TOKI-053 concern 11 leaves the cap to this work; no decision sets one. Proposal, not a decision: no cap, as the prototype does; a cap such as `99+` would be one change in the wiring (R4) and in the row label of TOKI-033. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
5. Whether the Mentions filter keeps a muted conversation whose @ mark is hidden by the switch. Not decided. D-074 says a count on a chip equals the rows shown, which argues for keeping only rows that draw the mark; no decision says it for this case. Proposal, not a decision: the filter keeps the rows that draw the mark (R3 (b)). The other reading, that the filter keeps every row with a mention regardless of the switch, changes one line of the store. The Unread filter keeps a muted conversation with a count, decided in TOKI-032 concern 7. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
6. Which wins when a conversation opens with unread messages and a target message. Not decided. D-052 says opening a conversation with unread messages scrolls to the divider; the journeys of Activity, Pins and Search say the conversation opens at that message. The list takes one `openAt`. Proposal, not a decision: the target wins and no divider is drawn in that mount (R10 (a)); the count stays until the person reaches the bottom. The other reading draws the divider and puts the target in view, which needs the list to take both. Owner: Thanh Truong (product owner).
7. Who turns the Unread and Mentions filters on. Settled by TOKI-033 concern 21 (the owner, 2026-11-20): TOKI-053 sets `readStateAvailable`. This spec does not take the line over. TOKI-053's text does not hold the name, so the change request to TOKI-053 (Plan changes) asks it to set the constant; this ticket needs TOKI-053 and checks the result (R3 (c)). Owner: Thanh Truong.
8. How the divider outlives the opening. Decided by D-052 ("stays while viewing", F10): the divider is drawn at the opening, stays through a round trip to another tab, and is gone at the next opening. TOKI-041 R4 chose that a tab return repeats no divider, which D-052 does not say. The way to keep both is technical and is a change request to TOKI-041 (Plan changes): the page keeps the id as `newFromId`, separate from the `openAt` it drops at `onOpened` (R9). Owner: Thanh Truong.
9. Where the first unread message is read. Decided in this spec: once, in the first render with a ready store, from the store's slot, and kept by the hook, keyed by conversation (R7). Reason: TOKI-053 R19 empties the value after the list reports the bottom, so a later read would lose the divider, a read at every render would move it, and a hook in a page that is not remounted must not hand the old conversation's id to the new one. Owner: Thanh Truong.
10. Pause and Focus time. Decided by D-072: "Badges still update", so the Home number and the tab title follow the counts during both, and the hook of R5 reads neither. Owner: Thanh Truong.
