# Conversation header and Details panel design

Date: 2026-11-17 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/conversation-header-details.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-035

## Goal
A person who opens a conversation needs to see what it is and to set it up for themselves. This work builds the top row of an open conversation (name, topic, star, head count, a Back button on a narrow screen) and the Details panel behind its members button: the facts about the conversation, who is in it, the person's own notification level, mute and star, the in-place edit of a channel's topic and description, and Leave. Leaving is the one action here where the two patterns of TOKI-093 meet: a public channel is left at once with Undo for 5 seconds, a private channel only after a confirmation that says a member must invite the person back, and #general cannot be left. Browser Back closes an open panel before it leaves the conversation.

## Scope
- In:
  - `ConversationHeader` and `DetailsPanel` in `apps/web/src/conversation/` (Design, "Layout"), their hooks, their CSS (the prototype's `.mh`, `.setrow`, `.opt` and `.mrow` rules) and their tests.
  - The panel's address rule: how it opens, how Back and Close close it (R8, R9).
  - The facts of a channel, read through the store and the client of TOKI-032 (the commands `readChannel`, `editChannel`, `leaveChannel`, `joinChannel`, the member list `api.channels.members`, and the lookups of the person's own entry, the people and the names), which call the routes of TOKI-029.
  - The controls for notification level, mute and star, and the seam they call to save (R19). The request behind the seam is not built here; TOKI-029 owns it (concern 1).
  - Leave: the public and the private way, the last-member warning of a private and of a public channel, the move to #general afterwards (R22 to R26).
  - The `conversation` i18n namespace and two keys of `common` (R28), English and Vietnamese. The `.mrow` rule is written here (Table C; Decided in this spec): TOKI-034 draws its own lists and uses its own class, not `.mrow`, so the two files do not define one selector twice (Design, "Hand-offs").
- Out (not now):
  - The conversation list, the conversation's address `/c/:id`, the route that mounts the page and its `ConversationPage` file, and the list row (grey, no count, no bold, starred group, order): TOKI-033. This work adds one thing to that route, the search parameter `panel` (R8), and fills the body of `ConversationPage` (Design, "How the page uses these pieces").
  - Browse channels, Join, and the people picker: TOKI-034; it adds nothing to the panel, because a channel the person has not joined has no header or panel (Design, "Hand-offs"). Add people and Remove a person (the buttons and the picker): TOKI-100, through the slots of `PeopleSection` (R13). Archive: TOKI-101, one section of the panel (R11); Restore is in Browse channels (TOKI-034), not in the panel.
  - The request that saves a person's notification level, mute and star: TOKI-029 owns it, as one route for every kind of conversation (concern 1).
  - The search field and button in the header: TOKI-072. The Meet button: phase 4. The tab row (Messages, Files & links, Pinned) under the header: the shell gives `TabList` (TOKI-015), TOKI-048 adds the Pinned tab, and TOKI-041 draws the row with the Messages tab (concern 7).
  - The profile that opens when a member row is pressed, and the person's presence, job title and status in the header's second line and in the rows: TOKI-057, TOKI-055, TOKI-056 (Design, "Hand-offs"). A group's second line is not built either: the prototype writes it as a fixed sample text (`Group conversation with Alex and Thu`, line 1285), no requirement gives a rule for it and the header shows none for a group.
  - The grey system lines ("Lan left the channel", a topic change): no ticket owns them (TOKI-029 concern 4). The effect of mute and of the levels on counts, toasts, push and sound: TOKI-054 (count, bold, badge) and TOKI-073 (toast, push, sound). Unread counts: TOKI-053.
  - Live updates of an open header or panel when another person changes the topic, joins or leaves: the sync engine, TOKI-040 (Design, "Hand-offs").
  - Guests, Email this channel and Automations, which the prototype's panel draws: phases 5 and 4. The panel has no row for them.
  - Renaming a channel and switching it between public and private: not built (D-105, D-040).

## Requirements
Header
- R1. `ConversationHeader` renders a `div.mh` holding, in this order: `button.ib.back` (the Back button, R5); `div.grow` holding `h1.ell` (the glyph of R2, `span.ell` with the name, and the star of R6 when the person starred the conversation) and, when R3 gives one, `div.topic.ell`; and `button.btn.ghost.memb` (R4). It draws no search button, no Meet button and no tab. Its texts come from `useTranslation` (R28). It is drawn for every kind of conversation that the store holds. Check: a markup test for a public channel with a topic, a private channel with none, a DM and a group, reading the classes and the order; a source scan finds no `Meet` and no search control.
- R2. The glyph and the name. A public channel: the `hash` icon; a private channel: the `lock` icon; a DM: an `Avatar` (size 28, hue `personHue(id)` of TOKI-033 R28) of the other person (the id of `memberIds` that is not the signed-in person's, `getUser().id`); a group: the `users` icon. The name is `getConversationName(id)` of the store (TOKI-032 R17): the channel's name, the other person's `displayName`, or the full `displayName` of the other people of a group, ordered and joined with `", "` by the store. No name is worked out in this work. How a group is named is concern 11 here (TOKI-032 concern 12), Decided: the group of the prototype reads `Alex Phạm, Thu Hoàng` and not the prototype's `Alex, Thu`, and that text, the store's, is the decision of concern 11; a long name is cut by the ellipsis of `h1.ell`. A deactivated person is still named. The name of a channel never gets a `#` in the header (the prototype draws the icon); the icon is `aria-hidden`, so the heading reads as the name. When the store holds no entry for the id (the person has just left, R25) the header draws nothing. Check: the four kinds, the exact `textContent` of the heading (`project-launch`; `Mai`; `Alex Phạm, Thu Hoàng`, whatever the store's `getConversationName` returns), a deactivated person in a DM, the icons by name, and an id with no entry (nothing drawn).
- R3. The second line. For a channel it is the topic when the topic is not null and not empty, and no `div.topic` otherwise. A DM has none for now: the person's status or job title and presence need TOKI-055, TOKI-056 and TOKI-057, which add that line when they have the data (Design, "Hand-offs"). A group has none and no ticket builds one (Scope, Out: the prototype's text is a fixed sample). The topic is plain text: `<`, `&` and a link are shown as typed, never as markup. Check: topic present, null, an empty string, `<b>x</b>` shown as text; a DM and a group have no `div.topic`.
- R4. The members button. It is a `Button` of TOKI-012 (`variant="ghost"`, class `memb`) that holds a `UsersIcon` and a `span.mono` with the head count, named `t("conversation.header.details")` ("Members and details", the prototype's own label), `aria-expanded` equal to whether the panel is open and `aria-controls` equal to `conversation-details`, the id of the `div` that `DetailsPanel` puts around its sections inside the `SidePanel` (R11), which is the only element of the panel this work can give an id: `SidePanel` and the `aside` belong to TOKI-015 and take none. A press opens the panel (R8) and, while it is open, closes it. It is drawn at every width and has no hover-only part (D-067). The count is `getMemberCount(id)` of the store (TOKI-032 R23): `memberIds.length` for a DM or group, the `memberCount` of the held detail for a channel; while it is undefined (the detail of R7 has not come back) the button shows no number and is still pressable. Check: the name and `aria-expanded` in both states; the count for a channel of 3, a DM (2) and a group of 8; no number while the read is pending; a press on a viewport of 360 px opens the panel; the id named by `aria-controls` is in the document while the panel is open.
- R5. The Back button. `button.ib.back` is an `IconButton` (`icon="left"`) named `t("conversation.header.back")` ("Back to conversations", the prototype's). It calls the prop `onBack`, which the page gives (Design, "How the page uses these pieces"; the route and the list are TOKI-033's). `conversation.css` holds the prototype's rule `.mh .back{display:none}` and, in a `@media (max-width: 719px)` block, `.mh .back{display:inline-grid}`: the button shows under 720 px and not from 720 px, the width at which the shell becomes a single pane (TOKI-015 R4, R16). Check: a press calls `onBack` once; a CSS test (`readCss`, TOKI-012 R27) holds both rules and the media condition; the rule is a `display` pair and needs no measurement.
- R6. The star. When the entry's `isStarred` is true, the heading holds a `span.later-ic` with the `star` icon and an accessible name `t("conversation.header.starred")`; when false, nothing. Another person's star is never shown (D-143): the entry is the signed-in person's own (TOKI-031 R8). Check: both values; the name of the star.
- R7. Where the header reads. The name, topic, kind, `isPrivate`, `isGeneral`, `isStarred` and `memberIds` come from the conversation entry of the store `conversations` of `apps/web/src/client.ts` (TOKI-032 R25: `getConversation(id)`), read in React through `useConversationSelector(select)` of TOKI-033 (`apps/web/src/conversation-list/use-conversations.ts`, its R29 and its "Contract for the screens"), the one binding of the store, so a change that the store receives (a saved topic, a new star, the person leaving) redraws the header with no reload. `select` receives the store and returns `select(store)`; a value that is a new object at every call would redraw without end, so each value is asked for in its own call, with a selector that returns the same object or a primitive while nothing it reads changes (TOKI-032 R22): `(s) => s.getConversation(id)`, `(s) => s.getConversationName(id)`, `(s) => s.getChannelDetail(id)`, `(s) => s.getMemberCount(id)`, `(s) => s.getPerson(userId)`. `useConversation(id)` in `useConversation.ts` makes the first four calls and returns `{ entry, name, detail, memberCount }`; this work writes no other hook over the store. The `description`, `createdBy`, `createdAt`, `archived` and `memberCount` come from the held detail of the channel, `getChannelDetail(id)`, which this work asks for with the store command `readChannel(id)` (TOKI-032 R14: it calls `GET /channels/{id}` and stores the answer) once when a channel's header is first drawn and again when the panel is opened; the first load holds none of them (TOKI-031 R7, R12). A DM and a group have no detail and no read. TOKI-032 does not make two `readChannel` calls for one id share one request (its R10 shares only `load()` and `refresh()`), so `useConversation.ts` does: it keeps the promise of the call in flight for each id, and a second call for the same id while it runs gets that promise. A failure of the header's read is not shown there (the header keeps the entry's data); the panel reads again when it opens and reports it (R14). Decided in this spec: the store already keeps the detail (TOKI-032 R14), so no second cache is made and TanStack Query is not used for it. Check: with a fake store and a fake `api`, one header and one panel on a channel make one `readChannel` between them when opened together (a second call while one runs is not sent); a DM makes none; saving the topic redraws the header; a store change from outside redraws it.

The panel: opening, closing and Back
- R8. Opening. The panel is open when the conversation's address carries the search parameter `panel` with the value `details`: `/c/{conversationId}?panel=details`. A press on the members button (R4) when the panel is closed navigates to that address with a new history entry (`push`, not `replace`) and `state: { detailsPushed: true }`. Any other value of `panel` is dropped: the `validateSearch` of `conversationRoute` (`src/routes/conversation.tsx`, TOKI-033 R21) returns `panel: "details"` or no `panel`; this work adds that one function to the route and nothing else. Opening the panel on a conversation whose address is a thread (`/c/{conversationId}/t/{messageId}`, TOKI-045) leaves the thread: the panel and a thread are one side slot (`useShellPanel`, TOKI-033 R26: the last call wins), so the address becomes `/c/{conversationId}?panel=details`. Opening another conversation from the list goes to its address, which has no `panel`, so the panel closes (the prototype does the same, `openConv`); the first conversation's entry, with `?panel=details`, stays in the history below it. Check: with the router on a memory history, a press pushes one entry whose search is `{ panel: "details" }` and whose state has `detailsPushed`; `?panel=other` shows no panel and the parameter is dropped; from a thread address the entry has no thread segment; a click on another conversation closes the panel; browser Back after that returns to the first conversation with its panel open and one more Back closes it (Decided in this spec: this is D-089 read as the history shows it, "Back closes the side panel first" holds on each conversation's own entry).
- R9. Closing. The panel's Close button (from 720 px), its Back button (under 720 px) and Escape (TOKI-015 R11 calls `onClose` for all three), and a second press on the members button, all call one function, `closeDetails`. When the current entry's `state.detailsPushed` is true, it calls `history.back()`; when it is not (the page was opened on `?panel=details`, a link or a reload that lost the state), it navigates to the same conversation without `panel` with `replace: true`. So browser Back, the panel's own button and Escape all end on `/c/{conversationId}` with the panel closed, and Back never leaves the conversation while the panel is open (D-089). Check, on a memory history: open then press Close, open then `history.back()`, open then Escape: each ends on `/c/{conversationId}` with no panel and the history one entry back, not two; a page opened on `?panel=details` and closed leaves the history length unchanged and shows no panel; after two opens and two closes the history has no extra entries.
- R10. One panel at a time, in the shell's slot. `ConversationPage` calls `useShellPanel({ label, content })` (TOKI-033 R26) with `label` `t("conversation.panel.label")` and `content` the `DetailsPanel` while the address carries `panel=details`, and `useShellPanel(null)` otherwise; the slot clears when the page unmounts. `content` is a new element at every render of the page, which `useShellPanel` allows (TOKI-033 R26: the provider keeps it outside React state, so the page does not render in a loop). `DetailsPanel` gets `conversationId` and `onNoAccess` (R14) as props, since it is created by the page. The shell's rules apply and are not repeated: from 720 to 1099 px the panel overlays the chat, from 1100 px it is a column, under 720 px it is the whole screen (TOKI-015 R19, R22). Check: a page test with the real `AppShellRoute` provider: the `data-panel` and `data-pane="side"` attributes of the shell with the panel open on a narrow window, the panel's name, and a cleared slot after the page unmounts.

- R11. The frame. `DetailsPanel` renders a `SidePanel` (TOKI-015 R11) with `title` `t("conversation.panel.titleDetails")` ("Details") for a channel and a group, and `t("conversation.panel.titleProfile")` ("Profile") for a DM (the prototype's titles), `labels.back` from `common.back` and `labels.close` from `common.closePanel` (R28). Its body holds, in this order, the sections of Table A, each with an `h3`: About; Members (a channel and a group; for a DM the person's row, R13); Notifications; Settings. The Leave button (R21) is the last element of the body and has no heading of its own (the prototype has none, line 1452). TOKI-101 adds one more block, `ArchiveSection` (an Admin's Archive button, no heading), between Settings and Leave, so the order of the body is About, Members, Notifications, Settings, Archive, Leave; `DetailsPanel` renders it for a channel and gives it its own `onNoAccess` (R10), and Leave stays last. The sections sit in one `div` with the id `conversation-details` (R4). It holds no Guests, Email or Automations section. Check: the headings for each kind, in order, and the title for each kind; the Leave button is the last element of the body.
- R12. About. A `div` with the glyph (`hash` or `lock`) and the name at 16 px bold; the topic (the second line of `.sm`, `color:var(--ink-2)`); the description as a `p.sm.muted`; and `div.xs.muted` with `t("conversation.panel.createdBy", { name, date })` ("Created by {name} · {date}", the prototype's form). For a channel: the creator's name is the `displayName` of `createdBy` from the people of the store (a deactivated creator is named; a creator the store does not hold is left out with its `Created by` part, and the date alone is shown); `date` is `formatDate(createdAt, ctx)` of TOKI-094 R2 with `weekday: false` and no year in the same year (R3 of that spec), where `ctx` is `{ timeZone, now, language, dayWords }`: `timeZone` is the browser's (`Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`) until a person's own zone is stored (TOKI-078; Decided in this spec, D-144 gives the profile's zone, not a rule for this date), `now` is read from the clock of TOKI-008, `language` is the person's language, and `dayWords` is the three `common` day words, which this line does not show (`formatDate` does not use them). A `null` from `formatDate` (R7 of that spec) leaves the date out. A channel with a null topic shows the line `t("conversation.panel.noTopic")`, one with a null description shows `t("conversation.panel.noDescription")`, both in `.muted`. For a DM the line under the name is `t("conversation.panel.directConversation")` ("Direct conversation", the prototype's) and nothing else; for a group the same line. Check: a channel with all fields (`Created by Lan · 8 Jan`), the next year (`8 Jan 2026`), a null creator, a creator that is deactivated, an invalid `createdAt` (no date), null topic and null description, a DM, a group; the zone is passed as the test's own (`Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` against `America/New_York` moves a date near midnight).
- R13. The people. For a channel, `Members · {count}` (`t("conversation.panel.members", { count })`, the prototype's form, with `getMemberCount(id)`) and a list of rows. A row is a `div.mrow` that holds, in this order, `div.mrow-main` (an `Avatar` of size 32, the person's `displayName` and hue `personHue(id)` of TOKI-033 R28, the name in bold, and the `Tag` `t("conversation.panel.deactivated")` when the person is deactivated) and the slot `row(person)` of the paragraph below, empty unless TOKI-100 gives one. The content of a row sits in `div.mrow-main`, so that TOKI-057, which makes the rows pressable, makes that part and not `div.mrow` the pressable element, and a control in the slot never sits inside a `button`. The rows come from `useChannelMembers(id)`, which returns `{ rows, ids, status, error, reload }`: `rows` are the people drawn, `ids` the `userId` of every page read (the ids the store does not know included), `status` is `"loading"` only until the first read has answered, then `"ready"`, or `"failed"` when the first read failed and no row is held, and `error` is the last `ApiError` (or null). It calls `api.channels.members(id, { limit: 100, cursor })` of the client (TOKI-032 R9; 100 is the server's maximum, TOKI-029 R8) page after page, following `nextCursor` until it is null, so 200 people (NFR-04) take 2 requests, 201 take 3, and it stops after 10 requests, keeping what it has and raising no error. It turns each `userId` into a person with `getPerson` of the store; an id the store does not know is left out of `rows` (not an error: the people and the member list are two reads). `reload()` is the "Retry" of R14, one function under one name: it reads again from the first page, replaces the held `rows` and `ids` only when every page has answered (a half-loaded list is never drawn), and while rows are held it never sets `status` to `"loading"`; a failed `reload` with rows held keeps them, and only a `no_access` error is applied (`readFailure`, R14). The list shows the first 50 rows and 50 more each time the panel body is scrolled to within 100 px of its end (D-109); the 50 is the window of the screen and not the size of a request. When the list has more than 50 rows a search field (`Field`, `variant="search"`, `t("conversation.panel.membersSearch")`) is drawn above it and filters the rows by name, containing the text, ignoring case; no match shows `t("conversation.panel.membersNone")` centred in the section (D-074). `PeopleSection` owns the search text and gives its owner `clearSearch()`. The rows are not buttons until TOKI-057 gives them a profile to open (Design, "Hand-offs"). For a group the rows come from `memberIds`, with no request and no search field. For a DM there is no list: the section is the one row of the other person, with the heading `t("conversation.panel.person")` ("Person"), which TOKI-057 replaces by the profile card (F24, Design, "Hand-offs"). Check: a channel of 3, one of 120 members (50 rows, then 100, then 120 after two scrolls; 2 requests of `limit` 100), one of 200 (2 requests) and one of 201 (3 requests), a list that still has a cursor after 10 requests (it stops and shows its rows), the search field absent at 50 and present at 51, a filter that matches and one that matches nothing, an unknown id (in `ids` and not in `rows`), a deactivated member, a group of 3, a DM, the markup of a row (`div.mrow-main` first, the slot after it), `reload()` that keeps the rows while it runs and replaces them only after every page has answered, and a failed `reload` with rows held.

  The optional prop `Controls` (the seam TOKI-100 uses). `PeopleSection` takes `Controls?: ComponentType<{ conversationId: string; members: { ids: string[]; status: "loading" | "ready" | "failed"; reload: () => Promise<void>; clearSearch: () => void }; children: (slots: { heading: ReactNode; row: (person: Person) => ReactNode; dialogs: ReactNode }) => ReactNode }>`. When it is given and the conversation is a channel, `PeopleSection` renders `<Controls ...>` and uses the slots: `heading` directly under the `h3` and above the search field, `row(person)` as the last child of each `div.mrow`, and `dialogs` once after the list. Without `Controls`, and for a DM and a group, the section is as above and draws no slot. `DetailsPanel` passes `Controls` through to the section and holds no other knowledge of what it draws. Check: a test `Controls` that draws a marker in each slot shows the three places and receives `ids`, `status`, `reload` and a working `clearSearch`; without `Controls` the tests above pass unchanged.
- R14. Loading and failure of the reads. While the detail of R7 is not held, About shows the name and topic of the entry and no description, creator or date; while `useChannelMembers` is loading, the section shows `t("conversation.panel.membersLoading")`. A failed read is sorted by `classifyApiError(error)` of TOKI-032 R8 (D-131): `no_access` (403, 404: the person is not or no longer a member) navigates to the not-found view (TOKI-006 now, the shared page of TOKI-082 later) by throwing the router's `notFound()`; `unauthenticated` and `ignore` do nothing here (the client's hook and the aborted request are not this panel's); `unreadable` (a 2xx answer that does not fit the schema: a repeat fails the same way, TOKI-032 R8) shows `t("conversation.panel.readFailed")` in the place of the section and no Retry; every other kind (`retry`, `fresh_copy`, `wait`, `field`, `unknown`) shows `t("conversation.panel.readFailed")` and a `Button` `t("conversation.panel.retry")` that reads again. For the members list the Retry is `reload` of `useChannelMembers` (R13), and when rows are held a failure other than `no_access` shows nothing and keeps the rows. A read with no answer after 10 seconds is a failure (D-126): the client raises it (TOKI-032 R5). The rest of the panel stays usable. The function is `readFailure(error)` in `readFailure.ts`; the writes of R16, R19, R22, R23 and R26 call it for their `no_access`. Check: with a fake `api` each kind (403, 404, 409, 429, 500, `network`, `timeout`, `aborted`, `invalid_response`) for the detail read and for the members read; the retry reads again once; the settings stay usable while the members read fails.

Editing the topic and description
- R15. Who may edit and where. A channel that is not archived shows, under About, a `Button` `t("conversation.panel.edit")` ("Edit"), when `canEditChannelDetails(getUser(), facts)` of `@toki/shared` (TOKI-027 R9, which defines it; TOKI-029 R9 only calls it) allows it: a member of a public or private channel, either role, that is not archived (D-105). `facts` is `{ kind, isGeneral, archived, isMember: true }`, with `kind` (`public_channel` or `private_channel`) and `isGeneral` from the entry and `archived` from the held detail; while the detail is not held `archived` is missing, the function answers `fact_missing` and Edit is not drawn (TOKI-027 R4: a missing fact denies), and it appears when the detail arrives; this work compares no role itself (TOKI-027 R16); a press replaces the topic and the description by two fields in place: `Field` "Topic" (single line) and `Field` "Description" (`multiline`), each with a visible label, filled with the current values, and the buttons `t("conversation.panel.save")` and `t("common.cancel")`. A DM and a group have no Edit button (they have no topic, TOKI-028 R6). The name has no field and no control anywhere (D-105). An archived channel is R30. Check: Edit is present for a Member and for an Admin, absent in a DM, a group, an archived channel and while no detail is held; no `input` or `textarea` is named for the name of the channel in any state.
- R16. Saving. `Save` is disabled while nothing differs from the stored text after trimming, while the topic is invalid, and while a request runs (D-104, D-111). The topic is valid when `isValidChannelTopic` of `packages/shared` (TOKI-028 R15) accepts it after the trim: an empty text is valid (it becomes null), a text of 1 to `CHANNEL_TOPIC_MAX` (250) code points is valid, a longer one shows `t("conversation.panel.topicTooLong", { max })` in a `div.gerr` under the field, linked by `aria-describedby`, as soon as it is typed (D-104). The description has no limit on the screen (TOKI-029 concern 6). A press calls the store command `editChannel(id, body)` (TOKI-032 R15) with only the field or fields that changed, each trimmed (an empty result is sent as `null`); the two fields are independent, so a person who changes only the description never sends the topic (TOKI-029 R9, D-121). Before sending, `ensureOnline()` of TOKI-093 R17 must be true; when it is false nothing is sent and the form stays open with its text (D-079). On success the command has stored the entry and the detail (TOKI-032 R15), so the header, About and Browse redraw, and the form closes; `changed: false` closes it the same way. On failure the form stays open with its text and, by `classifyApiError`: `field` (400) shows the server's message under the field; `no_access` applies `readFailure` (R14); `fresh_copy` (409, for example `channel_archived`) calls `readChannel(id)`, closes the form and shows `t("common.error.actionFailed")`; any other kind shows that toast (D-131, D-126). Check: with a fake store and `api`, a change of the topic only (body `{ "topic": "x" }`), of the description only, of both, an emptied topic (body `{ "topic": null }`), a topic of 250 emoji sent and 251 refused on screen, a topic of white space only (sent as null), no change (Save disabled), offline (nothing sent, text kept), each kind, a double press (one command), and the header after the save.

Notification level, mute and star
- R17. Notifications. The section holds three native radios in `label.opt`, one `name`, in this order and with these texts: `all` "All messages", `mentions` "Mentions only", `none` "Nothing" (the prototype's).
