# Conversation header and Details panel design

Date: 2026-11-17 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/conversation-header-details.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-035

## Goal
A person who opens a conversation needs to see what it is and to set it up for themselves. This work builds the top row of an open conversation (name, topic, star, head count, a Back button on a narrow screen) and the Details panel behind its members button: the facts about the conversation, who is in it, the person's own notification level, mute and star, the in-place edit of a channel's topic and description, and Leave. Leaving is the one action here where the two patterns of TOKI-093 meet: a public channel is left at once with Undo for 5 seconds, a private channel only after a confirmation that says a member must invite the person back, and #general cannot be left. Browser Back closes an open panel before it leaves the conversation.

## Scope
- In:
  - `ConversationHeader` and `DetailsPanel` in `apps/web/src/conversation/` (Design, "Layout"), their hooks, their CSS (the prototype's `.mh`, `.setrow`, `.opt` and `.mrow` rules) and their tests.
  - The panel's address rule: how it opens, how Back and Close close it (R8, R9).
  - The facts of a channel, read through the store and the client of TOKI-032 (the commands `readChannel`, `editChannel`, `leaveChannel`, `joinChannel`, the member list `api.channels.members`, and the lookups of the person's own entry, the people and the names), which call the routes of TOKI-029.
  - The controls for notification level, mute and star, and the seam they call to save (R19). The request behind the seam is not built here (concern 1).
  - Leave: the public and the private way, the last-member warning of a private channel, the move to #general afterwards (R22 to R26).
  - The `conversation` i18n namespace and two keys of `common` (R28), English and Vietnamese.
- Out (not now):
  - The conversation list, the conversation's address `/c/:id`, the route that mounts the page and its `ConversationPage` file, and the list row (grey, no count, no bold, starred group, order): TOKI-033. This work adds one thing to that route, the search parameter `panel` (R8), and fills the body of `ConversationPage` (Design, "How the page uses these pieces").
  - Browse channels, Join, and the people picker: TOKI-034. Add people and Remove a person (the buttons and the picker): TOKI-100. Archive and Restore: TOKI-101. Each adds its control to the panel (Design, "Hand-offs").
  - The request that saves a person's notification level, mute and star: no ticket owns it (concern 1). It is not decided here.
  - The search field and button in the header: TOKI-072. The Meet button: phase 4. The tab row (Messages, Files & links, Pinned) under the header: the shell gives `TabList` (TOKI-015), TOKI-048 adds the Pinned tab, and no plan line names the tab row itself (concern 7).
  - The profile that opens when a member row is pressed, and the person's presence, job title and status in the header's second line and in the rows: TOKI-057, TOKI-055, TOKI-056 (Design, "Hand-offs").
  - The grey system lines ("Lan left the channel", a topic change): no ticket owns them (TOKI-029 concern 4). The effect of mute and of the levels on counts, toasts, push and sound: TOKI-054 (count, bold, badge) and TOKI-073 (toast, push, sound). Unread counts: TOKI-053.
  - Live updates of an open header or panel when another person changes the topic, joins or leaves: the sync engine, TOKI-040 (Design, "Hand-offs").
  - Guests, Email this channel and Automations, which the prototype's panel draws: phases 5 and 4. The panel has no row for them.
  - Renaming a channel and switching it between public and private: not built (D-105, D-040).

## Requirements
Header
- R1. `ConversationHeader` renders a `div.mh` holding, in this order: `button.ib.back` (the Back button, R5); `div.grow` holding `h1.ell` (the glyph of R2, `span.ell` with the name, and the star of R6 when the person starred the conversation) and, when R3 gives one, `div.topic.ell`; and `button.btn.ghost.memb` (R4). It draws no search button, no Meet button and no tab. Its texts come from `useTranslation` (R28). It is drawn for every kind of conversation that the store holds. Check: a markup test for a public channel with a topic, a private channel with none, a DM and a group, reading the classes and the order; a source scan finds no `Meet` and no search control.
- R2. The glyph and the name. A public channel: the `hash` icon; a private channel: the `lock` icon; a DM: an `Avatar` (size 28, hue `personHue(id)` of TOKI-033 R28) of the other person (the id of `memberIds` that is not the signed-in person's, `getUser().id`); a group: the `users` icon. The name is `getConversationName(id)` of the store (TOKI-032 R17): the channel's name, the other person's `displayName`, or the other people's names of a group in the store's order, joined with `", "`. No name is worked out in this work. A deactivated person is still named. The name of a channel never gets a `#` in the header (the prototype draws the icon); the icon is `aria-hidden`, so the heading reads as the name. For a channel that the store does not hold as an entry (an archived one, concern 3) the name is the `name` of its held detail. Check: the four kinds, the exact `textContent` of the heading (`project-launch`; `Mai`; `Alex, Thu`), a deactivated person in a DM, the icons by name, and a channel with a detail and no entry.
- R3. The second line. For a channel it is the topic when the topic is not null and not empty, and no `div.topic` otherwise. A DM or a group has none for now: the person's status or job title and presence need TOKI-055, TOKI-056 and TOKI-057, which add that line when they have the data (Design, "Hand-offs"). The topic is plain text: `<`, `&` and a link are shown as typed, never as markup. Check: topic present, null, an empty string, `<b>x</b>` shown as text; a DM and a group have no `div.topic`.
- R4. The members button. It is a `Button` of TOKI-012 (`variant="ghost"`, class `memb`) that holds a `UsersIcon` and a `span.mono` with the head count, named `t("conversation.header.details")` ("Members and details", the prototype's own label), `aria-expanded` equal to whether the panel is open and `aria-controls` the id of the panel. A press opens the panel (R8) and, while it is open, closes it. It is drawn at every width and has no hover-only part (D-067). The count is `getMemberCount(id)` of the store (TOKI-032 R23): `memberIds.length` for a DM or group, the `memberCount` of the held detail for a channel; while it is undefined (the detail of R7 has not come back) the button shows no number and is still pressable. Check: the name and `aria-expanded` in both states; the count for a channel of 3, a DM (2) and a group of 8; no number while the read is pending; a press on a viewport of 360 px opens the panel.
- R5. The Back button. `button.ib.back` is an `IconButton` (`icon="left"`) named `t("conversation.header.back")` ("Back to conversations", the prototype's). It calls the prop `onBack`, which the page gives (Design, "How the page uses these pieces"; the route and the list are TOKI-033's). `conversation.css` holds the prototype's rule `.mh .back{display:none}` and, in a `@media (max-width: 719px)` block, `.mh .back{display:inline-grid}`: the button shows under 720 px and not from 720 px, the width at which the shell becomes a single pane (TOKI-015 R4, R16). Check: a press calls `onBack` once; a CSS test (`readCss`, TOKI-012 R27) holds both rules and the media condition; the rule is a `display` pair and needs no measurement.
- R6. The star. When the entry's `isStarred` is true, the heading holds a `span.later-ic` with the `star` icon and an accessible name `t("conversation.header.starred")`; when false, nothing. Another person's star is never shown (D-143): the entry is the signed-in person's own (TOKI-031 R8). Check: both values; the name of the star.
- R7. Where the header reads. The name, topic, kind, `isPrivate`, `isGeneral`, `isStarred` and `memberIds` come from the conversation entry of the store `conversations` of `apps/web/src/client.ts` (TOKI-032 R25: `getConversation(id)`), read in React through the store hook of TOKI-033 (R29), so a change that the store receives (a saved topic, a new star, the person leaving) redraws the header with no reload. The `description`, `createdBy`, `createdAt`, `archived` and `memberCount` come from the held detail of the channel, `getChannelDetail(id)`, which this work asks for with the store command `readChannel(id)` (TOKI-032 R14: it calls `GET /channels/{id}` and stores the answer) once when a channel's header is first drawn and again when the panel is opened; the first load holds none of them (TOKI-031 R7, R12). A DM and a group have no detail and no read. Decided in this spec: the store already keeps the detail (TOKI-032 R14), so no second cache is made and TanStack Query is not used for it. Check: with a fake store and a fake `api`, one header and one panel on a channel make one `readChannel` between them when opened together (the command is not repeated while one is running); a DM makes none; saving the topic redraws the header; a store change from outside redraws it.

The panel: opening, closing and Back
- R8. Opening. The panel is open when the conversation's address carries the search parameter `panel` with the value `details`: `/c/{conversationId}?panel=details`. A press on the members button (R4) when the panel is closed navigates to that address with a new history entry (`push`, not `replace`) and `state: { detailsPushed: true }`. Any other value of `panel` is dropped: the `validateSearch` of `conversationRoute` (`src/routes/conversation.tsx`, TOKI-033 R21) returns `panel: "details"` or no `panel`; this work adds that one function to the route and nothing else. Opening the panel on a conversation whose address is a thread (`/c/{conversationId}/t/{messageId}`, TOKI-045) leaves the thread: the panel and a thread are one side slot (`useShellPanel`, TOKI-033 R26: the last call wins), so the address becomes `/c/{conversationId}?panel=details`. Opening another conversation from the list goes to its address, which has no `panel`, so the panel closes (the prototype does the same, `openConv`). Check: with the router on a memory history, a press pushes one entry whose search is `{ panel: "details" }` and whose state has `detailsPushed`; `?panel=other` shows no panel and the parameter is dropped; from a thread address the entry has no thread segment; a click on another conversation closes the panel.
- R9. Closing. The panel's Close button (from 720 px), its Back button (under 720 px) and Escape (TOKI-015 R11 calls `onClose` for all three), and a second press on the members button, all call one function, `closeDetails`. When the current entry's `state.detailsPushed` is true, it calls `history.back()`; when it is not (the page was opened on `?panel=details`, a link or a reload that lost the state), it navigates to the same conversation without `panel` with `replace: true`. So browser Back, the panel's own button and Escape all end on `/c/{conversationId}` with the panel closed, and Back never leaves the conversation while the panel is open (D-089). Check, on a memory history: open then press Close, open then `history.back()`, open then Escape: each ends on `/c/{conversationId}` with no panel and the history one entry back, not two; a page opened on `?panel=details` and closed leaves the history length unchanged and shows no panel; after two opens and two closes the history has no extra entries.
- R10. One panel at a time, in the shell's slot. `ConversationPage` calls `useShellPanel({ label, content })` (TOKI-033 R26) with `label` `t("conversation.panel.label")` and `content` the `DetailsPanel` while the address carries `panel=details`, and `useShellPanel(null)` otherwise; the slot clears when the page unmounts. The shell's rules apply and are not repeated: from 720 to 1099 px the panel overlays the chat, from 1100 px it is a column, under 720 px it is the whole screen (TOKI-015 R19, R22). Check: a page test with the real `AppShellRoute` provider: the `data-panel` and `data-pane="side"` attributes of the shell with the panel open on a narrow window, the panel's name, and a cleared slot after the page unmounts.

The panel: content
- R11. The frame. `DetailsPanel` renders a `SidePanel` (TOKI-015 R11) with `title` `t("conversation.panel.titleDetails")` ("Details") for a channel and a group, and `t("conversation.panel.titleProfile")` ("Profile") for a DM (the prototype's titles), `labels.back` from `common.back` and `labels.close` from `common.closePanel` (R28). Its body holds, in this order, the sections of Table A, each with an `h3`: About; Members (a channel and a group; for a DM the person's row, R13); Notifications; Settings; Leave (R21). It holds no Guests, Email or Automations section. Check: the headings for each kind, in order, and the title for each kind.
- R12. About. A `div` with the glyph (`hash` or `lock`) and the name at 16 px bold; the topic (the second line of `.sm`, `color:var(--ink-2)`); the description as a `p.sm.muted`; and `div.xs.muted` with `t("conversation.panel.createdBy", { name, date })` ("Created by {name} · {date}", the prototype's form). For a channel: the creator's name is the `displayName` of `createdBy` from the people of the store (a deactivated creator is named; a creator the store does not hold is left out with its `Created by` part, and the date alone is shown); `date` is `formatDate(createdAt, ctx)` of TOKI-094 R2 with `weekday: false` and no year in the same year (R3 of that spec), where `ctx` is `{ timeZone, now, language, dayWords }`: `timeZone` is the browser's (`Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`) until a person's own zone is stored (TOKI-078; Decided in this spec, D-144 gives the profile's zone, not a rule for this date), `now` is read from the clock of TOKI-008, `language` is the person's language, and `dayWords` is the three `common` day words, which this line does not show (`formatDate` does not use them). A `null` from `formatDate` (R7 of that spec) leaves the date out. A channel with a null topic shows the line `t("conversation.panel.noTopic")`, one with a null description shows `t("conversation.panel.noDescription")`, both in `.muted`. For a DM the line under the name is `t("conversation.panel.directConversation")` ("Direct conversation", the prototype's) and nothing else; for a group the same line. Check: a channel with all fields (`Created by Lan · 8 Jan`), the next year (`8 Jan 2026`), a null creator, a creator that is deactivated, an invalid `createdAt` (no date), null topic and null description, a DM, a group; the zone is passed as the test's own (`Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` against `America/New_York` moves a date near midnight).
- R13. The people. For a channel, `Members · {count}` (`t("conversation.panel.members", { count })`, the prototype's form, with `getMemberCount(id)`) and a list of rows. A row is a `div.mrow` with an `Avatar` (size 32, the person's `displayName`, hue `personHue(id)` of TOKI-033 R28), the name in bold, and the `Tag` `t("conversation.panel.deactivated")` when the person is deactivated. The rows come from `useChannelMembers(id)`: it calls `api.channels.members(id, { limit: 50, cursor })` of the client (TOKI-032 R9) page after page, following `nextCursor` until it is null (at most 4 requests for the 200 people of NFR-04), and turns each `userId` into a person with `getPerson` of the store; an id the store does not know is left out (not an error: the people and the member list are two reads). The list shows the first 50 rows and 50 more each time the panel body is scrolled to within 100 px of its end (D-109). When the list has more than 50 rows a search field (`Field`, `variant="search"`, `t("conversation.panel.membersSearch")`) is drawn above it and filters the rows by name, containing the text, ignoring case; no match shows `t("conversation.panel.membersNone")` centred in the section (D-074). The rows are not buttons until TOKI-057 gives them a profile to open (Design, "Hand-offs"). For a group the rows come from `memberIds`, with no request and no search field. For a DM there is no list: the section is the one row of the other person, with the heading `t("conversation.panel.person")` ("Person"), which TOKI-057 replaces by the profile card (F24, Design, "Hand-offs"). Check: a channel of 3, one of 120 members (50 rows, then 100, then 120 after two scrolls; 3 requests for 120 ids), the search field absent at 50 and present at 51, a filter that matches and one that matches nothing, an unknown id, a deactivated member, a group of 3, a DM.
- R14. Loading and failure of the reads. While the detail of R7 is not held, About shows the name and topic of the entry and no description, creator or date; while `useChannelMembers` is loading, the section shows `t("conversation.panel.membersLoading")`. A failed read is sorted by `classifyApiError(error)` of TOKI-032 R8 (D-131): `no_access` (403, 404: the person is not or no longer a member) navigates to the not-found view (TOKI-006 now, the shared page of TOKI-082 later) by throwing the router's `notFound()`; `unauthenticated` and `ignore` do nothing here (the client's hook and the aborted request are not this panel's); every other kind (`retry`, `fresh_copy`, `wait`, `field`, `unknown`) shows, in the place of the section, `t("conversation.panel.readFailed")` and a `Button` `t("conversation.panel.retry")` that reads again. A read with no answer after 10 seconds is a failure (D-126): the client raises it (TOKI-032 R5). The rest of the panel stays usable. The function is `readFailure(error)` in `readFailure.ts`; the writes of R16, R19, R22, R23 and R26 call it for their `no_access`. Check: with a fake `api` each kind (403, 404, 409, 429, 500, `network`, `timeout`, `aborted`) for the detail read and for the members read; the retry reads again once; the settings stay usable while the members read fails.

Editing the topic and description
- R15. Who may edit and where. A channel that is not archived shows, under About, a `Button` `t("conversation.panel.edit")` ("Edit"), for every member of either role (D-105, TOKI-029 R9, `canEditChannelDetails`); a press replaces the topic and the description by two fields in place: `Field` "Topic" (single line) and `Field` "Description" (`multiline`), each with a visible label, filled with the current values, and the buttons `t("conversation.panel.save")` and `t("common.cancel")`. A DM and a group have no Edit button (they have no topic, TOKI-028 R6). The name has no field and no control anywhere (D-105). The archived channel is concern 3. Check: Edit is present for a Member and for an Admin, absent in a DM, a group and an archived channel; no `input` or `textarea` is named for the name of the channel in any state.
- R16. Saving. `Save` is disabled while nothing differs from the stored text after trimming, while the topic is invalid, and while a request runs (D-104, D-111). The topic is valid when `isValidChannelTopic` of `packages/shared` (TOKI-028 R15) accepts it after the trim: an empty text is valid (it becomes null), a text of 1 to `CHANNEL_TOPIC_MAX` (250) code points is valid, a longer one shows `t("conversation.panel.topicTooLong", { max })` in a `div.gerr` under the field, linked by `aria-describedby`, as soon as it is typed (D-104). The description has no limit on the screen (TOKI-029 concern 6). A press calls the store command `editChannel(id, body)` (TOKI-032 R15) with only the field or fields that changed, each trimmed (an empty result is sent as `null`); the two fields are independent, so a person who changes only the description never sends the topic (TOKI-029 R9, D-121). Before sending, `ensureOnline()` of TOKI-093 R17 must be true; when it is false nothing is sent and the form stays open with its text (D-079). On success the command has stored the entry and the detail (TOKI-032 R15), so the header, About and Browse redraw, and the form closes; `changed: false` closes it the same way. On failure the form stays open with its text and, by `classifyApiError`: `field` (400) shows the server's message under the field; `no_access` applies `readFailure` (R14); `fresh_copy` (409, for example `channel_archived`) calls `readChannel(id)`, closes the form and shows `t("common.error.actionFailed")`; any other kind shows that toast (D-131, D-126). Check: with a fake store and `api`, a change of the topic only (body `{ "topic": "x" }`), of the description only, of both, an emptied topic (body `{ "topic": null }`), a topic of 250 emoji sent and 251 refused on screen, a topic of white space only (sent as null), no change (Save disabled), offline (nothing sent, text kept), each kind, a double press (one command), and the header after the save.

Notification level, mute and star
- R17. Notifications. The section holds three native radios in `label.opt`, one `name`, in this order and with these texts: `all` "All messages", `mentions` "Mentions only", `none` "Nothing" (the prototype's). The checked one is the entry's `notificationLevel`, which the store holds already resolved (TOKI-031 R8): `mentions` for a new channel and `all` for a new DM when the person never chose (D-046, AC-043). Choosing another saves it at once (R19); the checked radio changes when the save has been answered, not before. The same three radios are drawn for every kind of conversation, #general included. Check: the three texts and their order; a channel entry at `mentions` and a DM entry at `all` have those radios checked (TS-065, TS-066 as far as this screen goes); choosing `none` sends one patch with `notificationLevel: "none"` and nothing else.
- R18. Settings. Two rows `div.setrow`, each with a `span.grow` holding the text and a `Switch` (TOKI-012 R14) named by it with `labelledBy`: `t("conversation.panel.mute")` ("Mute conversation") and `t("conversation.panel.star")` ("Star (move to Starred)", the prototype's). The switches show the entry's `isMuted` and `isStarred`. A press saves the other value (R19) and the switch changes when the save has been answered. Nothing in the panel, the header or the member list shows another person's level, mute or star (D-143, AC-138). Check: both switches in both states; a press sends a patch with exactly one key; with a second person's entry in the fixture, no text of the other person's values appears anywhere.
- R19. The saving seam. `useConversationSettings(conversationId)` returns `{ values, save, pending }`. `values` are the entry's `notificationLevel`, `isMuted` and `isStarred`. `save(patch)` takes an object with exactly one of the three and calls `conversations.saveSettings(conversationId, patch)`, a store command that does not exist: the request behind it has no owner (concern 1) and TOKI-032 (its R16 and concern 9) says the owner of that request adds the client method and a command that follows its R13 and R15. This work states what it needs from the command and does not write it: `saveSettings(id, patch)` calls the request, applies the three stored values it answers to the entry when the answer arrives (never before), calls the listeners once, and on an error changes nothing and rejects with the `ApiError`; its type is `SaveSettings` in `apps/web/src/conversation/useConversationSettings.ts`, which this work builds against with a fake. The order in `save` is: `ensureOnline()` (when false, nothing is saved and the control keeps its value, D-079); the control that was pressed is disabled while the call runs, and only that one (D-111); on success the entry holds the answered values, so the header's star, the list and the panel redraw; on failure the control keeps its value and `t("common.error.actionFailed")` is shown as a toast (D-131), and `no_access` applies `readFailure` (R14). Mute is personal and the call carries no other person's id. Check: with a fake `saveSettings`, a resolve, a reject, a pending call (the pressed control disabled and the others not), offline (the fake is not called), two quick presses (one call), and the entry after each.

Leave
- R20. What this work calls on leave. The controls of R21 to R26 use the store commands `leaveChannel(id)` and, for Undo, `joinChannel(id)` (TOKI-032 R15), which call `POST /channels/{id}/leave` and `/join` (TOKI-029 R12, R10) and change the list only when the answer arrives. The server refuses `leave` for #general and is the only place that decides who may leave; the screen only does not offer it. Check: the tests of R21 to R26 use a fake store that records the two commands.
- R21. Where Leave is offered. A `Button` `t("conversation.panel.leave")` ("Leave channel", the prototype's) in red text (`color:var(--danger)`), in a `div` with `margin-top:16px` at the end of the panel, for a public or private channel that is not #general. It is absent for #general, for every role (D-034, AC-016), for a DM and for a group. It is drawn at every width. Check: absent for #general as a Member and as an Admin, for a DM and a group; present for a public and a private channel; present on a 360 px viewport.
- R22. A public channel is left at once. The press runs `run` of `useUndo()` (TOKI-093 R12) with `action: "leavePublicChannel"`, `text` `t("conversation.leave.toast", { name })` ("You left #{name}", the prototype's), `perform` the command `leaveChannel(id)`, `undo` the command `joinChannel(id)`, and `key` `leave:{id}`. No confirmation is asked (D-061, D-069, AC-054). On `"done"` the helper shows the toast with Undo for 5 seconds; on `"failed"` and `"blocked"` the helper has already told the person, the channel is still joined and nothing moves. The press is ignored while a leave runs (D-111). The code never shows a second confirmation or a second Undo (D-069). Check: with fake timers, a press calls `leaveChannel` once with no dialog in the document; the toast holds `Undo`; Undo within 4999 ms calls `joinChannel` once; at 5000 ms Undo is gone and `joinChannel` was not called; a rejected `leaveChannel` gives the failure toast and no navigation; offline gives `"blocked"`.
- R23. A private channel is left after a confirmation. The press opens `ConfirmAction` (TOKI-093 R18) with `action: "leavePrivateChannel"`, `title` `t("conversation.leave.title")` ("Leave this channel?"), `target` `#{name}` (the channel's name with `#`), `description` `t("conversation.leave.privateNotice")` ("A member will have to invite you back.", the wording of journey F23, which says what D-051 and D-156 require), `confirmLabel` `t("conversation.leave.confirm")` ("Leave") and `variant` `danger`. Cancel, Esc and an outside press leave the person a member and change nothing. Confirm runs `leaveChannel(id)` with `busy` set until it answers; there is no Undo, now or afterwards (D-051, D-156, AC-055). `ConfirmAction` checks the connection itself (TOKI-093 R18). On success the dialog closes and R25 runs; on failure the dialog stays open with `busy` off and a toast `t("common.error.actionFailed")`, so the person can try again or cancel. Check: no `leaveChannel` before the confirm; Cancel, Esc and an outside press call nothing; Confirm calls it once, the buttons are disabled while it runs, and no toast with `Undo` is ever shown; the dialog's text holds the invite line and the target `#leadership`; a failure leaves the dialog open and the command can be sent again; offline leaves the dialog open and calls nothing.
- R24. The last member of a private channel. When the channel is private and the member count that was read when the press happened is 1, the description also holds `t("conversation.leave.lastMember")` ("You are the last member. The channel will be archived.", F23), after the invite line, so the person is told before the channel is archived (D-051, AC-056). The count is read again at the press, with `readChannel(id)` and then `getMemberCount(id)`, not taken from the held detail, and the dialog opens when that read answers; when the read fails the dialog opens with no last-member line and the server's answer decides (its `archived` is true when the last member leaves, TOKI-029 R12). The count includes deactivated people because the server's count does (TOKI-029 concern 9). A public channel's last member is concern 2: until it is answered the build treats a public leave as in R22 and shows no warning. Check: a private channel with `memberCount` 1 shows both lines, with 2 only the invite line; a count of 2 in the held detail and 1 in the fresh read shows both; a failed fresh read shows only the invite line; the line is never in a public channel's flow.
- R25. After a leave. When `leaveChannel` has answered (R26), the entry and the detail of the channel are gone from the store (TOKI-032 R15), so the channel has left the list. Decided in this spec: (a) the panel is open by the search parameter `panel=details` of the conversation address, pushed as one history entry with a state flag, so Back closes it first and a reload keeps it (R8, R9); chosen over a history entry with no address, because the technical design keeps interface state in the URL and the state alone would not survive a copied link; (b) the channel's detail is the store's (`readChannel`, TOKI-032 R14) and the member list is read by the panel itself with `api.channels.members` and kept in the panel, because TOKI-032 R15 gives no command for it; (c) all pages of the member list are read when the panel opens and shown 50 at a time on scroll, because the server has no search by name (the route returns ids, TOKI-029 R8) and a search needs every row (R13); at most 4 requests; (d) the member search field is drawn only above 50 rows, because below that the whole list is on screen and a field is clutter; D-109 describes the case of 200 people; (e) a switch or radio changes when the save has been answered, with only the pressed control disabled, instead of an optimistic change with a rollback, so no store needs to roll back (R19); (f) a 404 on `leave` is a success (R26); (g) the browser's time zone is used for the creation date until a person's zone is stored (R12); (h) the names, the avatar colour, the side-panel slot and the move after a leave are TOKI-032's and TOKI-033's (R17; R28; R26; R21), reused and not rebuilt; (i) the DM's panel shows the other person's row in the place of the members list and TOKI-057's card replaces it (R13), a reading of F24 that does not wait for the profile ticket; (j) a group has no Leave (Design).
