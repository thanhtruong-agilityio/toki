# AppShell in three layouts design

Date: 2026-11-16 · Status: draft for review
Intent: docs/intents/app-shell.md (Thanh Truong)
Ticket: TOKI-015

## Goal
The builder (Thanh and Claude) needs the frame that every phase 1 screen sits in, before the conversation list, the chat and the section screens are built. The prototype draws the frame with one grid and two sets of rules, and in three places it breaks what the decision log now requires (D-068, D-078): a side panel squeezes the chat to 84 px at 800 px, the conversation list or the navigation is cut off in a window 560 px high, and on a narrow screen the toasts sit at the top of the window.
After this change `ui-web` exports an `AppShell` that draws Calm, Focus and the narrow layout with bottom tabs, with the side panel overlaying the chat from 720 to 1099 px, a navigation that scrolls, a conversation list that stays the main area of the sidebar, and toasts that stay above the bottom bar and the composer. `apps/web` mounts it for every signed-in route. The pieces the screens inside it share (tab row, section page, side panel, utility classes, narrow text size, look attributes) are built here once.

## Requirements
In the checks below, "the fixture" is `http://localhost:5173/shell-fixture.html` served by `pnpm --filter ./apps/web dev` (R32), "the browser test" is `e2e/app-shell.spec.ts` run by `pnpm test:e2e` (Chromium, TOKI-008), and a "width" is the width of the browser window. - R13. `TabList` renders a `div.tabs` with `role="tablist"` and `aria-label` from its prop (required), holding `Tab`s. `Tab` renders a `button.tab` with `role="tab"`, `aria-selected`, `id` and, when `panelId` is given, `aria-controls`; the selected tab has the roving tab stop (`tabIndex` 0) and the others `-1`. `Tab` calls `onSelect` on a click. - R14. Keyboard in a `TabList`. The prototype has no key handling in its tab row; this is the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern with manual activation (Decided in this spec; concern 18): ArrowRight and ArrowLeft move focus to the next and the previous `Tab`, wrapping at the ends, without selecting it; Home and End move focus to the first and the last; Enter and Space select the focused tab (the button's own click); the other keys do nothing. A moved focus scrolls the tab into view with `scrollIntoView({ inline: "nearest", block: "nearest" })` when the browser has it, and so does a change of the selected tab. Only the selected tab is in the Tab order, so Tab leaves the row for the screen's panel. Manual activation, because a tab may load data (Files & links) and a key press must not fire a load for each tab passed. Check: `user-event` keyboard test (ArrowRight, ArrowLeft, wrap, Home, End, Enter, Space, another key; `onSelect` is called only for Enter, Space and a click), and one test that `scrollIntoView` is called when focus moves.

Check: in the browser test an `EmptyState` is placed in `.secbody`, in `.sb` and in `.main` of the fixture; at 360, 720 and 1100 px, in the three looks (was both looks), the centre of its content (`.eic` and the text) is within 1 px of the centre of the parent, as TOKI-013 R17 says.

Style rules
- R16. `shell.css` holds exactly the selectors and declarations of Tables C to G (value for value, in any order, any white space), grouped in the media blocks that the tables name, and no other rule; a later ticket that adds a rule to `shell.css` (TOKI-076 adds those of `.navclock`) adds its rows to the tables and to this test in the same change. The media conditions are exactly `(min-width: 720px)`, `(max-width: 719px)`, `(min-width: 720px) and (max-width: 1099px)` and `(min-width: 956px) and (max-width: 1099px)`; there is no `@container` query and no `!important`. "Narrow" is the window under 720 px; a window of exactly 720 px is not narrow (D-068, Design, "What the prototype does, measured"). Check: a CSS test with `readCss` of TOKI-012 R27, with the tables typed in.
- R17. `shell.css` uses only the design system's names: every colour is `var(--…)` or `transparent`, every `var(--name)` is one of the 32 properties of TOKI-009 R10, one of the six sidebar properties of TOKI-009 R11 (`--side-rail`, `--side-bg`, `--side-ink`, `--side-muted`, `--side-line`, `--side-hover`; TOKI-144) or `--tabh`, `--shell-panel` or `--shell-convs-min` (Table C) (was: one of the 32 properties or the three own ones), no colour literal appears, and the `font-family` lines are those of Table C. ### Narrow: panes and the bottom bar

### The look on the page
`applyAppearance` (R28) is the one function that sets `data-look`, `data-scheme`, `data-accent` and `data-density` on `document.documentElement`. `look` `calm`, `focus` or `classic` is copied to `data-look`; `scheme` `light` or `dark` is copied to `data-scheme`, and `device` becomes `dark` on a dark device and `light` otherwise and follows the device's setting; the accent and the density set their own attributes (a missing density removes the attribute). It is called by `apps/web/src/main.tsx` before the app is mounted, with `look: "calm"` and `scheme: "device"`, and by TOKI-078 with the saved values. TOKI-016 has its own function for its controls and does not call it (Scope). The shell's layout rules depend on `[data-look]`; the tokens follow the device through `:root:not([data-scheme])` and do not depend on it: a page with no `data-scheme` on a dark device shows the dark colours of Calm with the Calm layout. Calling `applyAppearance({ look: "calm", scheme: "device" })` before the app is mounted sets both attributes. It does not remove the difference for a person whose saved look or scheme is not the default, before the account has loaded; TOKI-078 owns that (Scope, concern 10). (was: `look` `device` followed the device and chose the layout, so a page with no `data-eff` on a dark device showed Focus colours with the Calm layout.)
